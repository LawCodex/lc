### AN ACT to provide for the collection of rates and dues in respect of Government jetties.

Commencement [1 January 1930]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Jetties Dues Act.

### 2. Regulations

(1) The Governor General may make regulations for raising, levying, managing and collecting rates, tolls or dues upon goods, animals or things landed or shipped at or from the Government jetties in Soufrière, Choiseul, Laborie and Vieux-Fort, and at or from any jetty which by notice approved by the House of Assembly may be declared to be a Government jetty.

(2) All such regulations shall be subject to the approval of the House of Assembly.

### 3. Penalty

There may be annexed to a breach of, or failure to observe, any regulation made under this Act a penalty not exceeding $48.

### 4. Appropriation

All rates, tolls, dues and penalties collected or recovered under the authority of this Act, or any regulation made thereunder, shall be paid into the Treasury for the public uses of Saint Lucia.
