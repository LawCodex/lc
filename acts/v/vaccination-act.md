### AN ACT to consolidate and amend the law relating to public vaccination.

Commencement [18 November 1881]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Vaccination Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**district registrar**” mean respectively the Officers appointed as such under the Civil Status Act;

“**medical district**” has the same meaning as in the Medical Officers Act;

“**medical officer**” has the same meaning as in the Medical Officers Act;

“**parent**” includes the father and mother of a legitimate child, the mother of an illegitimate child, and any other person having the custody of a child;

“**public vaccinator**” means any person whose appointment as such has been notified in the Gazette, and has not been revoked, or cancelled, or who has not resigned;

“**Registrar**” and “**district registrar**” mean respectively the Officers appointed as such under the Civil Status Act;

“**worker**” has the same meaning as in the Medical Officers Act.

### 3. Provision for suspension of compulsory sections

The operation of the provisions of sections 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 14, 18, and 21, with respect to any district constituting a medical district under the Medical Officers Act or to any part of it, may be suspended or revived by Proclamation of the Governor General. However, if the period of time during which any act is required to be done under these sections, or any period of time referred to in these sections, shall expire when such sections are suspended, such period shall be held to be extended to the twenty-eighth day inclusive after the operation of the said sections is revived.

*Compulsory Vaccination*

### 4. Obligation on parent to have newborn child vaccinated

(1) The parent of every child born in Saint Lucia shall, within 3 months after the birth of such child, or within 3 months after receiving into custody such child, take it or cause it to be taken to the medical officer of the medical district to which such child resides, to be vaccinated according to the provisions of this Act, or shall, within that period, cause such child to be so vaccinated by any other public vaccinator. However, if, when any such child is taken to the medical officer within the period, such medical officer has not any vaccine lymph or is otherwise unable to vaccinate the child, then and in every such case the parent of the child shall take it or cause it to be taken to the medical officer within such time thereafter, and at the place, as such medical officer may appoint.

(2) The medical officer to whom the child is brought is required, with all reasonable despatch, subject to the conditions mentioned below, to vaccinate such child.

### 5. Further proceedings after performance of vaccination

(1) After any operation of vaccination is performed, the parent shall again take the child, or cause it to be taken, to the medical officer or other public vaccinator, at such time and place as may have been appointed by the medical officer or public vaccinator at the time of the operation, the time being 8 days after such operation, and that place being not more distant from the residence of such parent than the nearest place appointed for the attendance of a medical officer under the Medical Officers Act or being a place of equal convenience to the parent and to the vaccinator.

(2) When such child is again brought, the medical officer or other public vaccinator shall inspect such child, and ascertain the result of the operation.

(3) In the event of the vaccination having been unsuccessful the parent shall, if the medical officer or other public vaccinator so directs, cause the child to be immediately again vaccinated and inspected as on the previous occasion.

### 6. Certificate of successful vaccination

As soon as the successful vaccination of any adult person or child has been verified by inspection, the medical officer or other public vaccinator who shall have performed the operation shall deliver to such adult, or to the father or mother of such child, or to the person having the care, nurture, or custody of such child, a certificate under his or her hand according to Form 1 in the Schedule, or to the like effect, and shall forward by post or otherwise a duplicate of such certificate to the Registrar of Civil Status of the registration district within which the birth of the child was registered, but if such district is not known to him or her, or if the child was born outside Saint Lucia, to the registrar of the district in which the vaccination was performed.

### 7. Giving of certificate of unfitness for vaccination

(1) If any medical officer or other public vaccinator is of opinion that any unvaccinated child, at a time when vaccination is obligatory under the provisions of this Act, is not in a fit state to be vaccinated, he or she shall inform the parent of such child of the same, and shall, within 7 days, forward to the registrar of the district in which the child resides a certificate under his or her hand, according to Form 2 in the Schedule, or to the like effect; and such certificate shall remain in force for 2 months, and shall be renewable for successive periods of 2 months, until a medical officer or other public vaccinator considers the child to be in a fit state for vaccination, when the child shall with all reasonable despatch be vaccinated, and the certificate mentioned in the last preceding section shall be forwarded, if warranted by the result.

(2) At or before the end of each successive period, the parent shall take the child or cause it to be taken to the medical officer or other public vaccinator, who shall then examine the child and forward the certificate according to the said Form 2, whenever he or she considers it requisite under the circumstances of the case.

### 8. Giving of certificate of unsusceptibility of successful vaccination

If any medical officer or other public vaccinator finds that a child, when it has been thrice unsuccessfully vaccinated, is unsusceptible of successful vaccination, or that a child brought to him or her for vaccination has already had the smallpox, he or she shall inform the parent of the child of the same, and shall, within 7 days, forward to the registrar of the district in which the child resides a certificate under his or her hand, according to Form 3 in the Schedule, or to the like effect, and the parent shall not be required to cause the child to be vaccinated.

### 9. Right of any person to be vaccinated or revaccinated

(1) A medical officer shall on application vaccinate or revaccinate any person without charge, at any time and place appointed for the attendance of a medical officer under the Medical Officers Act and on performing the revaccination, he or she shall inform such person that he or she is to attend at the same place at any time appointed by the medical officer, such time being, if practicable, the eighth day after the operation, and that in default of so attending he or she will be liable as in this section mentioned.

(2) If such person fails to attend at such place at the time appointed, or refuses to permit the medical officer or his or her deputy to ascertain the result of the operation, he or she shall pay a fee of $0.60 for each revaccination, and such fee shall be a debt from him or her to such medical officer.

### 10. Arm to arm vaccination prohibited

A person shall not take from any person successfully vaccinated lymph for the purpose of vaccination, and a person shall not vaccinate any person with lymph taken from any other person. A person who contravenes the provisions of this section is liable to a fine of $500.

### 11. Notification respecting vaccination to be given on registration of birth

A district registrar shall, on the registration in his or her district of the birth of any child not already vaccinated, deliver to the person registering such birth a notice, according to Form 4 in the Schedule, or to the like effect, requiring such child to be duly vaccinated according to the provisions of this Act, and specifying the days, hours, and places appointed under the Medical Officers Act for the attendance of the medical officer of the medical district wherein the child resides.

### 12. Book to be kept by District Registrar, and fees payable to him or her

(1) A district registrar shall keep a book in which he or she shall enter minutes of the notices of vaccination given by him or her as required under this Act, and also register the certificates transmitted to him or her, and shall at all reasonable times allow searches to be made in the book, and upon demand give a copy under his or her hand, or under that of his or her deputy, of any entry in the same, on payment of a fee of $0.12 for each search and $0.06 for each copy, to be paid by the person making the search or requiring the copy.

(2) A district registrar shall receive a fee of $0.06 in respect of every child whose birth he or she has registered and in respect of whom he or she gives the notice, and another fee of $0.12 in respect of every such child whose certificates in Form 1 or 3 he or she has registered, and he or she shall receive a fee of $0.12 in respect of each child whose certificate in the Form 1 or 3 he or she has registered without having registered the birth; the fee in the last 3 cases being payable from public funds. However, a fee shall not be charged for any search made by a public vaccinator, or by any justice of the peace or medical or revenue officer.

### 13. Account of fees payable to be made up by District Registrar

A district registrar of civil status shall make out an account of the fees to which he or she may be entitled under this Act and submit the same on the usual quarter days of the year to the Registrar of Civil Status, and the said Registrar shall after examining the same and comparing it with the register of vaccinations, and if he or she thinks fit, with the certificates of vaccinations or other vouchers and finding the account to be correct, certify the same for payment by the Director of Finance and Planning.

### 14. Making out of list of cases of omission to procure vaccination, and proceedings

A district registrar shall within one week after 1 January and 1 July in every year, and at any other time when called upon so to do by the Registrar of Civil Status, make out a list of all cases in which certificates of vaccination have not been duly received by him or her during the last preceding half-year, and shall submit the same to the chief officer of the Police Force resident in the medical district in which he or she acts, and the said officer shall inquire into the circumstances of the cases contained in the list, and if he or she finds that the provisions of this Act have been neglected, he or she shall cause proceedings to be taken against the persons in default.

### 15. Form of returns

A district registrar shall forward returns respecting vaccination to the Registrar, in such form as may be appointed by him or her, together with each return forwarded by him or her under the Civil Status Act.

### 16. Supply of books to District Registrars

The books of the district registrars for the entry of particulars of vaccination shall be supplied by the Registrar, at the public expense, and shall be kept in such form as may be appointed by him or her.

*Persons arriving from Infected Place*

### 17. Persons arriving from infected place to be vaccinated or re-vaccinated

A person who arrives in this island from any port or place declared by the Governor General to be infected with smallpox may, before the person is allowed to land, be required by the health officer to be vaccinated or re-vaccinated as the case may be, unless the person is able to produce, to the satisfaction of the health officer, proof that he or she has been successfully vaccinated, or revaccinated as the case may be, within 3 years from the date of his or her arrival. If such person refuses to be vaccinated, or re-vaccinated, as the case may be, the health officer may order him or her to be placed under surveillance for not more than 14 days, reckoned from the date of his or her departure from such infected port or place.

*Offences*

### 18. Penalty on parent neglecting to have child vaccinated or inspected

A parent who neglects to take his or her child to be vaccinated, or, after being vaccinated, to be inspected, according to the provisions of this Act, and does not render a reasonable excuse for his or her neglect, shall be prosecuted by the district registrar, and on conviction, is liable to a penalty not exceeding $4.80.

### 19. Penalty on Public Vaccinator neglecting to transmit certificate

A public vaccinator who neglects to transmit any certificate required of him or her by this Act, completely filled up and legibly written, to the District Registrar within the time specified, on conviction, be liable to a penalty not exceeding $9.60.

### 20. Punishment for signing false certificate

A person who wilfully signs any false certificate, duplicate, or account under this Act, on conviction, is liable to imprisonment, with or without hard labour, for any term not exceeding one year.

### 21. Judicial proceedings in certain cases of omission to procure vaccination

(1) If any public vaccinator, district registrar, revenue officer, or police officer gives information in writing to a magistrate that he or she has reason to believe that any child under the age of 5 years, being within the judicial district for which such magistrate acts, has not been vaccinated, and that he or she has given notice to the parent of such child to procure its being vaccinated, and that such notice has been disregarded, the magistrate may summon such parent to appear, with the child, before him or her at a certain time and place, and upon such appearance if the magistrate finds, after such examination as he or she may consider necessary, that the child has not been vaccinated, and has not already had the smallpox, he or she shall, if he or she sees fit, make an order under his or her hand directing such child to be vaccinated within a certain time.

(2) If at the expiration of such time the child has not been vaccinated, or is not shown to be then unfit to be vaccinated, or to be unsusceptible of vaccination, the parent upon whom such order is made shall be proceeded against summarily, and unless he or she shows some reasonable ground for his or her omission to carry such order into effect, he or she is liable to a penalty not exceeding $4.80. However, if the person summoned fails to appear with the child, at the time and place specified in the summons, the magistrate may make an order as above mentioned as though it were proved that the child had not been vaccinated, and whether the child has been vaccinated or not, such order may in this case contain a requirement for the payment of the costs of the proceedings, including the costs of such order. However, if the magistrate is of the opinion that the parent is improperly brought before him or her, and refuses to make any order for the vaccination of the child, he or she may order the informer to pay to such parent a sum of money that he or she considers to be a fair compensation for his or her expenses and loss of time in attending before the magistrate.

### 22. Punishment of person producing smallpox in another person

A person who produces or attempts to produce on any person, by inoculation with variolous matter, or by wilful exposure to variolous matter or any matter, article, or thing impregnated with variolous matter, or wilfully, by any other means whatsoever, produces smallpox in any person, on conviction is liable to imprisonment, with or without hard labour, for any term not exceeding one year.

### 23. Recovery of penalty and procedure thereof

A person who contravenes this Act may be prosecuted before a district court by the health officer, Commissioner of Police, or any police officer, and the procedure shall be that in force regulating procedure in the district courts.

### 24. Imprisonment in default of payment of penalty

A person who is condemned to pay a penalty under this Act may be sentenced, in default of payment to imprisonment for any term not exceeding the term specified in a like case in the scale of imprisonment in default of payment of fines and penalties in force in the district courts unless a different term is otherwise provided.

### 25. Proof of charge for neglecting to procure vaccination of child

In the prosecution of any person for neglecting to procure the vaccination of a child, it is not necessary in support to prove that the defendant had received notice from the district registrar, or any other officer, of the requirements of the law in this respect; but if the defendant produces a duplicate of any certificate respecting such child, or the register of certificates respecting vaccinations kept by the district registrar, in which any certificate respecting such child is duly entered, the same shall be a sufficient defence, except in regard to the certificate numbered 2, when the time specified therein for the postponement of the vaccination expired before the time when the information was laid.

### 26. Limitation of time for making complaint under the Act

(1) A complaint may be made, and any information, other than an information under section 21, may be laid, under this Act within 12 months from the time when the matter of such complaint or information arose, and not subsequently; and any information under section 21 may be laid at any time before the child mentioned in such information has attained the age of 5 years.

(2) The defendant in any proceeding under this Act may appear by any person authorised by him or her in that behalf. However, this section does not apply to any proceedings in respect of a false certificate or duplicate, or in respect of inoculation with variolous matter.

### 27. Governor General may amend Forms in Schedule

The Governor General may, by notice in the Gazette, annul, alter, or add to the Forms in the Schedule, and any reference in this Act to any such Form shall be construed to refer to the corresponding Form prescribed by any such notice.
