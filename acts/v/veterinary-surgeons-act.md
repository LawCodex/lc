### An Act relating to the practice of veterinary surgery.

Commencement [10 August 1957]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Veterinary Surgeons Act.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires the expression —

“**Board**” means the Medical Board appointed under section 4 of the Medical Registration Act;

“**member State**” means a member State of the Caribbean Community or the Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States listed in Schedule 2;

“**national**” means a person who:

(a) is a citizen of a member State;

(b) has a connection with a member State of a kind which entitles that person to be regarded as belonging to or, if it be so expressed, as being native or resident of that member State for the purposes of the laws of that member State relating to immigration;

(c) is a company or other legal entity constituted in a member State in accordance with the laws of that member State and which that member State regards as belonging to it, if that company or other legal entity has been formed for gainful purposes and has its registered office and central administration, and carries on substantial activity within the member State and is substantially owned and effectively controlled by persons mentioned in paragraphs (a) and (b);

and for the purposes of this definition, a company or other legal entity is:

(i) substantially owned if more than 50% of the equity interest in the company or entity is beneficially owned by a person mentioned in para-graphs (a) and (b),

(ii) effectively controlled if the persons mentioned in para-graphs (a) and (b) have the power to name a majority of its directors or are otherwise legally entitled to direct its actions;

“**veterinary surgery**” means the art and science of veterinary surgery and medicine;

“**Registrar**” means the Registrar of the High Court.

*(Amended by Act 21 of 2006)*

## PART 1 REGISTRATION

### 3. Unregistered persons not to have title of veterinary surgeon

A person is not entitled to take or use the name or title of veterinary surgeon (either alone or in combination with any other word or words) or any name, title, addition or description, implying that he or she is registered under this Act, or that he or she is a person specially qualified to practice veterinary surgery, unless he or she is registered under this Act.

### 4. Offences by unregistered persons

A person, not being registered under this Act, who takes or uses any such name, title, addition or description as aforesaid, or any addition to a name, designation or description, whether expressed in words, or by letters, or partly in words or partly by letters, is liable on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding $50.

### 5. Unregistered person not to recover fees

A person is not entitled to receive any fee or charge in any court for performing any veterinary operation or for giving any veterinary attendance or advice, unless he or she is registered under this Act.

### 6. Qualifications necessary for registration

A person who—

(a) is registered or entitled by law to be registered as a veterinary surgeon in the United Kingdom or in a member State under any Act in force; or

(b) holds any certificate granted in the British Commonwealth, a member State or foreign country, recognised by the Board as furnishing a sufficient guarantee of the possession of requisite knowledge and skill for the efficient practice of veterinary surgery,

on payment of a fee of $5 and subject to the provisions of section 8, is eligible to be registered under this Act.

*(Amended by Act 21 of 2006)*

### 7. Keeping of Register and publication

(1) The Registrar shall keep a register to be styled the Veterinary Surgeons Register in which shall be registered the names, addresses and qualifications of all persons registered as Veterinary Surgeons under this Act.

(2) The Registrar, in the month of January in each year, shall publish in the Gazette a copy of the Register.

### 8. Registration of persons in Register

(1) Where any person desires to be registered under this Act he or she may apply to the Board and shall submit such evidence to the Board that he or she is eligible to be registered under section 6, and that he or she is a fit and proper person to be registered.

(2) The Board, if satisfied that such person is eligible and is a fit and proper person to be registered shall direct the Registrar to register such person.

### 9. Erasing name from Register

If any veterinary surgeon registered under this Act is convicted on indictment of any offence, or after due inquiry is found guilty of infamous or disgraceful conduct in any professional respect, the Board may cause the name of such veterinary surgeon to be removed from the Register.

### 10. Correction of Register

(1) The Registrar shall insert in the Register any alteration which may come to his or her knowledge in the name and address of any person registered.

(2) The Registrar shall erase from the Register the name of any deceased person.

(3) In the execution of his or her duties, the Registrar shall act on such evidence as in each case appears sufficient.

## PART 2 VETERINARY OFFICERS

### 11. Definition

In this part “**veterinary officer**” means a veterinary surgeon in the service of the Government.

### 12. Appointment of Veterinary Officers

It is lawful for the Governor General to appoint such persons as he or she may think fit, being veterinary surgeons, to be veterinary officers of Saint Lucia, who shall receive such emoluments as may be assigned to them by the Governor General and House of Assembly.

### 13. Duties of Veterinary Officers

It is the duty of veterinary officers to give without further remuneration veterinary attention and animal husbandry advisory and supervisory services to Government Departments, institutions and livestock projects and such other services connected with animal health and animal husbandry as the Governor General may direct and such other duties as may be specified by any statute in force.

### 14. Fees for private practice

(1) A veterinary officer is entitled to demand and receive fees according to the tariff set out in Schedule 1 for private practice.

(2) The Governor General may amend such tariff or make new tariffs of fees and any such amendment or new tariff shall be published in the Gazette and shall take effect on the date of such publication or on such other date as may be specified therein.

### 15. Rights of a national of a member State

(1) A national of a member State who is registered as a Veterinary Surgeon under this Act does not require a work permit or a visa to enter Saint Lucia for the purpose of practising as a Veterinary Surgeon.

(2) Subsection (1) applies despite anything to the contrary in any other law.

*(Inserted by Act 21 of 2006)*

### 16. Equal application of Act to a national of a Member State

Despite anything to the contrary in any other law, the provisions of this Act shall apply equally and without discrimination to a citizen of Saint Lucia and a national of a Member State. *(Inserted by Act 21 of 2006)*
