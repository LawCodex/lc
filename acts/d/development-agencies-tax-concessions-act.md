### AN ACT to provide tax concessions to certain development agencies and for related matters.

Commencement [11 September 1999]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as Development Agencies (Tax Concessions) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**development agency**” means an agency specified in the Schedule;

“**Comptroller of Customs**” means the person so appointed under the Customs (Control and Management) Act;

“**customs duty**” means duty imposed on imported or purchased goods;

“**Minister**” means the Minister responsible for finance;

“**prescribed**” means prescribed in the regulations made under section 12;

“**professional staff**” means senior officers employed at the managerial, administrative and technical level.

### 3. Application

(1) A development agency may apply to the Minister for a permit for the importation of equipment or vehicles free of customs duties.

(2) An application made under subsection (1) shall be in such manner and form as may be prescribed.

### 4. Grant of permit

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Minister may grant a permit applied for under section 3.

(2) A Minister shall not grant a permit for the importation of a vehicle under subsection (1), unless the development agency owns less than 3 vehicles imported under that subsection.

(3) A permit granted under subsection (1) shall specify the equipment or vehicles to be imported and shall be in such form and subject to such terms and conditions as may be prescribed.

### 5. Exemption from customs duties and taxes

(1) Upon production of a permit to the Comptroller of Customs, a development agency may import free of all customs duties such equipment and vehicles as are specified in the permit.

(2) All equipment and vehicles imported under subsection (1) are subject to the condition that they shall be used exclusively by the development agency for its official use.

### 6. Special marking

The Comptroller of Customs may require that all equipment and vehicles imported or purchased under a permit be marked in a prescribed manner.

### 7. Disposal of equipment and vehicles

(1) Equipment and vehicles imported under section 5 shall not be disposed of within 5 years from the date of import unless the development agency pays to the Comptroller of Customs all customs duties applicable to the goods to be disposed of.

(2) A purchaser of any equipment or vehicle disposed of by the development agency after a period of 5 years is liable for the payment of all customs duties applicable to the goods disposed of.

(3) A development agency or person who fails to comply with the provisions of subsections (1) or (2) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not less than $5,000.

(4) Despite subsection (3), all sums payable under subsections (1) and (2) shall be recoverable by the Comptroller of Customs in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure.

### 8. Inventory of exempt articles

(1) A development agency shall—

(a) keep an inventory of all equipment and vehicles obtained under a permit granted under section 4(1);

(b) permit the Comptroller of Customs or any person authorised by the Comptroller of Customs to inspect and take copies or extracts of the inventory kept under paragraph (a);

(c) grant to the Comptroller of Customs or a person authorised by the Comptroller of Customs access to any of its premises for the purpose of examining the equipment, vehicles and inventory referred to in paragraph (a).

(2) A development agency which fails to comply with any of the provisions of this section commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $5,000.

### 9. Termination of permit

The Minister may, by written notice, suspend for such time as he or she thinks fit or revoke a permit granted to a development agency where the Minister is satisfied that—

(a) the permit was obtained by false statement;

(b) the development agency has failed—

(i) to comply with the terms and conditions of the permit, or

(ii) to pay any sum payable under the provisions of this Act.

### 10. Professional staff exemption from taxes

(1) Non-national professional staff employed by a development agency shall be exempt from the payment of income tax and other duties and taxes on all emoluments received by reason of their employment with the development agency.

(2) Professional staff employed by a development agency shall be exempt from the payment of travel tax when travelling out of Saint Lucia on the official business of a development agency.

(3) Professional staff employed by a development agency and recruited from outside Saint Lucia shall, within 6 months of their arrival in Saint Lucia, be entitled to import free of all customs duty, all personal and household effects including one motor vehicle per family.

(4) On completion of their tour of duty, non-national professional staff employed by a development agency shall be entitled to export out of Saint Lucia all personal and household effects including one motor vehicle free of all export duties, and other related taxes.

### 11. Power to amend Schedule

The Minister may, by order published in the Gazette, amend the Schedule.

### 12. Regulations

The Minister may make regulations for the carrying out of the provisions of this Act, and in particular, may make regulations—

(a) prescribing the form and manner in which applications shall be made for any permit which may be granted under this Act and the forms of such permits;

(b) prescribing the fees to be paid under this Act;

(c) prescribing the equipment and vehicles to be exempted under the Act;

(d) prescribing the form of the inventory to be kept in respect of equipment and vehicles imported or purchased under a permit granted under this Act;

(e) prescribing any other thing which may be, or is required to be prescribed under this Act.
