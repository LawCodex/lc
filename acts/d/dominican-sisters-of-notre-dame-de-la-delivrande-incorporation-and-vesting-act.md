### AN ACT to provide for the Incorporation and Vesting of the Dominican Sisters of Notre Dame de la Delivrande and for matters connected therewith.

Commencement [1 November 1986]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Dominican Sisters of Notre Dame de la Delivrande (Incorporation and Vesting) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Corporation**”; “Religious Order”; or “Order” means the Dominican Sisters of Notre Dame de la Delivrande;

“**charitable purpose**” means a home for the poor, the aged or an orphange;

“**Registrar**” means the Registrar of Companies.

### 3. Incorporation of the Dominican Sisters of Notre Dame de la Delivrande

The following members of the Religious Order are hereby declared and appointed a corporation or body corporate with continuance forever and perpetual succession by the name of The Dominican Sisters of Notre Dame de la Delivrande, and possessed of a corporate seal and by that name may sue and be sued in all courts in Saint Lucia—

(a) the Mother General of the Order;

(b) the Regional Superior of the Order;

(c) the Local Superior of the Order;

(d) the Novice Mistress of the Order; and

their successors-in-office, whose names and addresses shall be notified to the Registrar in the manner provided by section 4.

### 4. Notification of names and addresses of officers of the registrar

(1) The names and addresses of the persons holding the several offices referred to in the preceding section shall be notified from time to time by the Local Superior to the Registrar and registered in his or her office and be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

(2) A person last registered in the manner provided by subsection (1) shall be deemed to be a member of the Religious Order authorised to execute any document under the provisions of section 7.

### 5. Appointment of officers notification and registration not invalidated by defects

No act or proceeding of the Corporation shall be invalidated by reason of any defect in the appointment of any person to any of the offices mentioned in section 3 or by reason of any defect in the notification or in the registration required by subsection (1) of section 4.

### 6. Corporate Seal

The Seal of the Corporation shall be of such design and pattern as the Corporation may determine.

### 7. Execution of documents

No deed or document purporting to be executed by the Corporation shall be of any force or validity unless it is sealed with the Corporate Seal and signed by not fewer than 2 members of the Corporation described in section 3.

### 8. Vesting of property

All property movable and immovable now vested in any person or persons for or on behalf of the Religious Order shall be and the same is hereby vested in the Corporation for the same estate and interest and subject to the same covenants, terms and conditions (if any) as the same where held by or vested in the persons hereinbefore mentioned.

### 9. Powers of Corporation

The Corporation shall have power—

(a) to acquire, hold, purchase, lease, possess and enjoy any lands and hereditaments whatsoever, absolutely or for leasehold or for any other estate or interest therein and for all other property, real or personal;

(b) to give, grant, let, change, improve, manage, develop, exchange, lease, mortgage, sell, convey, assign, dispose of, turn to account or otherwise deal with all or any of the property both present and future, or any part thereof held by or vested in the Corporation;

(c) to borrow, raise, or secure the payment of money in such manner as may be thought fit and in particular by the issue of debentures or script charged upon all or any of the property, both present and future, held by or vested in the Corporation, and to redeem and pay off any such securities; and

(d) to appoint a person as the attorney of the Corporation either generally or for a limited period and for such purposes and with such powers as may be stated in a power of attorney, and to revoke any such appointments.

### 10. Exemption from stamp duty

The Corporation shall be exempt from the payment of stamp duty in respect of any purchase of immovable property to be used as a residence for the Religious Order or for any charitable purpose.

### 11. Savings

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect or be deemed to prejudice or affect the right of Her Majesty The Queen, Her Heirs and Successors, or of any body, politic or corporate or of any other person or persons and those claiming by, from or under them, except such as are mentioned in this Act.
