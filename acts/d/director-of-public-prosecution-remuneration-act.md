### AN ACT to provide for the payment of remuneration to the Director of Public Prosecutions and other related matters.

Commencement [1 April 1995]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Director of Public Prosecutions (Remuneration) Act.

### 2. Remuneration

There shall be paid to the holder of the office of the Director of Public Prosecutions or to a person lawfully performing the functions of that office a salary of $86,400 per year.

### 3. Allowances

The Director of Public Prosecutions is entitled to—

(a) an entertainment allowance of $1,200 per year;

(b) a telephone allowance of $600 per year; and

(c) a travelling allowance of $4,800 per year.
