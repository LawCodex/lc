### AN ACT for the constitution of district courts of judicature for Saint Lucia; for the regulation of the jurisdiction, sittings, and practice and procedure thereof; for the appointment of the magistrates and other officers thereof, and of justices of the peace, and the regulation of their duties; and for other like purposes.

Commencement [22 June 1888]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the District Courts Act.

### 2. Interpretation of terms

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**district**” means a judicial district constituted under this Act;

“**court**” means a district court;

“**criminal**” includes quasi-criminal, and, with reference to matters of jurisdiction, comprehends all such matters not falling within the term “civil”.
