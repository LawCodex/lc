### AN ACT to empower diplomatic agents and consular officers to administer oaths, take affidavits and to perform notarial acts.

Commencement [6 October 1984]

### 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Diplomatic Agents and Consular Officers (Oaths and Notarial Acts) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Act—

**“consular officer**” means, subject to subsection (2), any person, including the head of a consular post, entrusted in that capacity with the exercise of consular functions;

“**consular post**” means any consulate general, consulate, vice-consulate or consular agency;

“**diplomatic agent**” means, subject to subsection (3), the head of a mission or a member of the diplomatic staff of a mission;

“**mission**” means an Embassy or High Commission.

(2) A reference to a Saint Lucia consular officer includes a reference to a consular officer of a State other than Saint Lucia, acting as such on behalf of the Government of Saint Lucia.

(3) A reference to a Saint Lucia diplomatic agent includes a reference to a diplomatic agent of a State other than Saint Lucia acting as such on behalf of the Government of Saint Lucia.

### 3. Saint Lucia diplomatic agent may administer oaths

(1) Every Saint Lucia diplomatic agent or Saint Lucia consular officer exercising his or her functions as such in any country or place other than Saint Lucia may, in that country or place administer any oath, take any affidavit or statutory declaration and do any notarial act. Every such oath, affidavit, statutory declaration or notarial act administered, taken or done by or before any such person shall be as effectual as if administered, taken or done by or before any lawful authority in Saint Lucia.

(2) Any document purporting to bear the seal and signature of any person authorised by this section to administer an oath in testimony of any oath, affidavit, statutory declaration or act being administered, taken or done by or before him or her shall be admitted in evidence without proof of the seal or signature being the seal or signature of that person, or of the official character of that person.

### 4. Diplomatic agent of any State may administer oaths if so authorised by laws of that State

(1) A diplomatic agent or consular officer of any State exercising his or her functions as such in Saint Lucia may, if authorised to do so under the laws of that State, administer oaths, take affidavits or statutory declarations and do notarial acts—

(a) required by any person for use in that State or under the laws thereof; or

(b) otherwise required by a national of that State but not for use in Saint Lucia except under the laws of some other State.

(2) The Cabinet may by order exclude or restrict the provisions of subsection (1) in relation to the diplomatic agents or consular officers of any State if it appears that in such State Saint Lucia diplomatic agents or Saint Lucia consular officers are not permitted to perform functions corresponding in nature and extent to those authorised by that subsection.

### 5. Jurat

A person before whom any oath, affidavit or statutory declaration is taken or made under this Act shall state in the *jurat* or attestation at what place and on what date the oath or affidavit is taken or made.
