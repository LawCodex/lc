### AN ACT to provide for the establishment and maintenance of a Law Library.

Commencement [16 March 1985]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Law Library Act.

### 2. Establishment of Library

There is hereby established a Law Library called the Saint Lucia Law Library.

### 3. Property of the Law Library

All the books, pamphlets, publications, documents and other property of any kind whatsoever now appropriated for the purpose of a Law Library and usually kept in the Court House at Castries in Saint Lucia, and all such property hereafter acquired for such purposes shall constitute the Saint Lucia Law Library.

### 4. Members of the Committee

The general management and control of the Saint Lucia Law Library shall be vested in and exercised by a committee comprising—

(a) the resident puisne judge of Saint Lucia;

(b) the Attorney General or his or her nominee;

(c) the Registrar of the High Court; and

(d) two members of the Saint Lucia Bar Association to be appointed by the Governor General for a period of 2 years.

### 5. Meetings and Quorum

(1) The Committee shall meet at least twice in each year, and at such other times as they may think fit.

(2) At any Committee meeting 3 forms a quorum.

### 6. Chairperson

The resident puisne judge of Saint Lucia or, in his or her absence, the Attorney General is chairperson of the Committee.

### 7. Questions to be decided by a majority of votes

All questions arising at any meeting of the Committee shall be decided by a majority of votes, and the chairperson shall have an original vote on all questions and a casting vote if the votes are equal.

### 8. Committee to make rules

(1) The Committee may make rules for the general management, upkeep and control of the Library hereby established including provisions relating to the purchase, preservation and loan of the books and periodicals, the subscriptions and fines payable by members and remuneration to the Librarian.

(2) The Rules shall not come into force unless approved by Cabinet and published in the Gazette.
