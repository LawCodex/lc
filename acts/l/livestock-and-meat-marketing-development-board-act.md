### AN ACT to provide for the establishment of a Livestock and Meat Marketing Development Board and connected matters.

Commencement [5 September 1964]

### 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Livestock and Meat Marketing Development Board Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**Agency**” means the Agency for International Development of the United States of America;

“**The Agreement**” means the Agreement made between the Government of Saint Lucia and the Agency for International Development of the United States of America relating to the establishment of a feed mill and corn storage facilities;

“**drugs**” mean all veterinary drugs including patent medicines, antibiotics, sulfonamides, vaccines, vermicides and supplementary iron, and includes related products such as insecticides and disinfectants;

“**livestock feed**” includes cereal grains, complete mixed feed rations and feed additives such as protein supplements, food concentrates, minerals, salts, vitamins, antibiotic supplements, etc.;

“**Minister**” means the member of the Cabinet charged with the responsibility for the subject of agriculture.

### 3. Incorporation of Livestock and Meat Marketing Development Board

(1) For the purposes of this Act there is hereby established a Board to be called the Livestock and Meat Marketing Development Board (hereinafter referred to as “the Board”) which shall be a body corporate under that name with perpetual succession a common seal which shall be officially and judicially noticed. The seal shall be authenticated by the signature of the chairperson and the secretary of the Board. The Board may sue and be sued in its corporate name and may for all purposes be described by that name.

(2) Until a seal is provided in accordance with the provisions of this section, a stamp bearing the words “The Livestock and Meat Marketing Development Board” and authenticated by the signatures of the chairperson and the secretary may be used instead of such a seal.

### 4. Power of the Board to acquire and dispose of property

(1) The Board may purchase or otherwise acquire and hold and may take on lease any moveable property acquired for the purposes of this Act, and may dispose of any such moveable property.

(2) The Board may purchase or otherwise acquire and hold and may take on lease any immoveable property and any interest therein required for the purposes of this Act.

(3) The Board may, with the consent of the Governor General sell and alienate any immoveable property or interest therein vested in the Board and lease any such property or interest for any term, and may, without such consent, lease any immoveable property or interest therein for any term not exceeding 10 years provided the full rental value in respect of the same be reserved year by year on such lease, and no premium be payable in respect thereof.

### 5. Membership of the Board

(1) The Board shall consist of 3 *ex officio* members, and 6 other members 2 of whom shall be butchers, 2 of whom shall be producers of livestock, and 2 other persons representing consumers to be appointed by the Governor General on the recommendation of the Minister.

(2) The *ex officio* members shall be the persons holding or acting in the following public offices—

(a) the Director of Agricultural Services who shall be the chairperson of the Board;

(b) the veterinary officer who shall be deputy chairperson;

(c) the Director of Finance and Planning.

(3) At any meeting of the Board, provided a quorum is present the Board may act despite any vacancy in the number of its members.

(4) The Governor General may authorise payment out of the funds of the Board of any sum way of remuneration or other allowance to the chairperson and other members of the Board. However, no remuneration or other allowance shall be paid under this subsection to a member of the Board who is also the secretary thereof.

### 6. Tenure of office of appointed members

(1) An appointed member shall hold office for a period of 2 years from the date of his or her appointment.

However, the Governor General may remove an appointed member from his or her office at any time by notice in writing to him or her to that effect. In addition, an appointed member at any time may resign his or her office by notice in writing under hand to that effect given to the Governor General.

(2) The Governor General may, on the recommendation of the Minister, appoint any person to act in the place of an appointed member in case of the latter's absence or inability to perform his or her duties.

(3) Where an appointed member is absent from any 3 consecutive meetings of the Board except some reason approved by the Governor General, such person shall cease to be a member of Board.

(4) If a vacancy occurs by death, resignation, effluxion of time, or otherwise, in the office of any appointed member, the vacancy may be filled by the Governor General in the manner provided by section 5(1), but any person appointed to fill a casual vacancy shall hold office so long only as the member in whose place he or she is appointed would have held office.

(5) Any person ceasing to be an appointed member shall be eligible for re-appointment to the Board.

(6) The appointment, resignation or removal of an appointed member shall be notified in the Gazette by the Secretary to Cabinet. However, failure to give such notice shall not affect the validity of the appointment, resignation or removal.

### 7. Power of Governor general to make Regulations

(1) The Governor General may make Regulations—

(a) regulating the procedure of the Board and requiring returns and reports;

(b) prescribing the principles to be followed by the Board in the exercise of their powers;

(c) generally for carrying out the purposes of this Act.

(2) Until regulations regulating the procedure of the Board are made under subsection (1), the provisions of the Schedule to this Act shall have effect. Upon the coming into force of such regulations the provisions of the said Schedule shall cease to have effect.

(3) Subject to any such regulations (and to the Schedule to this Act while in effect) the Board may regulate its own procedure.

### 8. Appointment of Secretary and other officers and servants

The Board, with the prior approval of the Governor General, may appoint, at such remuneration and on such terms and conditions as it may determine, a Secretary and such officers and servants as may be necessary and proper for the efficient management of the business of the Board, and may require any officer or servant so appointed to give such security as may be deemed proper for the due execution, of his or her duties.

### 9. Powers and duties of Secretary

(1) The Secretary shall be the Chief Executive Officer of the Board and shall have all such powers and perform all such duties as are or may be conferred or imposed upon him or her by this Act, or by the Board, or by any rules or regulations made under this Act.

(2) The Secretary shall attend all meetings of the Board and shall keep or cause to be kept minutes of the proceedings at every such meeting. He or she shall not, unless he or she is a member of the Board, be entitled to vote at any such meeting but may be called upon by the chairperson to give such information as may be required by the Board at any meeting.

### 10. Indemnity of members and officers of the Board

The members of the Board and the Secretary and other officers and servants of the Board may be indemnified out of the assets of the Board from and against all actions, proceedings, costs, charges, losses, damages and expenses which they or any of them shall or may incur or sustain by reason of any act done or omitted in or about the execution of their duty in their respective offices except such as they shall incur or sustain by or through their own wilful neglect or default respectively.

### 11. Funds of the Board

The funds of the Board shall consist of—

(a) such sums as may accrue from the operations of the Board;

(b) such sums as may be borrowed by the Board;

(c) such sums as may, with the approval of the House of Assembly, be appropriated from public funds;

(d) such sums as may accrue from the disposal of its assets;

(e) such other sums as may from any source and at any time be granted or allocated to the Board.

### 12. Loans, mortgages and advances

(1) The Board shall not, without the prior approval of the Governor General, borrow any sum of money, or mortgage or pledge its assets.

(2) The Governor General or the Minister of Finance may by warrant under their hand with the approval of the House of Assembly, authorise the Accountant General to make advances to the Board in such sums and on such terms and conditions as the Governor General may think fit for the purpose of the Act.

### 13. Application of funds of the Board

The funds of the Board shall be applied towards—

(a) the remuneration of its members, officers and servants;

(b) the payment of the cost of acquisition of any property, moveable or immoveable, for the purposes of this Act. However, land or building shall not be purchased or leased by the Board without the prior approval of the Governor General;

(c) the purchase, construction, maintenance and replacement for the purposes of this Act of any machines or equipment;

(d) the payment of the rent of premises used by the Board in the carrying on of its business;

(e) the payment of all rates, taxes and insurance on any land, building or other property acquired by the Board;

(f) the payment of all expenses duly incurred for the purposes of this Act;

(g) the purchase of livestock and the processing and marketing of meat or meat products;

(h) the repayment of advances, sums borrowed by the Board, and interest thereon;

(i) the creation of a reserve fund, as the Board may, in its discretion, consider fit;

(j) any other purpose which the Board may, with the approval of the Governor General, decide to be for the purposes of this Act.

(2) Money standing to the credit of a reserve fund may be invested in securities generally or specifically approved by the Governor General for that purpose and the Board may and with like approval sell or vary any or all such securities.

### 14. Duties of the Board

It is the duty of the Board—

(a) to buy livestock for slaughtering and conversion into meat and meat products;

(b) to sell meat and meat products;

(c) to operate the feed mill and corn storage facility constructed to process corn and other livestock feed, established under the Agreement;

(d) to improve, by such means as it may determine, the processing and marketing of meat and meat products;

(e) upon being required to do so by the Governor General, to make recommendations on any matter directly or indirectly relating to or affecting the processing and marketing of meat and meat products.

### 15. Powers of the Board

The Board may—

(a) establish and operate depots for the purchase, grading and delivery of livestock and for the grading and sale of meat and meat products;

(b) enter into contracts for the purchase and transport of livestock upon such terms and conditions as the Board may deem fit;

(c) subject to any powers vested in municipal authorities establish and operate abattoirs and other plant for the slaughtering of livestock and for the preparation, grading and processing of meat and meat products;

(d) establish feed mills and depots wherein drugs and livestock feed may be purchased, processed and sold on such terms and conditions as the Authority may consider fit;

(e) do all things needful for the development, preservation and maintenance of a livestock industry in the island;

(f) regulate the grading of livestock and prescribe the grades which shall be employed in the purchase of livestock and the prices to be paid for each grade;

(g) regulate the grading of meat and meat products and prescribe the grades which shall be employed in the sale of meat and meat products and the prices to be paid for each grade at the depots and abattoirs operated by the Board;

(h) purchase, transport and handle livestock in or outside of the Island in such quantities, at such prices and at such times as the Board may determine;

(i) slaughter, handle and sell meat and meat products in or outside of the Island in such quantities, at such prices and at such times as the Board may determine.

### 16. Power of Board to make Rules

(1) The Board may, with the approval of the Governor General, make rules—

(a) prescribing the method in which livestock may be purchased from producers;

(b) prescribing the prices to be paid in respect of each grade or type of livestock;

(c) prescribing the price to be paid in respect of each grade or type of meat and meat product;

(d) prescribing the times for accepting livestock at the various depots established in accordance with section 15(a);

(e) prescribe forms of licences to be issued to dealers in meat and meat products;

(f) generally for carrying out the purposes of this Act.

(2) Rules made under this section may prescribe in respect of any contravention thereof or failure to comply therewith a penalty on summary conviction not exceeding $100.

### 17. Power of Governor General to give directions to the Board

The Governor General may, after consultation with the chairperson of the Board, give to the Board directions as to the policy to be followed by the Board in the exercise of its functions, and the Board shall comply with all such directions.

### 18. Formation of the Meat Marketing Fund

(1) All monies due to the Board shall be paid into the Treasury and shall form a fund to be called “The Livestock and Meat Marketing Development Fund”, in this Act referred to as “The Meat Marketing Fund”.

(2) The accounts of the Meat Marketing Fund shall be kept separate from all other accounts in the Treasury, and all the debts of the Board shall be discharged from such fund.

(3) All payments from the Meat Marketing Fund shall be made on the written order of the secretary countersigned by the chairperson of the Board.

### 19. Accounts and Books

(1) The Board shall keep true accounts of—

(a) all sums of money received and expended by the Board and the matters in respect of which such receipts and expenditures take place;

(b) the stock in trade of the Board; and

(c) the assets and liabilities of the Board which shall be prepared in such a manner as to show separately the fixed or capital assets of the Board and the sums owing on account of loans or advances.

(2) The Board shall cause to be kept proper and sufficient books of account and other books in relation to its business and such books shall be kept at the office of the Board and the principal audit officer and his or her deputies.

### 20. Funds and Accounts of the facilities established under the Agreement

(1) All monies derived from the sale of corn at the feed mill and corn storage facility established under the Agreement shall be deposited into a separate identifiable account to be called “The Feed Mill Account” in one of the banks in the Island and may be withdrawn by the Government of Saint Lucia.

(2) The funds of the Feed Mill Account shall be used only for the purchase of livestock feed, drugs and other supplies to be used in stocking the aforesaid feed mill and corn storage facility.

(3) The livestock feed, drugs and other supplies shall be sold subject to the provisions of the Pharmacy Act or any order made thereon to the participants of the Heifer Peace Corps Pig project and all other livestock producers in the island.

(4) The Government of Saint Lucia through the Authority shall maintain a separate set of accounts for all transactions financed under the Agreement and shall forward to the Agency every 3 months commencing from 30 September 1964 a statement showing the following—

(a) the names and addresses of suppliers;

(b) the invoice, dates and numbers of the said suppliers;

(c) a general statement of the merchandise purchased; and

(d) evidence of the payment thereof.

(5) The Board shall retain in its records for inspection and review by the Agency, applicable contracts or purchase orders between suppliers, the suppliers' detailed invoices and evidence of payment.

(6) The Board shall also make available to the Agency its sales records covering receipt by the ultimate user.

(7) All the aforesaid records shall be available to the Agency for a period of not less than 3 years following the transaction.

### 21. Annual report and audit

The Board shall furnish to the Governor General as soon as possible after the expiration of each financial year a report upon the administration of the affairs of the Board during the preceding year, together with a balance sheet and statement of the revenue and expenditure of the Board duly audited by the principal audit officer or such other auditor as may be approved by the Governor General. The report, balance sheet and statement shall as soon as possible thereafter be laid before the House of Assembly.

### 22. Exemption from Income Tax

Despite the provisions of the Income Tax Act the income of the Board is wholly exempt from income tax.

### MEETINGS OF THE BOARD

1. The Board shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient for transacting its business.

2. The chairperson may at any time summon a meeting of the Board and on a requisition by any 3 members he shall summon a meeting. Such requisition shall state the object for which the meeting is required to be summoned.

3. In cases in which the chairperson shall so direct, decisions of the Board may be taken by the recording of the opinions of members on papers circulated among them. Where papers are circulated, the chairperson may direct that the papers shall not be circulated to any member who through interest, illness, absence from the island or otherwise is, in the opinion of the chairperson, incapacitated from voting on such papers.

4. The quorum of the Board at any meeting or for the purpose of voting on papers circulated shall be 4, but subject thereto, the Board may act despite any vacancy in the number of its members.

5. The decisions of the Board shall be by the majority of votes. In any case in which the voting shall be equal, the chairperson shall have a second or casting vote.

6. In the absence of the chairperson and deputy chairperson, the members present shall appoint one of their numbers to act as chairperson of any meeting.

7. Minutes of each meeting shall be confirmed by the chairperson at the next succeeding meeting. Certified copies of such minutes when so confirmed shall be forwarded to the Minister.
