Commencement [26 August 1947 – Parts 1 &2, 16 December 1887 – Part3]

### AN ACT to provide for the establishment of local authorities for Towns and Villages, and to make provision in other respects for the Government of Towns and Villages.

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Local Authorities Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**by-law**” means any by-law made under the authority of this Act and for the time being in force;

“**clear income**” means the gross income received by a person for his or her own use, less such payments made by him or her as would be allowable as deductions – expecting those allowed in respect of earned income, residence in Saint Lucia, British nationality, a wife, children and life insurance premiums – in arriving at a taxable income under the provisions of any Income Tax Act for the time being in force in Saint Lucia;

“**clerk**” means the clerk of the local authority appointed under section 15 of this Act;

“**house**” includes any building or erection whatever;

“**local authority**” means town council, village council or other district council constituted under this Act;

“**lot of land**” includes a vacant lot;

“**Minister**” means the Minister responsible for local government; *(Inserted by Act 22 of 1970)*

“**person**” includes woman;

“**proprietor**” includes any person in whose name any lot of land is rated for taxes in the assessment list;

“**resident**” means in relation to the district in respect of which a residence qualification is claimed, being ordinarily resident in the district for a period of 6 months or more and since the completion of such period of residence not having been ordinarily resident in any other place outside the district for a period of 6 months or more.

## PART 2 GOVERNMENT OF TOWNS AND VILLAGES

### 3. Division of Saint Lucia into districts

(1) For the purposes of this Act Saint Lucia with the exclusion of the Town of Castries, may be divided into districts to be called respectively—

(a) urban districts;

(b) village districts; and

(c) rural districts.

(2) The urban districts, village districts and rural districts shall respectively be subject to the jurisdiction of local authorities called town councils, village councils, or district councils, invested with the powers mentioned in this Act.

### 4. Soufriere and Vieux-Fort to be urban districts

The towns of Soufriere and Vieux-Fort shall be urban districts and there shall be in and for each of the aforesaid towns a body corporate to be called respectively the Soufriere Town Council and the Vieux-Fort Town Council being the local authority for the government of the respective towns.

### 5. Choiseul, Laborie, Micoud, Dennery, Gros Islet, Anse-la-Raye and Canaries village districts

The villages of Choiseul, Laborie, Micoud, Dennery, Gros Islet, Anse-la-Raye and Canaries shall be village districts and there shall be in and for each village a body corporate to be called respectively the Choiseul Village Council, the Laborie Village Council, the Micoud Village Council, the Laborie Village Council, the Micoud Village Council, the Dennery Village Council, the Gros Islet Village Council, the Anse-la-Ray Village Council and the Canaries Village Council being the local authority for the government of the respective villages.

### 6. Powers of cabinet to create districts and define boundaries

(1) Cabinet may declare any area in Saint Lucia to be an urban, village or rural district for the purposes of this Act and may define the boundaries of such district and also of any district created by this Act and may alter, restrict, or enlarge the same.

(2) Cabinet may at any time declare that any urban, village or rural district shall cease to be such district for the purposes of this Act and may make any orders necessary to give effect to the declaration.

#### Constitutions of Local Authorities

### 7. Local authority not less than seven members for urban districts, nor less than five for village or rural districts

A local authority constituted under this Act shall consist of such number of members in the case of an urban district not being less than 7 and in the case of a village or rural district not being less than 5 but in neither case exceeding 9 as shall in each case be determined by the Governor General.

### 8. Urban district authority either wholly elected or partly nominated

(1) Members of the local authority for an urban district may be either wholly elected in accordance with the provisions for election hereinafter contained or partly elected and partly nominated by the Governor General.

Provided that at least half of the number of members constituting the local authority shall be elected.

(2) At the first meeting in each year the members of an urban district authority present and voting shall elect by ballot one member not being a public officer to the Chairman to hold office for the year.

### 9. Village and rural district authorities nominated

(1) Members of the local authority for a village or rural district may, with the exception of 2 members who shall be elected, be nominated, but the Governor General shall have power to direct that any increased proportion of their number be elected.

(2) At the first meeting in each year of the members of the local authority for a village or rural district the members of the local authority for the village or rural district shall elect from among themselves a Chairman, who shall be elected by a majority of the members of the local authority for the village or rural district present and voting, and if the office of Chairman fails vacant at any time the members of the local authority for the village or rural district shall, as soon as practicable, elect another such member to that office.

(3) The Chairman shall assume office immediately after his or her election and shall continue therein until he or she ceases to be a member of the local authority for the village or rural district or until his or her successor in office is elected whichever first happens.

(4) A retiring Chairman shall be eligible for re-election. Provided that no person shall be eligible for election and re-election as Chairman more than 3 consecutive times.

*(Amended by Act 7 of 1967)*

### 10. Tenure of office

The tenure of office of any member of Council constituted under this Act shall unless the Council be sooner dissolved be of 2 years duration.

### 11. Capacity

Every local authority shall have an office in the district under its jurisdiction and may make use of an official seal, shall be entitled to make contracts and to sue and be sued in its name and to acquire, hold, mortgage and dispose of all property real or personal, movable or immovable.

### 12. Qualifications of members of local authority

(1) No person shall be qualified to be elected as a member of a local authority unless he or she—

(a) is a British subject;

(b) has attained the age of 21 years;

(c) can read and write the English language;

(d) is not a person of unsound mind so found by any law in force in Saint Lucia; and

(e) either—

(i) is the proprietor or occupier of land or of a house and land in the urban village or rural district of an assessed annual rental value of not less than $28.80 as recorded in the assessment list of the district, or

(ii) being resident in the urban, village or rural district is in receipt of a clear annual income of not less than $144, or

(iii) being resident in the urban, village or rural district has paid direct taxes for the previous year to any amount whatsoever.

(2) For the purposes of the foregoing subsection the expression “direct taxes” shall include Income Tax payable in respect of income taxable in Saint Lucia under any law for the time being in force and any tax leviable on property situate within the boundaries of the urban, village or rural district but no other taxes whatever.

### 13. Forfeiture of seat by member

If any member of the local authority—

(a) becomes bankrupt; or

(b) is absent from any 3 consecutive meetings of the local authority without leave of the Chairman; or

(c) is absent from Saint Lucia for more than 30 days without leave of the Chairman; or

(d) is in any part of Her Majesty's dominions or in any territory under Her Majesty's protection convicted of perjury or sentenced to death or penal servitude or imprisonment for a term exceeding 12 months,

he or she shall cease to be a member of the local authority and his or her seat shall thereupon become vacant and a notification of the facts and circumstances of such vacancy shall be published in the Gazette and in the case of a nominated member, sent to the Governor General.

### 14. Resignation of member

Any member of the local authority may resign his or her seat by giving 14 days notice in writing to the Chairman.

#### Functions and Powers of Local Authority

### 15. Duties of local authority

(1) It shall be the duty of the local authority to provide for the collection and expenditure for the benefit of the urban, village or rural district under its jurisdiction of monies authorised by law to be raised for such purpose; to provide for the good government and improvement of the district; and to enforce the provisions of this or any other Act relative thereto and also all regulations and by-laws made under this or any other Act.

(2) The local authority may make by-laws for the rule and good government of the district generally and in particular with respect to the following matters—

     (i) the naming, cleanliness, regularity and width of streets and roads, and the opening, making, repairing, stopping up and diverting thereof;

     (ii) the removal, erection, demolition and closing of buildings and the numbering of buildings and lots;

     (iii) traffic on streets and roads;

     (iv) suppression and abatement of nuisances;

     (v) the establishment of markets, slaughter houses, wash houses and latrines and the regulation thereof; *(Amended by Act 22 of 1972)*

     (vi) the licensing and fixing the amount payable in respect of each licence, prescribing the method of computing the amount so payable, the time for obtaining such licence and conditions attaching to same

(a) for the inspection and regulation of market and slaughter house dues;

(b) for carrying out any trade or business;

(c) for the keeping of dogs and other animals;

(d) for porters, jobbers, boatmen stevedores;

(e) for all forms of public entertainment and amusement; *(Substituted by Act 22 of 1972)*

     (vii) the keeping of animals domestic or captive birds and bees; *(Amended by Act 22 of 1972)*

     (viii) the destruction of insects;

     (ix) unoccupied land;

     (x) cemeteries and graves;

     (xi) protection against fires;

     (xii) tanks and wells;

     (xiii) drainage and sewerage;

     (xiv) scavenging and the removal and disposal of excreta;

     (xv) the sale and inspection of livestock, meat, fish, vegetables and other foodstuffs and of liquids intended for human consumption;

     (xvi) lighting;

     (xvii) mortuaries and dead bodies;

     (xviii) the cleanliness of public places or places of public resort;

     (xix) the storing and sale of dangerous or inflammable substances;

     (xx) guides for tourists and the prevention of annoyance to tourists by beggars and idlers;

     (xxi) public entertainments and performances;

     (xxii) management and control of public cultural institutions and places of public recreation.

(3) Any by-law made by the local authority shall be subject to the approval of Cabinet and shall not come into operation until it has been so approved and published in the Gazette.

(4) Such by-law shall have full effect within the boundaries of the district under the jurisdiction of the local authority and where so expressly stated within one mile thereof but shall only operate in addition to and not in derogation of any power conferred by any other law upon any person or authority and exercisable with respect to the entire State.

(5) With the sanction of the Governor General the local authority may borrow any sum or sums of money for the purpose of carrying out works of public utility on such terms and on such security as the Governor General shall deem fit; contribute towards or undertake any scheme or institution for educational or charitable purposes connected with or for the benefit of the district; acquire by lease, purchase or otherwise lands and buildings or any part thereof for any purpose of public utility or any part thereof for any purpose of public utility, and in particular with respect to waterworks, markets, parks and places of recreation, streets and roads.

(6) The local authority may with the prior approval of the Governor General appoint fit and proper persons to be officers of the authority.

(7) The local authority may with the approval of the Governor General appoint a fit and proper person to be clerk to the local authority.

#### Qualification and Registration of Voters

### 16. Qualification of electors

(1) Every person shall be entitled to be registered as a voter for the purpose of the election of members of the local authority having jurisdiction over the district to which the register refers, and when registered, to vote at the election of members of the local authority, who—

(a) is a British subject;

(b) has attained the age of 21 years; and

(c) is not a person of unsound mind so found under any law in force in Saint Lucia; and

(d) possesses one of the following qualifications:

(i) is the proprietor or occupier of land or of a house and land in the urban village or rural district of an assessed annual rental value of not less than $9.60, or

(ii) being resident in the urban, village or rural district is in receipt of a clear annual income of not less than $28.80, or

(iii) being resident in the urban, village or rural district has paid direct taxes for the previous year to any amount whatsoever.

(2) For the purposes of the foregoing subsection the expression “direct taxes” shall have the same meaning as in section 12 hereof.

(3) The annual rental value of property, the receipt of income, and the payment of taxes in respect of which any right to vote is claimed shall be such as is proved to the satisfaction of the clerk to the local authority.

### 17. Register of voters

(1) The local authority shall cause a register to be kept of all persons claiming and entitled to be registered as voters in respect of the district under its jurisdiction.

(2) Any person claiming to be registered as a voter shall before the first day of June in any year deliver or cause to be delivered to the clerk to the local authority a notice of his or her claim to be registered in the form prescribed in Schedule 1 and the clerk shall examine such claim and receive such evidence as may be necessary to prove to his or her satisfaction that a person making the claim possesses the qualifications in respect of which he or she claims to be registered as a voter. On being satisfied, the clerk shall cause his or her name to be entered in the register of voters.

(3) In the month of June in every year the clerk shall examine the register of voters and strike out the names of any persons he or she has grounds to believe are dead and give to any person he or she believes has ceased to retain the qualification in respect of which he or she is registered one month's notice in writing to enable such person to furnish proof of his or her qualification to vote and if such person either fails to furnish proof or furnishes proof which is considered by the clerk to be unsatisfactory then he or she shall strike out the name of such person from the register.

(4) The revised list of voters shall be published in the Gazette in the month of October in each year.

#### Proceedings at Elections

### 18. Terms of office and rotation of members of the Council

(1) On the 31 December, in every year, one-half of the elected members of the Council shall go out of office.

(2) The members to go out of office shall always be those members who have been longest in office without re-election; and if this test is indecisive in the case of any 2 or more members, the Council shall determine which of such members shall go out of office.

(3) Any retiring member shall be eligible for re-election.

(4) Subject to the provisions of section 10 a nominated member shall hold office for such period and on such terms as the Governor General shall think fit.

### 19. Clerk to be Returning Officer

The clerk to the local authority shall be the Returning Officer for the purposes of an election.

### 20. Time and place of election

The Returning Officer shall fix the date not earlier than the first and not later than the fifteenth day of December in any year for holding the election and shall cause notices announcing the same to be posted on the outer doors of the office of the local authority, and of any church and police station within the limits of the jurisdiction of the local authority and in any other conspicuous places he or she shall think fit, at least 2 weeks before the date fixed for election.

### 21. Procedure for nomination

Seven days before the date fixed for election the Returning Officer shall attend at 11:00a.m. at the place mentioned in the notice and shall receive the nominations of persons qualified for election as members of the authority until noon of the same day. Each candidate shall be proposed and seconded by duly registered voters.

### 22. Uncontested election

If there are not more candidates proposed than there are seats to be filled, the Returning Officer shall at noon of the same day or so soon after as is practicable publicly declare those persons, being duly qualified for election, to be elected as members of the local authority.

### 23. Contested election

If there are more candidates proposed than there are seats to be filled the Returning Officer shall state that a poll shall be held for the election of members from those candidates from 9:00 a.m. till 12:30p.m. and from 1:30p.m. till 4:00p.m. of the day fixed for election and at the same place.

### 24. Receiving of votes

(1) The Returning Officer shall attend accordingly and receive and record the votes of those duly qualified to vote.

(2) Voting shall be by ballot. At 4:00 p.m. on the day of holding such election the Returning Officer shall count the votes, the candidates or their duly authorised agents not being more than one for each absent candidate being present if they elect. The Returning Officer shall then publicly declare the persons having the largest number of votes to be duly elected members of the local authority.

(3) The result of the election shall as soon as is practicable thereafter be reported by the Returning Officer to the Governor General and shall be published in the Gazette and in at least one public newspaper of Saint Lucia.

### 25. Questions at election

The Returning Officer, subject to the right of appeal provided for in section 28, shall have full power to determine all questions which arise in relation to the election.

### 26. List of persons voting

At every election the Returning Officer shall make a correct list of the voters present and voting at such election, and shall cause the manner of voting to be kept secret as far as rests in his or her power.

### 27. Member replacing one who has vacated his or her seat

A member of an authority who is elected or nominated in place of a member who has vacated his or her seat before serving his or her full term shall hold office in the same manner as, and for the remainder of the time for which, such member in whose place he or she is elected or nominated would have held. Provided that when an election is held such election shall be held within 2 months of such vacancy having occurred and the voting and other proceedings shall be conducted in the same manner as is hereinbefore provided in respect of the ordinary elections of the Authority.

### 28. Disputes

Any dispute arising out of any election, whether the Returning Officer has decided thereon or not, may be referred by Cabinet to the Magistrate having jurisdiction in the district and the Magistrate shall thereupon inquire into and decide the same and the Magistrate's decision shall be final.

### 29. Dissolution

(1) Cabinet may upon representation made by not less than 12 registered voters of any district and upon being satisfied that the local authority having jurisdiction in the district has been acting persistently in a manner prejudicial to the inhabitants as a whole or contrary to the intentions of this Act dissolve the local authority.

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in section 8 in the event of the local authority being dissolved under the foregoing subsection or in the case of an election held under this Act, where there are not 4 or more registered voters present and voting or where there are no candidates to fill any or all of the seats to be filled by election, the Governor General may in his or her discretion nominate the persons necessary to complete the constitution of the Council.

Provided that on a dissolution or on a failure to elect any members at a general election the Governor General may appoint any person or persons on such terms as he or she shall think fit to act in the place of a local authority for carrying out the provisions of this Act.

#### Finance

### 30. Payments of money to Treasurer for town or village or rural district Fund

(1) All monies due to a local authority shall be paid to the Treasurer and shall form a fund to be called the Urban, Village or Rural District Fund which shall be kept distinct in the Treasurer's books from all other accounts.

(2) All payments from the Fund as constituted in subsection (1) shall be made by the written order of the clerk of the district council, countersigned by the Chairman or in his or her absence by some other member duly authorised by the local authority.

### 31. Composition of Fund

(1) The Fund shall be composed of monies derived in the district from—

(a) House and Land Tax imposed under Part 3 of this Act;

(b) Water Rates imposed under section 8 of the Towns and Villages (Water Supply) Act;

(c) Rates, taxes, dues, licences and fees payable to the Council under this Act or any rule or by-law made thereunder;

(d) fines for breaches of the by-laws of the Council;

(e) monies derived from the sale, hire or mortgage of properties vested in the Council;

(f) all other sources from which the same may lawfully be derived; including any vote or contribution from the general revenue of Saint Lucia.

(2) Estimates of Revenue and Expenditure for any year shall be drawn up annually by each local authority and submitted to the Governor General on or before the first day of October in the preceding year and the Governor General may amend the same as he or she shall think fit. Unless the sanction of the Governor General shall have been previously obtained no payment from the Fund as constituted in section 30(1) shall be authorised by the local authority except in accordance with the estimates duly approved by the Governor General.

### 32. Accounts

The accounts of every local authority shall be open at all reasonable times to inspection by the Audit Department, any member of the local authority or any member of the **Legislative Council.**

### 33. Cabinet may make loans to local authorities

It shall be lawful for Cabinet from time to time to make loans from the General Revenue of Saint Lucia to the local authorities of the several Towns and Villages mentioned in sections 4 and 5 of this Act for the purpose of effecting such improvements as Cabinet may deem advisable.

### 34. Loans to be paid to the credit of the urban or village Fund

Every such loan shall be paid by the Treasurer under the warrant of the Governor General to the credit of the town or village Fund of the particular local authority to which the loan has been made: Provided that, until the approval of the **Secretary of State** has been first obtained—

(a) no single loan to any local authority shall exceed $960;

(b) no loan or loans to any local authority shall exceed in the aggregate $2880.

### 35. Interest on and repayment of loans

Every loan shall bear interest at a rate not exceeding $6 per centum per annum and the loan with interest thereon shall be repaid, within such time and in such instalments as Cabinet may decide at the time of the making of the loan, from the Fund of the particular local authority to which the loan was made.

### 36. Loans to be a first charge upon urban or village Fund

Every such loan with interest accruing thereon shall be a first charge on the Fund of the particular local authority effecting the loan.

### 37. Statements of expenditure to be furnished by clerk for publication

The clerk to every local authority to which a loan has been made shall furnish detailed statements of the expenditure of each loan at such times and in such form as the Governor General may direct, and all such statements shall be published in the Gazette.

#### Miscellaneous

### 38. Procedure at meeting

The local authority shall have power to make rules relating to the time and place and mode of convening meetings, the attendance of members, the powers and duties of officers and servants, the form and order of debates and the general regulation of proceedings of the local authority and the mode of conducting its business.

### 39. Warden replaced by clerk

All duties performed prior to 26 August 1947 by the Warden in connection with the assessment and collection of rates and taxes specified in section 31 of this Act shall be performed by the clerk to the local authority.

### 40. Pubishment of person offending against this act

(1) Every person who commits an offence against this Act or against any by-law made under this Act shall, on being convicted, be liable to a penalty not exceeding $48, and in default of payment to imprisonment with or without hard labour, for any term not exceeding 2 months.

(2) Every proceeding under this Act shall be in the name and at the instance of the Clerk in whose urban, village or rural district the offence was committed, before the Magistrate of the Judicial District in which such district is situated.

## PART 3 HOUSE AND LAND TAX

### Assessment and Payment of tax

### 41. Making up of general assessment list of houses and lots of land

(1) The clerk of the local authority of every urban or village district shall, in or before the month of February in the year 1940 and in every fifth year thereafter prepare an assessment list, hereinafter called the general list, according to the Form contained in the Schedule 2 to this Act, of all houses and lots of land in his or her Town or Village, and of the names of the owners or attorneys of the owners or, if the owners are not known, of the tenants or occupiers, and of the rent as assessed by such clerk. *(Added by Act 14 of 1939)*

(2) The clerk shall, in the month of February in any intermediate year in which no general list has been prepared, prepare, according to the Form as aforesaid, a supplementary list of all houses and lots of land in his or her Town or Village, and of the names of the owners or attorneys of the owners or, if the owners are not known, of the tenants or occupiers, and of the rent as assessed by such clerk, in the case where there has been, since the publication of the last general list, any error or omission, or any change of ownership, or of occupation, or of representation, or of the loss or deterioration or increase in value of the property, or otherwise, in order to correct, revise and supplement the general list in respect of any errors, omissions or changes which may have been discovered or have occurred since the last general assessment.

If in any intermediate year no such errors, omissions or changes have been discovered or have occurred the clerk shall prepare a supplementary list showing a nil return.

(3) The Minister may appoint some fit and proper person to be joint assessor with the clerk to assist in the preparation of either of such lists, and such joint assessor shall have such duties and be remunerated in such sums, payable out of the revenues of the Town or Village for which he or she is appointed, as the Minister shall direct.

*(Amended by Act 22 of 1970)*

(4) The rent of any house or lot of land shall for the purposes of this Act, be considered to be the amount of rent which would be obtained from such house or lot of land if rented at its full value, such amount not being less than the amount of rent actually payable.

(5) The clerk and the assessor so appointed as aforesaid may enter any house or premises in the Town or Village for which they have been appointed for the purpose of assessing the rental of the same. Every person who obstructs the clerk or the assessor so appointed in the discharge of this duty commits an offence against this Act.

(6) The clerk shall forward the general list to Cabinet, who shall fix a rate or tax, not exceeding 8% , to be paid on the assessment value in such list and in any supplementary list prepared as aforesaid in any intermediate year of all houses and lots of land by the owners or attorneys of the owners or, if the owners are not known, by the tenants or occupiers respectively.

(7) The clerk shall transmit both such lists as aforesaid to the Minister for publication in the Gazette, and shall cause copies to be posted at the Police Station and other public places in the Town or Village to which such lists refer.

Provided that Cabinet may, in the month of February in any year subsequent to the year 1940, alter the rate or tax fixed under subsection (6) of this section within the limit prescribed in the said subsection, whereupon the clerk concerned shall transmit a list, which shall be deemed for all purposes to replace the existing general list, and be subject to all the provisions of this Act affecting the same, in the form of the general list, in respect of the rate or tax so altered, to the Minister for publication in the Gazette, and shall cause copies to be posted as provided in the preceding subsection.

*(Amended by Act 22 of 1970)*

### 42. Making and hearing of objections to assessment, etc.

(1) After the publication of either of such lists, any person who objects to any assessment, or to his or her inclusion or exclusion, shall lodge his or her objection in writing with the Clerk to the local authority to which either of such lists refers within 14 days after such publication in the Gazette.

(2) The Local Authority shall fix a day or days within 14 days next after the time for lodging such objections, for hearing such objections and shall dispose of the same by confirmation, alteration, inclusion in or exclusion from such lists as the case may be, and shall cause a list of such objections and the decision of the Local Authority in each case to be published in the next issue of the Gazette after the expiration of the last mentioned 14 days.

(3) Any person aggrieved by the decision given against him or her in such list may appeal therefrom by lodging an objection in writing with the Clerk of the District Court of the district in which is situated the Town or Village to which such list refers, and with the Clerk to the Local Authority within 14 days after such publication in the Gazette.

(4) The Magistrate of the said Court shall fix a day or days for the hearing of such objections and of evidence of witnesses of value or otherwise tendered by the parties, and shall confirm, alter or amend any such assessment or include in or exclude from such list, as the case may be, the name of the person objecting with or without costs. An appeal shall lie on a point of law from the decision of the Magistrate under this section to the High Court in accordance with the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure as to appeals from the District Court, but in all other respects the decision of the Magistrate shall be final.

(5) Such costs shall be according to the scale of fees contained in Schedule 3 of this Act an may be recovered at the instance of the Clerk, in the same manner as costs are recovered after judgment in civil cases in a District Court.

Provided that any costs adjudged to be paid by the Clerk shall be borne and paid out of the Urban or Village District Fund of the town or village to which the objection relates on the production of a certificate of the Magistrate specifying the amount of costs against the respective local authority.

*(Amended by Act 22 of 1970)*

### 43. Making and publication of alteration of assessment lists

(1) After all objections have been disposed of, the clerk shall immediately prepare a list of the alterations made by the District Court to either list.

(2) Each list of alterations shall be published in the same manner as the original general or supplementary list.

(3) The assessment of any house or lot of land as entered in the original general list or in the list or alterations made by the Court, as the case may be, shall be held to be its ascertained rent for the purpose of taxation and the rate fixed by Cabinet to be paid shall be the tax to be paid during the year of the publication of such general list and the following year, except so far as it has been added to, altered or amended by the original supplementary list or the list of alterations made by the Court, as the case may be, which, to the extent so added to, altered or amended, shall be the list on which the rate fixed by Cabinet shall be paid for the intermediate year in which there has been no general assessment.

### 44. Notification in gazette that assessment list may be seen at the treasury

On or before the 1 June in each year (on which date the rates and taxes shall become due and payable) the Treasurer shall notify in the Gazette that a copy of the original list and the list of alterations, if any, may be seen at the Treasury, or at the office of the Sub-Collector, or at the Police Station, and that the rates and taxes due must be paid to the Treasurer on or before the 31 August then next following.

*(Amended by Act 17 of 1974)*

### 45. Tax ranks as a first privileged claim

All rates and taxes, together with the interest accrued and costs shall rank by privilege without registration upon the proceeds of the movables which according to the provisions of this Act may be taken in execution for the same, as well as upon the proceeds of the movable or immovable property in respect of which the rates or taxes are due, prior to all claims except law costs and debts due to the Crown.

Provided that excepting unpaid rates or taxes payable in or before the year 1932 only the rates or taxes payable for the year of sale if payable at the time of sale and their accessories and the rates or taxes payable for the year preceding the year of sale and their accessories shall be entitled to such priority.

### 46. Tax in default is a judgment debt and interest is payable thereon

After September in any year the rates and taxes for that year shall be deemed to be judgment debts due by the persons assessed, as defendants, in favour of the Treasurer, as plaintiff, and shall be payable to the Treasurer together with a further sum, by way of interest, equal to 21/2% of the amount of rates and taxes due, for every period of 3 months or part thereof computing from the first day of June preceding until paid, with costs; and execution for the recovery of the same may be issued on the written application of the Treasurer as hereinafter provided.

*(Amended by Act 17 of 1974)*

### 47. Tax in default may be registered like a judgment

A certificate under the hand of the Treasurer, stating the amount of any unpaid rate or tax and the date it became due, may be registered like a judgment in the Registry of Deeds and Mortgages and with like effect; and after the satisfaction of this judgment a certificate of payment, under the hand of the Treasurer, may be registered in the Registry of Deeds and Mortgages and shall have the effect of discharging this judgment; such certificate shall be registered free of charge.

### 48. Notification of execution on movables

The Treasurer shall on or before 15 September notify in the Gazette, and in a newspaper of Saint Lucia at the expense of the Urban or Village District Fund, that after August movables may be taken in execution and sold for rates and taxes unpaid after 30 September. On or before the 17 September or so soon thereafter as practicable, the Treasurer shall cause a copy of such notification to be affixed to an outer door of each Church (subject to the permission of the Incumbent or Curé), District Court, Police Station and Sub-Collector's Office in the Towns and Villages.

*(Amended by Act 17 of 1974)*

### 49. Notification of execution on houses and land

The Treasurer shall on or before 15 October notify in the Gazette that after September buildings which are movables, land, and land with immovable buildings, may be taken in execution and sold for any rates or taxes unpaid after 31 October. On or before 17 October, or so soon thereafter as practicable, the Treasurer shall cause a copy of such notification to be affixed as directed in the last preceding section.

*(Amended by Act 17 of 1974)*

### 50. Publication of notices not essential

The failure to publish or affix or serve any of the foregoing notices shall not affect the exigibility of any unpaid rate or tax.

### 51. Execution on movables after august by district court

(1) Execution on movables may be made at any time after September for any unpaid rate or tax under a writ of execution according to the Form prescribed in Schedule 4 of this Act issued by the Treasurer to the clerk of the District Court in the district in which the assessment was made, on the movables of the defendant or on the movables which may be found upon the property in respect of which the rate or tax is due. But execution on buildings which are movables shall only be made after 31 October.

*(Amended by Act 17 of 1974)*

(2) The writ may be issued against one or more defendants or one or more movable buildings, and shall be sufficient authority for any bailiff of a District Court, or any police constable, or other person directed by the Clerk of the Court to collect any such sum payable or to proceed to seizure and sale by way of execution.

(3) In respect to any property seized, any such writ is deemed to be returnable, in the District Court to which it was issued, at any time within 6 weeks of the seizure of such property.

### 52. Sufficiency of writ with list of defaulters or buildings attached

In all cases where default has been made by several persons it shall be sufficient for the purposes of seizure and sale for the Treasurer to attach to any writ a list signed by him or her setting forth the names of such defaulters, or indicating the movable buildings, and the amount of taxes and fines and other sums due by or in respect of such persons or buildings respectively.

### 53. Disposal of surplus proceeds or sale of movables

Any unpaid surplus of the proceeds of the sale of movable, remaining after deduction of the whole amount exigible under the writ of execution in the District Court, shall be paid to the person in whose possession the movables were when the seizure was made. If there by a claim to such surplus which is admitted by the person against whom the seizure was made, it shall be paid to the claimant; but if the claim is contested the surplus shall be retained subject to the decision of the District Court in which the writ of execution was issued.

### 54. Executionof immovables after September by the Supreme Court

(1) Execution on land only or on land together with immovable buildings may be made at any time after September for any unpaid rate or tax under a writ of execution according to the Form prescribed in Schedule 5 to this Act issued by the Treasurer to the Sheriff, and the proceeds of sale shall after deduction by the Sheriff of the rates and taxes due, with interest, costs and expenses, be returned in the Supreme Court for distribution among the persons, and their creditors, who according to the record and the claims filed setting out and proving ownership, are judicially held to be entitled to the same.

(2) Any such writ may be issued against one or more defendants or immovables and shall be sufficient authority for any bailiff of the Supreme Court, Sheriff's officer, or any police constable or other person directed by the Sheriff to collect the sums payable.

(3) Before issuing any such writ the Treasurer shall cause to be served on each person assessed whose rates or taxes are unpaid after 30 September, and on the owner (if known) of the land or the land with immovable buildings in respect of which rates or taxes are unpaid after 30 September a notice of his or her intention to issue such writ at any time after 7 days after the service of such notice.

(4) If from any cause the notice cannot conveniently be served on any person as aforesaid, it shall be sufficient to affix the same in some conspicuous manner to any part of the immovable intended to be sold.

(5) In respect to any immovable mentioned therein such writ is deemed to be returnable in the Supreme Court at any time within 4 months from the first publication of the advertisement of sale of such immovable in the Gazette.

### 55. Clerk to assist with information

The Clerk shall give information and assistance for the purpose of identifying and describing the movable buildings, lands or lands with immovable buildings to be taken in execution, and shall give assistance generally to the Treasurer in carrying out the provisions of this Act.

### 56. Postponement of sales

If from any cause a sale cannot or does not take place, or is incomplete, on the day appointed for the sale, it may be postponed or continued from time to time until it is effected or completed. Provided that in the case of executions in the Supreme Court a further notice stating the time and place of the adjourned sale shall be published once at least in the Gazette and, if the Sheriff deems it necessary, in a newspaper of Saint Lucia. And if a sale in the Supreme Court or the District Court does not take place before the writ of execution is returnable into Court, the delay for returning the said writ shall be *ipso facto* extended and it may be returned within 7 days of the sale.

### 57. Seizure of immovables for purposes of sale unnecessary

For the purpose of recovering taxes by the sale of any immovable no seizure of such immovable shall be necessary, but it shall be sufficient to describe such immovable and to advertise it as in ordinary civil cases.

### 58. Irregularities do not invalidate execution or sale

No execution or sale under the provisions of this Act shall be invalid for any error, omission, irregularity or informality, but if the existence of any such is lawfully urged and is proved and an actual injustice results, the Court, Judge or Magistrate shall maintain the proceedings previous, and if a resale is necessary, it shall take place in accordance with the conditions prescribed in the judgment or order of the Court, Judge or Magistrate.

### 59. Sale of immovables by instalments

Subject to the provisions of this Act, any execution and sale under this Act, whether against movable or immovable property, shall be made on the same terms and conditions and have the like effect as in the case of corresponding executions and sales in ordinary civil cases, under the law and procedure for the time being in force governing any such execution and sale.

### 60. Cost of collecting and recovering tax in arrear

The costs and expenses of collecting and recovering rates and taxes which have become judgment debts for being in arrear and unpaid after September shall be as set forth in Schedule 6 to this Act; and when recovered, all such costs shall be paid into the Treasury for the use of Saint Lucia, and all such expenses to the parties entitled.

*(Amended by Act 17 of 1974)*

### 61. Taxes may be paid before 1 June

It shall be lawful for the Treasurer, before 1 June in any year, to receive payment of rates and taxes for that year.

### 62. Procedure in this act applicable to collection of rates under other acts

Where any law provides that any rate shall be assessed and collected according to the method prescribed for the assessment and collection of House and Land Tax under this Act, the assessed amount of such rate shall be inserted in the List in Schedule 2 to this Act, and such rate and the House and Land Tax shall be paid and collected together in a total sum, with interest and costs, in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

### 63. Power of cabinet to make new schedules

Cabinet may from time to time, by notice in the Gazette, alter, add to or rescind any of the Schedules to this Act or prescribe new Schedules in place of them.

### 64. Right of tenant or occupier paying tax to deduct it

Any tenant or occupier of any property on which a tax levied under this Act is due may, on payment of such tax, whether after distress or not, deduct the amount paid, together with compensation for all loss actually suffered in respect of any distress, from any rent which may then be due, or may thereafter become payable, in respect of such property; and in the event of any action being brought by the owner of any such property for the recovery of rent from any such tenant or occupier, the Court before which such action is brought shall allow to such tenant or occupier the amount so paid and compensation for loss so sustained as aforesaid.

### 65. Power to cabinet to remit tax in specified cases

Cabinet may remit wholly or in part any tax on the ground of the poverty of the person liable, or to the property in respect of which the tax is payable having become deteriorated or being used exclusively for charitable and educational purposes, or where it seems to him or her to be just and equitable so to do subject to the payment to the Treasurer out of the Urban or Village Fund of any costs or expenses necessarily incurred by him or her, and the power to remit a tax given by the said section shall include the power to remit any interest, fees or other charges thereon.

### 66. Appropriation of taxes, fines and fees

The taxes and interest imposed and recovered under this Part shall be paid into the Treasury, and shall be carried to the credit of the Urban or Village District Fund of the particular Town or Village in respect of which the same may have been imposed and recovered.

### 67. Power to Cabinet to suspend the operation of Part 3

Cabinet may from time to time, by Proclamation, suspend the operation of this Part in respect of all or any of the Towns and Villages mentioned in this Act, and in like manner may annul the said Proclamation in regard to any or all of the Town and Villages mentioned therein, in which case this Part shall be again in full force with regard to the Town or Village or Towns or Villages specified in such last-mentioned Proclamation.

### 68. Power to alter time mentioned in this act

Cabinet may by Order at any period and on such terms and conditions as may be deemed fit alter or extend the time fixed under this Act for the payment of taxes or alter or extend the time prescribed for the performance of any duty.
