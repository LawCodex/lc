### AN ACT to provide for licences for animals and vehicles.

Commencement [30 November 1907]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Licences (General) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**“carriage**” means and includes any carriage, gig, buggy, dog-cart or other vehicle on springs, but does not include a motor vehicle as defined in the Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic Act;

“**cart**” means and includes any cart, dray, wagon or drag usually drawn by animals;

“**constable**” means any police or rural constable;

“**description**” means such a description as will enable the animal or article described to be identified, and in respect of an animal it shall include a statement of the animal's sex, age, colour and distinguishing marks;

“**district**” means a judicial district constituted under the District Courts Act and any Act amending the same;

“**horse**” includes any stallion, mare, gelding, colt or filly;

“**owner**” includes the person in actual possession of any animal or article in respect of which a licence is required to be taken out under this Act, but does not include a person who, being the servant or agent of the licensed owner of such animal or article, shall upon request of a revenue officer or constable declare his or her and such licensed owner's full and true name and place of residence;

“**revenue officer**” includes all persons whose right or duty it may be to exact the performance of or to perform any duty or service relating to the treasury, customs or excise departments;

“**road**” means and includes any public highway or public byway constructed or maintained at the public expense, and any street in any town or village.

### 3. Licence for animals and vehicles

(1) The owner of every horse, mule, carriage or cart shall in every half-year take out a licence to keep or use the same, from the Director of Finance and Planning or the revenue officer in charge of a revenue office in the district in which such owner resides, and shall pay therefor respectively the following fees—

(a) Animal Licences

horse—for every horse—$1.20

mule—for every mule—$1.20;

(b) Vehicle Licences

bicycle—for every bicycle—$0.60

carriage—for every carriage (other than a motor car as defined by the Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, or any Act for the time being in force)—$0.60 for every wheel

cart—for every cart—$0.30 for every wheel

tricycle—for every tricycle—$0.60,

Such licence shall be in the form in the Schedule hereto.

(2) It is not necessary to take out a licence in respect of any such animal or vehicle not used on a road nor in respect of any such animal or a cart used by its owner on any road bordering on or intersecting any estate on lot of land of which he or she is the proprietor or occupier solely for the conveyance of any goods or burden in the course of husbandry from any one part of such estate or lot of land to another part of the same.

### 4. Duration of licences

Every person who is liable to take out any licence under this Act shall do so within the first 14 days of the months of January and July in every year; and each such licence shall expire on 30 June or the 31 December next following the day of the date of such licence. However, if any person after 14 January or July in any year becomes the owner of any animal or article in respect of which a licence is required by this Act to be taken out, he or she shall within 14 days after the day on which he or she shall become such owner (unless he or she has previously obtained a transferred licence as provided in the next succeeding section) take out a licence for the then current half-year whether any other person has or has not previously taken out a licence for the same period in respect of the same animal or article, and he or she shall pay the full fee hereinbefore prescribed for such licence.

### 5. Notice must be given of sale of animal, etc.

(1) Whenever any person sells or otherwise transfers the ownership of any animal or article in respect of which a licence has been taken out by him or her under this Act, he or she shall give notice in writing of such sale or transfer to the Director of Finance and Planning, or to the revenue officer in charge of a revenue office in the district in which he or she resides, within 7 days after such sale or transfer, and he or she shall in such notice give a description of such animal or article and the name and place of residence of the person to whom the same has been so sold or transferred. And he or she may in such notice apply for a transfer of such licence to the new owner, in which case the Director of Finance and Planning or revenue officer shall write on such licence the name and place of residence of such new owner together with words to the effect that such licence has been transferred to him or her, and such transferred licence shall operate in all respects as the original licence and the holder thereof shall be subject to all the provisions of this Act as if such transferred licence had been the original licence.

(2) Every person who neglects or fails to give the above notice in the manner required commits an offence. However, the provisions of this section shall not apply to the sale of gunpowder.

### 6. Licensee may replace animal, etc., without taking out new licence

(1) Whenever any person who has taken out a licence under this Act in respect of any horse, mule, carriage or cart shall during the currency of such licence lose such animal or article, or sell or otherwise transfer the ownership of such animal or article, he or she may replace the same by another similar animal or article without being liable to take out another licence for the same period in respect of the animal or article so substituted. However, such person shall, within 7 days after the day on which he or she shall so substitute such animal or article as aforesaid, give notice in writing of such substitution and of such loss, sale or transfer, to the Director of Finance and Planning or to the revenue officer in charge of a revenue office in the district in which such person resides, and he or she shall in such notice make application for a licence to him or her in place of the licence already taken out as aforesaid and give a description of such animal or article so substituted as aforesaid, and the Director of Finance and Planning or revenue officer shall thereupon strike out from such licence the description of such former animal or article and insert instead the description of such substituted animal or article together with words to the effect that the licence so amended is a substitute for the original licence, and such substituted licence shall operate in all respects as the original licence and the holder thereof shall be subject to all the provisions of this Act as if such substituted licence had been the original licence.

(2) Every person who neglects or fails to give the above notice in the manner required commits an offence.

### 7. Duplicate licence may be issued when licence is lost

It is lawful for the Director of Finance and Planning, upon such proof as he or she shall consider satisfactory that any licence issued under this Act has been lost or destroyed, to issue a duplicate of such licence, without any payment being made therefor, which has the word “Duplicate” written on it, and it shall operate in all respects as the original licence, and the holder thereof shall be subject to all the provisions of this Act as if such duplicate licence had been the original licence.

### 8. Penalty for neglecting to take out a licence

(1) Every person liable to take out a licence under this Act neglecting to do so in the manner prescribed by this Act, where no other penalty is herein provided, is liable on summary conviction to a penalty not exceeding $24, and in default of payment to be imprisoned, with or without hard labour, for a period not exceeding 2 months, and where at the time of conviction he or she has not taken out the required licence he or she is liable to pay in addition to such penalty a sum equal to the amount of the fee payable for the licence so neglected to be taken out; and payment of such additional sum may be enforced in like manner as the payment of such penalty.

(2) Where upon any conviction under this section the said additional sum has been adjudged to be paid and has been paid by the defendant, the person receiving the same shall as soon as practicable thereafter pay the same to the Director of Finance and Planning or to the revenue officer in charge of the nearest revenue office and thereupon the defendant shall be entitled to be given by the Director of Finance and Planning or such revenue officer, without further payment, the licence in respect of which such additional sum was adjudged to be paid.

### 9. Penalty for refusing to produce licence

It is lawful for any revenue officer or constable to demand and require from any person who shall have taken out a licence under this Act the production of such licence, and such person within a reasonable time after such demand shall produce such licence to such revenue officer or constable and shall permit him or her to read the same and (if he or she shall think fit) to take a copy thereof or of any part thereof, and if such person refuses or fails to produce such licence as aforesaid, or if he or she refuses to permit any licence which he or she may produce to be read or a copy as aforesaid to be taken, he or she commits an offence.

### 10. Licence to be produced or name and address given

It is lawful for any revenue officer or constable who has reason to suppose that any person is liable to take out any licence under this Act to demand and require from such person the production of such licence, and if such person does not within a reasonable time produce such licence or shall not permit such revenue officer or constable to read the same and take a copy thereof, it is lawful for such revenue officer or constable to require such person to declare immediately his or her full and true name and his or her place of residence, and if such person shall refuse or fail to declare his or her name and place of residence as aforesaid it is lawful for such revenue officer or constable to apprehend such person and take him or her to the nearest police station, and he or she is liable on summary conviction to a penalty not exceeding $24, and in default of payment to be imprisoned, with or without hard labour, for a period not exceeding 2 months.

### 11. Authority to enter upon lands to examine licences

It is lawful for any revenue officer or constable to enter and remain so long as may be necessary upon any lands or premises other than a dwelling house for the purpose of demanding and requiring the production of any licence under either of the 2 last preceding sections.

### 12. On reasonable suspicion that any gun is not licensed it may be seized and sold if not claimed

Any constable may seize and detain any gun in respect of which a licence is required to be taken out by this Act, if he or she reasonably suspects that no licence has been taken out in respect of such gun, and if no person claims such gun within 10 days after notice of such seizure given by advertisement posted publicly at the nearest police station in the district in which the seizure is made, the same shall be sold at auction at such police station by the constable in charge thereof in such manner as the Commissioner of Police may direct.

### 13. Obstructing persons acting under this Act an offence

Every person who shall in any wise hinder, molest or obstruct any revenue officer or constable in the exercise of the powers or duties conferred or imposed on him or her by this Act commits an offence.

### 14. Penalty for using licence issued to another person

Every person who allows any licence taken out by him or her under this Act to be used by any other person is liable on summary conviction to a penalty not exceeding $24, and any such other person using such licence is liable to a like penalty, and in either case, in default of payment, to be imprisoned, with or without hard labour, for a period not exceeding 2 months.

### 15. Penalty for not painting on cart the letter and number of the licence

Every person who takes out a licence under this Act in respect of a cart shall within 3 days thereafter have the letter and number of such licence painted in some conspicuous place at the right side of such cart and each such letter and number shall be legibly painted at least 2 inches in length in white on a dark ground. Every such person who does not have such letter and number painted as aforesaid on such cart is liable on summary conviction to a penalty not exceeding $9.60, and in default of payment to be imprisoned, with or without hard labour, for a period not exceeding one month.

### 16. Punishment for forging a licence

Every person who forges any licence required by this Act, or who knowingly uses any forged licence, is liable on summary conviction to a penalty not exceeding $240, and in default of payment to be imprisoned, with or without hard labour, for a period not exceeding 6 months.

### 17. Penalty for offences not otherwise provided for

Every person who commits an offence is liable on summary conviction, where no other punishment is provided, to a penalty not exceeding $48, and in default of payment to be imprisoned, with or without hard labour, for a period not exceeding 3 months.

### 18. Procedure in prosecutions

(1) Every offence against this Act or any rules made hereunder shall be heard and determined by a magistrate as an offence punishable on summary conviction, and the mode of procedure shall be according to the provisions of any law regulating the procedure before magistrates.

(2) Proceedings in respect of any such offence shall be taken by and in the name of the Director of Finance and Planning, the Commissioner of Police, the police officer or non-commissioned officer in charge of a police station or any person thereto authorised in writing by the Governor General, Director of Finance and Planning or Commissioner of Police.

### 19. Reward to witness

If any person gives evidence in any proceedings under this Act whereby any other person is ordered to pay a fine or penalty or sum of money, the Governor General may, if he or she sees fit, direct payment to the person giving such evidence of any sum not exceeding ½ of the amount recovered in respect of such order.

### 20. Onus of proof

Whenever in any proceedings under this Act the defendant claims that he or she has duly taken out a licence or delivered any notice or declaration required by this Act, or claims any qualification or exemption from liability, the burden of proving such licence, notice, declaration, qualification or exemption shall be upon the defendant and no proof in relation to such claim shall be required on the part of the complainant.

### 21. Rules and forms to be framed by the Governor General

(1) The Governor General may and as may be considered expedient for the better carrying into effect the provisions of this Act—

(a) make, alter or rescind rules;

(b) add to, revoke or alter the form prescribed in the Schedule hereto or any other forms required to be used under this Act; and

(c) direct that any forms or books be printed or distributed.

(2) Any person who offends against any of the rules made hereunder is liable on summary conviction to a penalty not exceeding $24, and in default of payment to be imprisoned, with or without hard labour, for a period not exceeding 2 months.

### 22. Register of licences to be kept

The Director of Finance and Planning and each revenue officer in charge of a revenue office shall keep a register of all licences issued by them respectively, specifying therein—

(a) the name and place of residence of the holder of each licence;

(b) the date and place of issue of each licence;

(c) the amount paid for each licence;

(d) the serial number of each licence;

(e) a description of each animal or article in respect of which a licence is issued;

(f) a statement showing whether the licence is an original, substitute, transferred or duplicate licence; and

(g) in the same register shall be mentioned the animals or articles in respect of which it is not necessary to take out a licence with the names of the owners of, and a description of, such animals or articles,

or specifying such further or other particulars in addition to or *in lieu* of the above as the Governor General may direct.

### 23. List of licences to be supplied to the Commissioner of police

The Director of Finance and Planning and each revenue officer in charge of a revenue office shall forward at the end of every month to the Commissioner of Police a list of licences (if any), issued by them respectively during such month, setting out such particulars as the Governor General may direct.

### 24. Forms of declarations to be issued from the Treasury

(1) The Director of Finance and Planning shall cause to be prepared and issued such forms of declarations as may be required for carrying out the purposes of this and the next succeeding section, as may be approved by the Governor General.

(2) Every person applying to take out any licence under this Act may be required by the Director of Finance and Planning or a revenue officer to fill up and sign a declaration or declarations in such form as shall be prescribed, wherein shall be stated among other things—

(a) whether such person keeps any horse, mule, carriage or cart, and if he or she does, the number, particulars and description thereof;

(b) in respect of which of such animals or articles he or she has taken out a licence and the date, number and place of issue of such licence; and

(c) if a licence is unnecessary in respect of any such animal or article, such person shall also state in what respect and upon what ground the licence is unnecessary.

### 25. Special notice requiring declarations to be made

(1) The Director of Finance and Planning or the revenue officer in charge of a revenue office may at any time direct a special notice to be served upon any person whatsoever requiring such person to fill up, sign and deliver to the person named in such notice a form of declaration to be left with such notice stating therein the several particulars required by the last preceding section and to pay to the person named therein within 14 days from the date of the service of such notice the fees for licences which he or she appears by such declaration to be liable to take out.

(2) Such notice may be served upon the person to whom it is directed, either by delivering a copy of it to him or her personally, or by leaving the same at his or her usual or last known place of abode.

(3) Every person who shall neglect or refuse to deliver any declaration in conformity with the provisions of this or the last preceding section, and every person who shall knowingly deliver any such declaration wherein the particulars required to be set out shall not be fully and truly stated, is liable on summary conviction to a penalty not exceeding $48, and in default of payment to be imprisoned, with or without hard labour, for a period not exceeding 3 months.

### 26. Appropriation of monies

All monies received under this Act, whether in respect of any licences issued, or of any order to pay penalties and sums of money, shall, where not otherwise provided, be paid to the Director of Finance and Planning for the public uses of Saint Lucia.

### 27. Governor General may alter scale of fees for licences

It is lawful for the Governor General to alter, conditionally or otherwise, the fees for licences hereinbefore prescribed by increasing, decreasing or annulling the same or any of them, and also to require a licence to be taken out under the provisions of this Act in respect of any animal or article other than those herein before mentioned or mentioned in any order by virtue of this section, and also to settle and prescribe the fees payable for such licences, and also to alter as aforesaid such last mentioned fees or any of them. However, before any such order comes into force it shall be approved by the House of Assembly.
