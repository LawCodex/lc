### AN ACT to incorporate Les Fils de Marie Immaculée (Saint Lucia) and to vest certain property in the body so incorporated.

Commencement [15 October 1966]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Les Fils de Marie Immaculée (Saint Lucia) Incorporation and Vesting Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act the terms “**Corporation**” or “**Religious Order**” shall mean Les Fils de Marie Immaculée (Saint Lucia).

### 3. Incoporation of Les Fils de Marie Immaculé (Saint Lucia)

The following are hereby declared and appointed a Corporation or Body Corporate to have continuance forever and perpetual succession by the name of “Les Fils de Marie Immaculée (Saint Lucia)” and possessed of a Corporate Seal and by that name may sue and be sued in all Courts in this Island—

The Superior of Les Fils de Marie Immaculée of Saint Lucia who for the time being is Rev. Fr. Paul Gouraud and his successor in office. The Assistant of Les Fils de Marie Immaculée, of Saint Lucia (hereinafter referred to as the Assistant who for the time being is Rev. Fr. Charles Grit and his successor in office; and one of the members of the Religious Order (not being a person holding any of the above mentioned offices) who shall be resident in this Island (hereinafter referred to as resident in this Island (hereinafter referred to as the “Procurator”) and whose name and address shall be notified to the Registrar of Companies in the manner provided by the next following section and who for the time being and until the name of some other person is notified and registered shall be Rev. Fr. Bernard Pacteau.

### 4. Notification of names and addresses of officers to registrar of companies

(1) The names and addresses of the persons holding the several offices referred to in the preceding section and the name and address of the Trustee shall be notified from time to time by the Superior to the Registrar of Companies and registered in his or her office and be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

(2) A person last registered in the manner provided by the preceding subsection shall be deemed to be a member of the Religious Order authorised to act under the provisions of section 7 of this Act.

### 5. Appointment of officers and notification and registration not invalidated by defects

No act or proceeding of the Corporation shall be invalidated by reason of any defect in the appointment of any person to any of the offices mentioned in section 3 of this Act or by reason of any defect in the notification or in the registration required by subsection (1) of section 4 of this Act.

### 6. Corporate seal

The seal of the Corporation shall be of such design and pattern as the Corporation may determine.

### 7. Execution of documents

No deed or document purporting to be executed by the Corporation shall be of any force or validity unless it is sealed with the Corporate Seal and signed by not less than 2 members of the Corporation.

### 8. Vesting of property

All property moveable and immovable now vested in any person or persons for or on behalf of the Religious Order shall be and the same is hereby vested in the Corporation for the same estate and interest and subject to the same covenants, terms and conditions (if any) as the same were held by or vested in the persons hereinbefore mentioned.

### 9. Powers of corporation

The Corporation shall have the following powers—

(a) to acquire, hold, purchase, lease, possess and enjoy any lands and hereditaments whatsoever, absolutely or for leasehold or for any other estate or interest therein and all other property, real or personal;

(b) to give, grant, let, change, improve, manage, develop, exchange, lease, mortgage, sell, convey, assign, dispose of, turn to account or otherwise deal with all or any of the property both present and future, so held or vested or any part thereof;

(c) to borrow, raise, or secure the payment of money in such manner as may be thought fit and in particular by the issue of debentures or script charged upon all or any of the property, both present and future, held by or vested in the Corporation, and to redeem and pay off any such securities;

(d) to appoint a person or persons as the attorney or attorneys of the Corporation either generally or for a limited period and for such purposes and with such powers as may be stated in the power of attorney, and to revoke any such appointments.

### 10. Savings

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect or be deemed to prejudice or affect the rights of Her Majesty The Queen, Her Heirs and Successors, or of any body politic or corporate or of any other person or persons except such as are mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, from or under them.
