### AN ACT to regulate and control the pasturage of animals on Government lands.

Commencement [21 April 1956]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Government Pasturage Lands Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires the expression—

“**animals**” means cattle as herein defined, horses, mules, asses, sheep, goats and swine;

“**appropriate area**” means the area in the vicinity of any government pasturage lands;

“**cattle**” means quadrupeds of the bovine family;

“**Government pasturage lands**” means any lands declared by the Governor General to be Government pasturage lands for the purposes of this Act and any lands let by Government to tenants whereon animals are kept;

“**licence**” means a licence issued in accordance with the provisions of this Act;

“**licensed animal**” means any animal admitted to or kept on Government pasturage lands under a licence as herein defined;

“**Livestock Officer**” means the person duly appointed to perform the duties of Senior Livestock Officer and includes the Director of Agricultural Services and such other officer as the Governor General may appoint to carry out the provisions of this Act;

“**owner**” includes a person who has any share or interest in any animal in respect of which an application for a licence has been made.

### 3. Governor General may declare pasturage lands

The Governor General may declare any lands owned acquired or controlled by Government to be Government pasturage lands for the purposes of this Act.

### 4. Management of Government pasturage lands and livestock schemes

The Livestock Officer is responsible for the carrying out of this Act and any livestock scheme prepared for Saint Lucia, and subject to the approval of the Governor General he or she may employ such workers as may be considered necessary for the purpose.

### 5. Animals on government pasturage lands to be licensed

Save under a licence issued by the Livestock Officer no animal shall be kept, tethered, or let loose, on Government pasturage lands.

### 6. Licences to issue only to owners and for animals duly examined

A licence shall not be issued in respect of any animal—

(a) except to a person who is an owner of such animals; and

(b) unless such animal has been examined by the Livestock Officer.

### 7. Application for licence

Any owner who is desirous of obtaining a licence shall apply in form one as set out in Schedule 1, and shall present the animal in respect of which the application has been made for examination and testing at such times and places as the Livestock Officer may direct.

### 8. Animals to be examined and may be tested

The Livestock Officer before issuing a licence for an animal shall examine visually and may examine and test clinically such animal and he or she may, in the case of cattle, include any of the following tests—

(a) the single intradermal comparative tuberculin test;

(b) the *brucella abortus* serum agglutination test;

(c) such other tests as may be determined by the Governor General.

### 9. Duty of Livestock Officer to issue licence

Where an applicant has complied with the provisions of this Act and the examination and testing of his or her animals give satisfactory results, the Livestock Officer shall issue to such applicant a licence in the relevant form set out in Schedule 1. Such licence is not transferable.

### 10. Pasturage dues

Upon the grant of a licence to any person he or she shall pay as pasturage dues the relevant fees set out in Schedule 2 or any amendments thereto.

### 11. Refusal to issue licence

Despite the provisions of section 9 the Livestock Officer may refuse to grant a licence where—

(a) examination or testing of such animal shows that it is unhealthy or otherwise unfit for pasturage with other animals;

(b) owing to deficiency of water or herbage, or owing to unsuitability of the genetic type, or in order to avoid or control disease, it is necessary to restrict or reduce the number of licensed animals on the Government pasturage lands in question;

(c) the applicant does not normally reside in the appropriate area or has or occupies other suitable land; or

(d) the applicant has been convicted of any offence in respect of stealing, receiving, unlawful possession or wounding any animal.

### 12. Cancellation of licences

The Livestock Officer may by notice in writing cancel any licence—

(a) where the holder of such licence has been convicted of any offence as specified in section 11(d) of this Act;

(b) where the holder has been convicted of an offence under section 20 of the Animals (Diseases and Importation) Act;

(c) where it becomes necessary to reduce or restrict the number of animals on the Government pasturage lands in question for any of the reasons set out in section 11(b); or

(d) where an animal displays vicious tendencies, or becomes otherwise unfit to be pastured with other animals.

However, in cases of adverse weather a licence may be suspended only, and such suspension shall only take effect after 7 days notice in writing has been given to the holder of the licence.

### 13. Tenants permitted to keep animals

Despite anything to the contrary contained in this Act it is lawful for a person who is a tenant of any Government lands to keep an animal thereon without holding a licence.

However, such person if required by the Livestock Officer, shall apply for a licence for such animal which shall not thereafter be kept on such lands by any person unless he or she has obtained a licence therefor. Any person who contravenes this paragraph commits an offence and on summary conviction is liable to a fine not exceeding $10 or to imprisonment for one month.

### 14. Licences to keep or graze animals issuable to tenants

A licence issued under section 13 above may give the applicant permission either to keep or to graze such animal. No fee is payable under a licence to keep such animal.

### 15. Care and treatment to be given licensed animals

Subject to the provisions of articles 985 and 986 of the Civil Code, it is lawful for the Livestock Officer to administer or cause to be administered to licensed animals such care and treatment as may be necessary or desirable to maintain or improve their health or breed and to carry out any of the following operations on such animals: blood testing, castration, dehorning, insemination, branding, marking, dipping and spraying.

### 16. Licensed Animals to be marked

The Livestock Officer shall place or cause to be placed a clearly defined brand or mark upon each licensed animal and shall make a record of such brand or mark together with the name of the owner of such animal in a register which he or she shall keep for the purpose.

### 17. Power to award compensation

The Governor General may in his or her discretion award compensation payable out of the general revenue of Saint Lucia for injury to or the loss of any licensed animal where such animal is injured or dies as a result of anything done under section 8 or section 15.

### 18. Stray animals to be impounded

Any animals which may be found on any Government pasturage lands save as permitted under the provisions of this Act and any regulations made thereunder may be impounded and dealt with in accordance with the Animals Act.

### 19. Dues recoverable by impounding

All dues for pasturage and other sums of money imposed or which have become payable by virtue of this Act or any regulations made hereunder may be recovered by seizure and impounding of the animals of the person so liable, and the provisions of the Animals Act as to seizure and impounding apply.

### 20. Removal of stock before payment of dues

All animals removed from Government pasturage lands before the dues thereon are paid wherever found, is liable to be seized, impounded and sold, and the provisions of the Animals Act as to seizure, impounding and sale shall apply.

### 21. Licensed animals not to be removed without notice

A person shall not remove from the Government pasturage lands any licensed animal without giving the Livestock Officer notice of such removal. Any person who contravenes the provisions of this section commits an offence and on summary conviction is liable to a fine not exceeding $25 or to imprisonment for 2 months.

### 22. Licence to determine where animal is removed without notice

Where the holder of a licence for an animal removes such animal from Government pasturage lands and fails to notify the Livestock Officer thereof, the licence issued in respect of such animal shall cease and determine as from the date of such removal.

### 23. Notices of sales, births and deaths to be given

The holder of a licence for any animal admitted to Government pasturage lands shall report to the Livestock Officer any sale or other change of ownership of such animal and any birth, death or illness of any animal of which he or she has knowledge. Whoever fails to comply with the provisions of this section commits an offence and on summary conviction is liable to a fine not exceeding $10 or to imprisonment for one month.

### 24. Regulations

The Governor General may make regulations—

(a) prescribing the fees to be paid under this Act and altering the fees in Schedule 2;

(b) altering any of the forms in Schedule 1, or adding thereto;

(c) prescribing days, times and places for carrying out any of the tests or operations permitted under this Act;

(d) providing for the use of additional tests;

(e) regulating or restricting the use of Government pasturage lands;

(f) providing generally for the effective carrying out of the purposes of this Act.
