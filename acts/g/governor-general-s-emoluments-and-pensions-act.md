### AN ACT to provide for the salary, allowances and pension benefits of the Governor General and for incidental matters.

Commencement [1 March 1967]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Governor General's Emoluments and Pension Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Acting Governor General**” means any person who performs the functions of the office of Governor General under section 20 of the Saint Lucia Constitution;

“**house allowance**” means the sum of $24,000 being the annual rental value or any part thereof of quarters occupied by the Governor General;

“**salary**” when used in relation to gratuity or pension granted under section 6 or 7 includes house allowance;

“**service in the permanent civil service**” means service in respect of which a pension may be granted under the Pensions Act.

*(Amended by Act 13 of 1990)*

### 3. Emoluments of Governor General

(1) The emoluments appertaining to the office of Governor General are the emoluments specified in the Schedule.

(2) The emoluments shall be paid in equal monthly instalments in arrears.

### 4. Emoluments during absence, leave and incapacity

(1) The Governor General shall—

(a) in respect of any period during which he or she is absent from Saint Lucia for the purposes of performing any of the functions of his or her office, receive the full emoluments appertaining to his or her office;

(b) in respect of any period during which he or she is absent on leave or is unable to perform the functions of his or her office, receive the salary appertaining to his or her office but shall not receive the allowances specified in the said Schedule.

(2) The Governor General shall not, for the purposes of this section, be regarded as unable to perform the functions of his or her office—

(a) by reason only that he or she is in passage from one part of Saint Lucia to another; or

(b) where he or she has appointed a deputy to perform any of the functions of the office of Governor General and such appointment is subsisting.

### 5. Emoluments of Acting Governor General

(1) Subject to the provisions of subsection (2), an Acting Governor General shall, in respect of the period during which he or she discharges the functions of the office of Governor General, receive emoluments at the rate specified in respect of the office of Governor General.

(2) Where an Acting Governor General, in reference to any period during which he or she discharges the functions of the office of Governor General, receives salary defrayed from the Consolidated Fund in respect of any other office, his or her emoluments under subsection (1) in respect of such period shall be abated to the extent of the salary so received.

### 6. Gratuity payable to Governor General

There shall be granted to any person who has held the office of Governor General with effect from the date on which he or she ceases to be Governor General, a gratuity equivalent to 25% of the aggregate of the salaries paid to him or her during his or her term of office.

*(Substituted by Act 13 of 1990)*

### 7. Pension and living allowance granted to person who has ceased to hold office of Governor General

(1) Subject to the provisions of section 8, there shall be granted to any person who has held the office of Governor General, an annual pension equivalent to 2/3 of his or her highest annual salary as Governor General, with effect from the date on which he or she ceased to hold the office of Governor General or from 1 January 2005 whichever is later, and regardless of the length of service as Governor General.

(2) There shall be payable to any person who has held the office of the Governor General, a monthly living allowance of $2,000, with effect from the date on which he or she ceased to hold the office of Governor General or from 1 January 2005 whichever is later, and regardless of the length of service as Governor General.

*(Amended by Act 18 of 2005)*

### 8. Reckoning of Governor General's service for pension

Where any person who has held the office of Governor General has immediately preceding his or her appointment to that office been in service in the permanent civil service and is not in receipt of a pension in respect of that service, he or she may opt—

(a) to receive a pension under section 7, if he or she is so entitled; or

(b) to treat his or her service in the office of Governor General as service in the permanent civil service and receive a pension, or a pension and reduced gratuity or any applicable gratuity under the Pensions Act if the length of his or her joint service in the office of Governor General and his or her service in the permanent civil service qualifies him or her for a pension or gratuity under that Act, and he or she shall be granted a pension under section 7 or a pension or reduced pension and gratuity or other gratuity under the Pensions Act as he or she so opts.

### 9. Gratuity where Governor General dies while holding office

Where a person dies while he or she is Governor General, there shall be granted to his or her legal personal representative a gratuity equal to his or her annual salary.

### 10. Other pensions

Any person who is in receipt of a pension in respect of service in the permanent civil service shall be entitled to and receive such pension during such time that—

(a) he or she holds the office of Governor General; or

(b) having held the office of Governor General, he or she is entitled to or receives a pension under this Act.

### 11. Pension and living allowance payable to surviving widow or widower of Governor General

(1) Subject to subsections (3) and (4), there shall be granted to the surviving widow or widower of a person who has held the office of Governor General, with effect from the date of death of that person or from 1 January 2005 whichever is later, an annual pension equivalent to 2/3 of the highest annual pension which would have been granted to that person under section 7(1) if—

(a) the death had not occurred; and

(b) that person had ceased to hold the office of Governor General.

(2) Subject to subsections (3) and (4), there shall be payable to the surviving widow or widower of a person who has held the office of Governor General, with effect from the date of death of that person or from 1 January 2005 whichever is later, a monthly living allowance equal to 2/3 of the monthly living allowance which would have been payable to that person under section 7(2) if—

(a) the death had not occurred; and

(b) that person had ceased to hold the office of Governor General.

(3) Subsections (1) and (2) shall apply regardless of whether the person dies while holding office or after ceasing to hold the office of Governor General.

(4) A widow or a widower shall cease to receive an annual pension under subsection (1) and a monthly living allowance under subsection (2) if the widow or widower becomes the Governor General or the Prime Minister.

*(Substituted by Act 18 of 2005)*

### 12. Emoluments, pension, etc., charged on consolidated fund

The emoluments of the Governor General and all pensions and gratuities payable under this Act shall be and are hereby charged on the Consolidated Fund.
