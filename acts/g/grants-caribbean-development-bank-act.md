### AN ACT to authorise the Minister to sign grant agreements by the Caribbean Development Bank on behalf of Saint Lucia.

Commencement [5 January 1981]

### 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Grants (Caribbean Development Bank) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Bank**” means the Caribbean Development Bank;

“**grant**” means any sum of money donated by the Bank under specific projects, agreed on by the Government of Saint Lucia and the Bank;

“**Minister**” means the Minister responsible for finance.

### 3. Power of Minister to sign Grant Agreement

Any agreement between the Government of Saint Lucia and the Bank in respect of any grant, shall be signed on behalf of Saint Lucia by the Minister or any person authorised in writing by the Minister.
