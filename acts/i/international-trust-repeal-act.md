### AN ACT to repeal the International Trust Act, Cap. 12.19.

Commencement [30 June 2021]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the International Trust (Repeal) Act.

### 2. Repeal

The International Trust Act, Cap. 12.19 is repealed from the 30th day of June, 2021.
