### AN ACT to regulate the importation of bees.

Commencement [12 October 1912]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Importation of Bees Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**Agricultural Authority**” means the Director of Agricultural Services and includes the Deputy Director of Agricultural Service and any person acting on their behalf by their direction;

“**imported**” means brought into Saint Lucia by any channel whatsoever;

“**importer**” includes the owner and consignee and their agents and servants;

“**package**” includes cage, box or anything whatsoever in which queen-bees are imported;

“**Treasurer**” includes every officer of customs and, for the purpose of dealing with the importation through the post office of queen-bees and bee-hives or other articles adapted for use in connection with bee-keeping, includes the Postmaster and any officer of the post office acting on his or her behalf by his or her direction or that of the Governor General.

### 3. Restrictions on the importation of bees and hives in certain cases

(1) It is not lawful to import into Saint Lucia—

(a) any queen-bee which is not accompanied by a certificate, approved by the Director of Agricultural Services, that the disease known as Foul Brood does not exist in the apiary from which the queen-bee was taken; or

(b) any bee-hive or part of a bee-hive or any article whatsoever which has been used in connection with bees, or any bees other than queen-bees as aforesaid.

(2) The importer of any queen-bee accompanied by a certificate approved by the Director of Agricultural Services shall immediately after the arrival of such queen-bee in Saint Lucia, and to the satisfaction of the Agricultural Authority, destroy by fire the package in which the queen-bee was imported and also kill and destroy by fire any worker-bee which accompanied the queen-bee. The Agricultural Authority may appoint a place where, and a person in whose presence, such destruction shall be carried out.

(3) Every bee-hive or part of a bee-hive or other article adapted for use in connection with bee-keeping shall be landed at the Port of Castries, and the Treasurer shall, prior to the delivery thereof to the importer, cause it to be conveyed to the Agricultural Authority, who shall thereupon inspect it and if he or she is satisfied that it has not been previously used in connection with bee-keeping, he or she shall cause it to be returned to the Treasurer for delivery in due course to the importer thereof; but if he or she is satisfied that it has been previously used in connection with bee-keeping he or she shall cause it to be destroyed or, at his or her discretion, shall detain it for exportation by the importer in such manner as he or she may direct.

### 4. Expenses of conveyance of articles to and from Agricultural Authority

The expenses of conveying bee-hives and other articles to and from the Agricultural Authority and the Treasurer shall be borne by the importer, and shall be recoverable in the First District Court at the suit of the Treasurer or any officer of customs authorised by him or her.

### 5. Seizure of bees, &c., unlawfully imported

Every queen-bee, package, bee-hive or other article adapted for use in connection with bee-keeping which is imported into Saint Lucia contrary to the provisions of this Act or of any regulations under this Act may be seized and forfeited and may be destroyed or otherwise dealt with as the Director of Agricultural Services may direct.

### 6. Offences and penalties

Every person who—

(a) does or omits to do anything which is by this Act, or by any regulation hereunder forbidden or required to be done; or

(b) assaults, resists, hinders, obstructs or threatens any person acting under the authority of this Act or of any regulation under it,

commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction before the First District Court to a penalty not exceeding $96.

### 7. Regulations

The Governor General may make regulations for the better carrying out of the provisions of this Act.
