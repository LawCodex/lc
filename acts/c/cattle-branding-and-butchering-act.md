### AN ACT to prevent the stealing of cattle.

Commencement [12 September 1902]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Cattle (Branding and Butchering) Act.

### 2. Interpretation of terms

In this Act—

“**cattle**” includes any horse, mare, gelding, colt or filly; any mule or ass; any bull, cow, ox, heifer, steer or calf; any ram, ewe, sheep or lamb; any goat or kid, and any swine;

“**business of a butcher**” means any business having for its object the slaughter of cattle and sale of meat, whether carried on continuously or not or whether confined to the slaughter and sale of one animal or not, but shall not be intended to mean the slaughter of cattle and the disposal of the meat thereof on any estate or place where such animal has been accidentally killed or where, owing to any accident to any animal, such animal has been slaughtered to avoid the loss of its value;

“**designated officer**” means a livestock officer or such other person appointed by the Director of Agricultural Services.

*(Amended by Act 15 of 1977)*

#### Branding of Cattle

### 3. Notice of intention to brand

A person shall not brand with any brand or shall not mark with any mark likely to be permanent, any cattle, until the expiration of 14 days after he or she has given at the chief police station of the district wherein he or she resides, or wherein his or her farm is situate or his or her cattle are kept, a notice under his or her hand in the Form in Form A of the Schedule, specifying the brand or mark which such person intends to use for the purpose of marking the cattle belonging to him or her, or in his or her custody, or under his or her control, and further specifying on what part of the body of the animal he or she intends to place such brand or mark; which notice shall state the name in full and the place of residence of the person giving the notice and the name of the owner or owners of the cattle in respect of which the brands or marks are proposed to be used.

### 4. Mark of person who cannot write

If any person, desiring to give notice as hereinbefore mentioned, is unable to write, such person shall affix his or her mark to any such notice in the presence of the Commissioner of Police or any justice of the peace or notary.

### 5. Receipt for notice

A police officer who receives any such notice shall give a receipt for the same, in Form B of the Schedule, to the person giving such notice and shall at once forward such notice to the Commissioner of Police, who shall cause a Register of Brands and Marks for cattle to be kept for the whole State. Such Register shall be termed the Register of Brands and shall be in Form C of the Schedule.

### 6. Refusal of Commissioner of Police to allow use of brand

If, on receiving any such notice the Commissioner of Police shall consider that the use of the brand or mark specified in such notice would be inconvenient or cruel or likely to give rise to error or mistake, he or she shall cause to be served at the place of residence of the person mentioned in such notice an intimation under his or her hand that such brand or mark is not to be used; and thereupon the person giving such notice shall be bound to select for his or her use some other brand or mark, and to give notice thereof in the manner hereinbefore prescribed.

### 7. Offences with regard to branding

A person commits an offence punishable on summary conviction and is liable to a penalty not exceeding $50—

(a) who brands with any brand, or place any mark likely to be permanent on any cattle, before the expiration of 14 days from the date of giving the notice under section 3;

(b) who brands or marks in any manner likely to be permanent any cattle, with any brand or mark other than the brand or mark specified in the notice given by him or her;

(c) who brands or marks in any manner likely to be permanent any cattle, on any part of the animal other than the part specified in the notice given by him or her; or

(d) who uses any brand or mark after an intimation from the Commissioner of Police has been duly served upon him or her that such brand or mark is not to be used.

*(Amended by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 8. Branding with intent to defraud

A person who unlawfully, and with intent to defraud, brands with any brand or place any mark likely to be permanent on any cattle, commits a misdemeanour and on conviction thereof, is liable to be imprisoned with hard labour for any term not exceeding 2 years.

#### Butchers

### 9. Butcher to be registered

A person shall not carry on the business of a butcher in any place in Saint Lucia unless he or she is registered in the manner hereinafter provided.

### 10. Notice of intention to carry on business of a butcher

A person who desires to carry on the business of a butcher shall give notice of his or her intention to do so at the chief police station in the district in which the person is domiciled and such notice shall be according to Form D of the Schedule.

### 11. Commissioner of Police to approve of Registration

(1) On receipt of such notice the constable in charge of the police station shall communicate with the Commissioner of Police who, if he or she approves of the registration of the applicant, shall give such applicant an order in Form E of the Schedule, to pay the registration fee of $5 into the Treasury, if the applicant resides in the First District, or to the sub-collector of the district if the applicant resides in the Second or Third District. *(Amended by Act 15 of 1977)*

(2) The Commissioner of Police shall refuse to register any person as a butcher who has been convicted of stealing cattle or of knowingly receiving stolen cattle or who fails to give to the Commissioner of Police satisfactory proof of his or her respectability.

(3) On the refusal of the Commissioner of Police to give an order to, or to register, any person under this section, such person may appeal to the Governor General who may require the Commissioner of Police to give such order or to register such person.

### 12. Accountant General or Sub-Collector to issue licence

(1) On receipt of the order and of the fee the Accountant General or sub-collector shall issue to the applicant a licence in Form F of the Schedule, and shall inform the Commissioner of Police that such licence has been issued.

(2) All licences shall expire on 31 March in each year.

*(Amended by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 13. Commissioner of Police to keep and publish register

The Commissioner of Police shall cause a register to be kept of all butchers licensed under this Act and shall publish monthly in the Gazette a list of such butchers specifying the street or road, the town or village or lands and the district where the business of every butcher is to be carried on.

### 14. Register of cattle slaughtered

(1) A licensed butcher shall provide and keep a register of all cattle slaughtered by him or her in the course of his or her business, and shall enter or cause to be entered therein the particulars set out in Form G of the Schedule.

(2) Such register shall at all reasonable times be open to inspection by any police officer, any officer of the Agricultural Department or the Labour Commissioner, who may take a copy of any entry therein.

(3) A holder of a butcher's licence shall produce his or her register to the police officer in charge of the police station in the district wherein such licence holder is resident, not later than 15 January in each year and such police officer shall take a copy of such entries therein made during the previous year as he or she considers desirable.

### 15. Penalty for carrying on business of butcher unlawfully

A person who carries on the business of a butcher without conforming to the provisions of sections 9 to 14 commits an offence punishable on summary conviction, and is liable to a penalty not exceeding $50. *(Amended by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 16. Movement of cattle restricted

(1) A person shall not transfer otherwise move from one place to another (except for the purpose of tethering) any bull, cow, ox, steer, heifer, calf, ram, ewe, sheep, lamp, goat or kid or any swine between the hours of 6:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. without the permission in writing of a police officer, rural constable or designated officer.

(2) A person who contravenes the provisions of this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $ 250.

*(Inserted by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 17. Certificate of transfer

(1) A person who sells, gives or otherwise transfers the ownership of, any cattle, shall on the sale, giving or transferring of the ownership, execute or cause to be executed a certificate in triplicate in the Form H of the Schedule and shall give or cause to be given to the purchaser, taker or acceptor of such cattle a copy of such certificate and retain a copy for production to any police officer, rural constable or designated officer who requests to see the same.

(2) Every justice of the peace, police officer, rural constable or designated officer who is a witness to any sale, gift or transfer shall make a record of the particulars of the certificate in a book to be kept by him or her.

(3) Any person who—

(a) fails or neglects to execute or causes to be executed;

(b) fails or neglects to obtain; or

(c) fails, neglects or refuses to show to a police officer, rural constable or designated officer when requested,

the certificate under section (1) is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $250.

*(Inserted by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 18. Provisions with regard to slaughter

(1) A person shall not slaughter or cause to be slaughtered in any place any bull, cow, ox, steer, heifer, calf, ram, ewe, sheep, lamb, goat or kid or any swine without first having obtained from a justice of the peace, a police officer, a rural constable or designated officer a permit in Form I of the Schedule.

(2) A person who slaughters or causes to be slaughtered in any place any bull, cow, ox, steer, heifer, calf, ram, ewe, sheep, lamb, goat or kid or any swine is bound to keep the skin of such animal for 6 days after such animal is slaughtered.

(3) A person slaughtering or causing to be slaughtered in any place any such animal mentioned in this section shall be bound within the said 6 days to produce the skin of such animal to any police officer, rural constable or designated officer who shall request to see the same.

(4) A person who slaughters or causes to be slaughtered in any place, any bull, cow, ox, steer, heifer, calf, ram, ewe, sheep, lamb, goat or kid or any swine—

(a) without having obtained such permit;

(b) fails or neglects to produce such permit relating to such animal to any police officer, rural constable or designated officer who may request to see the same;

(c) fails or neglects to keep, or cause to be kept the skin of such animal; or

(d) fails or neglects to produce the skin relating to any such animal to any police officer, rural constable, or designated officer, who may request to see the same,

commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $250. *(Substituted by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 19. Prohibitions on slaughter

A person whether a licensed butcher or not who slaughters or causes to be slaughtered in any place—

(a) any cow or heifer without the permission of designated officer; or

(b) any animal which is known to be or appears to be in calf, in lamb, in kid, or in pig,

is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $250. *(Substituted by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 20. Onus of proof on defendant

In any proceedings under the preceding section—

(a) the defendant shall be acquitted if he or she proves to the satisfaction of the court that the animal was slaughtered in obedience of lawful authority, with the permission of a designated officer, or from a humanitarian motive such as the prevention of suffering or any other cause;

(b) the onus of proving that the animal was not known, or did not appear to be in calf, in lamb, in kid, or in pig, as the case may be shall be on the defendant.

*(Substituted by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 21. Entering premises where animal is slaughtered

A police officer, a rural constable or designated officer may, at any time enter any premises on which he or she has reasonable cause to believe that any cattle is kept, slaughtered or about to be slaughtered, contrary to the provisions of this Act, and may examine any such cattle, or the carcass and skins of such cattle found therein. *(Substituted by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 22. Detention of person taking animal along highway

A police officer, rural constable or designated officer, may stop and detain any person driving or otherwise conveying any bull, cow, ox, steer, heifer, or calf or any ram, ewe, sheep or lamb, or any goat or kid, or any swine along any public highway or road, and may request such person to give him or her full information as to the place from which and to which such animal is being taken, and as to the name and residence of the owner thereof, and may arrest every such person who refuses or neglects to give full or satisfactory information as aforesaid, and may detain such person in custody until he or she can be brought before a magistrate, and may detain every animal found in the possession of such person. *(Substituted by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 23. Extract of register as proof in Courts

An extract from the Registers kept under the provisions of this Act, if the same shall purport to be certified and signed by the Commissioner of Police or other party whose duty it is to make a record in the Register and a copy of a certificate given under the provisions of this Act and purported to be signed by a person who was a witness thereto, shall without proof of the signature appearing in the extract or in the copy of the certificate, be received in any court as *prima facie* evidence of the contents thereof. *(Substituted by Act 15 of 1977)*

### 24. Recovery of penalties

All penalties under this Act may be recovered by the Commissioner of Police, any police officer, rural constable or designated officer and the procedure for the recovery thereof shall be according to the laws for regulating procedure before the Courts. (Substituted by Act 15 of 1977)
