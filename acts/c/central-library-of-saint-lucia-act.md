### AN ACT to make provision for the establishment of a central library of Saint Lucia.

Commencement [7 April 1951]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Central Library of Saint Lucia Act.

### 2. Establishment of Central Library of Saint Lucia

The Governor General, after consulting the Board established under this Act, may establish and maintain, out of such funds as may be available to him or her for that purpose, such library facilities to be called “The Central Library of Saint Lucia” as in his or her opinion are desirable in order to further the provision of an efficient public library service throughout Saint Lucia and may enter into mutual arrangements with other official and unofficial libraries within Saint Lucia.

### 3. Appointment of librarian officers and servants

The Governor General may appoint a librarian and such other officers and servants as he or she may consider fit, for the purpose of carrying out this Act at such salaries as he or she may, with the consent of the House of Assembly, direct.

### 4. Establishment of Central Library Board

(1) For the purpose of this Act there is hereby established a Board to be styled the Central Library Board hereinafter called “the Board” and composed of 5 members appointed by the Governor General of whom one member shall be nominated by the Castries Corporation and of such other members as may be co-opted by the Board under subsection (2).

(2) The Board may, with the approval of the Governor General, co-opt as members for a stated period not exceeding 12 months, persons with expert knowledge or experience required by the Board in the discharge of its duties. The total number of members thus co-opted shall at no period exceed 3.

(3) The period of service of members of the Board appointed by the Governor General shall be 2 years, but any member may be reappointed by the Governor General.

(4) Any member of the Board may resign his or her seat by letter addressed to the Governor General, and the Governor General may at any time revoke any nomination which he or she has made.

(5) Any member of the Board who has not, on 31 December in each year, attended at least 1/3 of the meetings of the Board during the preceding 12 months or during his or her tenure of office in such months, shall be considered to have vacated his or her seat on the Board, unless he or she has been absent owing to ill-health or with the leave of the Governor General.

(6) In case of the absence on leave or owing to ill-health of any member of the Board, the Governor General may appoint any person similarly qualified to act in his or her stead.

### 5. Chairperson of Board

The chairperson of the Board shall be nominated by the Governor General. If he or she is absent from any meeting, the Board may elect one of its members as chairperson of that meeting.

### 6. Meeting of Board

(1) The Board shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient and at least once every 3 months for transacting its business.

(2) The chairperson may at any time summon a meeting of the Board and on a requisition by any 3 members he or she shall summon a meeting. Such requisition shall state the object for which the meeting is required to be summoned.

(3) Decisions of the Board may be taken at meetings or, in cases in which the chairperson shall so direct, by the recording of the opinions of members on papers circulated among them. The chairperson may direct that papers shall not be circulated to any member who through interest, illness, absence from Saint Lucia or otherwise is, in the opinion of the chairperson, incapacitated from voting on such papers.

(4) The quorum of the Board at any meeting or for the purpose of voting on papers circulated shall be 3.

(5) The decisions of the Board shall be by the majority of votes. In any case in which the voting is equal, the chairperson shall have a second or casting vote.

(6) The librarian, or in his or her absence any other suitable person appointed under section 3, shall be secretary to the Board and shall attend each meeting of the Board and may speak but shall have no vote.

### 7. Powers of Board

The Board shall have the exclusive charge and administration of the rooms and of all the property and affairs of the Central Library of Saint Lucia.

### 8. Functions of Board

The functions of the Board shall be—

(a) to advise the Governor General in regard to public library policy and principles and to submit its views on any question concerning public libraries which the Governor General may refer to it or on any aspect of public library policy or administration which in its opinion requires attention;

(b) to undertake such executive duties as the Governor General may assign, either indefinitely or for a stated period.

### 9. Regulations

(1) The Board may make regulations for the use by any person or body of persons of the Library facilities provided under this Act, for prescribing the duties and powers of the librarian and other officers and servants appointed under this Act, for prescribing penalties or fines for breaches of such regulations and generally for carrying out other purposes of this Act.

(2) A person who commits an offence against the regulations made under subsection (1) is liable on summary conviction for every such offence to such fine or penalty as may be prescribed by the said regulations not exceeding $25.

(3) Regulations made under the preceding subsections of this section shall be submitted to the Governor General for approval and when approved shall come into effect on publication in the Gazette.

### 10. Financial

The expenses of carrying out this Act shall be defrayed out of—

(a) such monies as may be appropriated for the purpose by the House of Assembly out of the general revenue of Saint Lucia;

(b) penalties and fines imposed and recovered under the provisions of any regulation made under this Act;

(c) gifts, grants, subscriptions and bequests derived from any public or private source.

### 11. Recovery of penalties

(1) All penalties and fines imposed by regulations made under the provisions of this Act shall be recoverable in the manner provided by the Criminal Code with regard to summary offences and proceedings may be instituted and conducted by the librarian or by any person authorised by the Board either generally or for any particular proceedings.

(2) All penalties and fines received by a magistrate under this Act shall be paid by him or her to the librarian.
