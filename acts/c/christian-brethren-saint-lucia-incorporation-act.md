### AN ACT to provide for the incorporation of certain persons as trustees of the Congregation of Christian Brethren.

Commencement [21 March 1987]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Christian Brethren (Saint Lucia) Incorporation Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Congregation**” means the Assembly of persons following the Bible as their guide and constituting jointly the assembly fellowship of the Corporation;

“**Corporation**” means the Christian Brethren (Saint Lucia) Incorporated established by section 3;

“**Minister**” means the elder recognized by the Congregation.

### 3. Constitution of the Christian Brethren (Saint Lucia) Incorporated

With the commencement of this Act, there shall be constituted a body corporate by the name Christian Brethren (Saint Lucia) Incorporated which by that name shall have perpetual succession and may sue and be sued in all Courts of Justice in the State and shall have and use a common seal provided that such seal shall only be used in the presence of at least 2 trustees.

### 4. Trustees of the Corporation

The Treasurer and the Correspondent Secretary of the Corporation and the Minister on behalf of the Congregation and their successors in office shall be the trustees of the Corporation.

### 5. Defect in appointment

No act or proceeding of the Corporation shall be invalidated by reason of any defect in the appointment of any person to any of the offices mentioned in section 4 of this Act or by reason of any defect in the notification or in the registration required by subsection (1) of section 6.

### 6. Notification of names and addresses of officers

(1) The names and addresses of the persons holding the several offices referred to in section 4 shall be notified from time to time to the Registrar of Companies and registered in his or her office and be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

(2) A person last registered in the manner prescribed by subsection (1) shall be deemed to be a trustee authorised to act under the provisions of section 8.

### 7. Corporation seal

The seal of the Corporation shall be of such design and pattern as the Corporation may determine.

### 8. Execution of documents

No deed or document purporting to be executed by the Corporation shall be of any force or validity unless it is properly sealed with the Corporate Seal and signed by not less than 2 trustees of the Corporation.

### 9. Powers of the Corporation

The Corporation shall have the following powers

to acquire, hold, purchase, lease, possess and enjoy any lands and hereditaments whatsoever absolutely or for leasehold or for any other estates or interest therein and all other property real or personal;

to give, grant, let, charge, improve, manage, develop, exchange, lease, mortgage, sell, convey, assign, dispose of, turn to account or otherwise deal with all or any of the property, both present and future, so held or vested or any part thereof;

to borrow, raise, or secure the payment of money in such manner as may be thought fit and in particular by the issue of debentures or script charged upon all or any of the property, both present and future, held by or vested in the Corporation; and to redeem and pay off any such securities;

to appoint a person or persons as the attorneys of the Corporation either generally or for a limited period and for such purposes and with such powers as may be stated in the power of attorney, and to revoke any such appointment.

### 10. Saving of crown and other rights

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs of Successors or of any bodies politic or corporate or of any other persons and those claiming by, through, from or under them, except such as are mentioned or referred to in this Act.
