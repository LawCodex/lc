### AN ACT relating to the Castries Cemetery.

Commencement [14 July 1899]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Castries Cemetery Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Corporation**” means the Castries Corporation;

“**Cemetery**” means the public cemetery for Castries and the land on which the said cemetery is to be established, vested by this Act in the Castries Corporation, and any other land which may hereafter be vested in the said Corporation for the purposes of a public cemetery;

“**pauper**” means any person whose body is required to be buried by an order of the chairperson or of any member of the Poor Law Committee of the First District or any person who has died in any gaol, hospital or charitable institution of Castries.

### 3. Area of Cemetery

The following parcels of land are hereby vested in the Castries Corporation, for the purposes of a public cemetery for Castries, and not otherwise, namely—

1. All that portion of the Queen's Chain along Choc Bay in the Quarter of Castries, bounded North-west by the Sea, North-east by the prolongation of the North-eastern boundary of certain lands lately occupied by one Willie Deligny, bearing 153 degrees 26 minutes, South-east by the said lands, and South-west by the prolongation of the South-western boundary of the said lands by the Sea, bearing 311 degrees 21 minutes, the whole containing 2 acres and 31 perches;

2. All that parcel of land, situated in the Quarter of Castries and consisting of four acres 2 roods and 22 perches, more or less, and bounded North by the Queen's Chain along Choc Bay, South and East by lands known as Vide Bouteille, and West by lands belonging to the Heirs of Joseph Langellier; and

3. All that portion of the Queen's Chain along Choc Bay in the Quarter of Castries, bounded North-west by the sea, North-east by the portion of the Queen's Chain described in paragraph one of this section bearing 311 degrees 21 minutes, South-east by lands belonging to the Heirs of Joseph Langellier, and South-west by the road to Choc Bay, the whole containing 3 acres and 2 roods.

### 4. Corporation to pay half of expenses

(1) One-half of the costs of survey in respect of the portions and parcels of land mentioned in section 3 and of the cost of the acquisition of the parcel of land described in section 3(2) and of all incidental expenses shall be paid by the Corporation to the Government in the manner provided in the following section, and the other half shall be contributed by Saint Lucia from general revenue to the cost of the Cemetery.

(2) In consideration of such contribution there shall be reserved to Saint Lucia for the burial of paupers from outside the limits of the City, one quarter of the area of the Cemetery.

### 5. Payment by Castries Corporation of interest and sinking fund

The sums necessary to extinguish in 40 years the amount with interest payable under section 4 shall, and whenever the same may be required, be paid by the Castries Corporation into the Treasury. However, the interest charged shall not exceed the interest which may be payable in respect of any loan raised by Saint Lucia, on the security of the general revenue, wholly or partly, to cover the cost of the Cemetery, and shall not in any case exceed 4% per year.

### 6. Corporation to have management and control of Cemetery

The Corporation has the sole and exclusive management and control of the Cemetery including the portion reserved and shall defray all the expenses of the maintenance.

### 7. Authority to Corporation to appoint officers

(1) It is lawful for the Corporation to appoint such officers and servants, as the Corporation may consider proper, for the purposes of the Cemetery.

(2) Every such officer or servant shall hold office during the pleasure of the Corporation.

(3) Every such officer or servant shall receive such salary or remuneration and shall perform such duties as may be assigned to him or her by the Corporation.

### 8. Authority to Corporation to make by-laws

It is lawful for the Corporation to make by-laws—

(a) for the inclusive use and custody of the Cemetery;

(b) for the keeping of registers, the interment of bodies; and for the government generally of the Cemetery; and

(c) for regulating the fees payable on interments within the cemetery and on the erection of monuments and enclosures thereof provided that as regards the interment of paupers the fees payable by Saint Lucia shall be such sum as the Corporation and the Governor General agree to be the sum payable for the interment of paupers.***Editor's note:*** *By Gazette notice 267/1989 burial fees increased with effect from 15* *January1988*.

### 9. Corporation to allot certain portions of cemetery to different Religious Denominations

It is lawful for the Corporation to allot certain portions of the Cemetery in such manner as to the Corporation may seem fit, for the burial of the members of any particular religious denomination and each such allotment shall as the case may require, be consecrated according to the rites and by the proper ministers of the respective denominations for which each such allotment is so set apart.

### 10. Penalty for violating any rule made under this Act

A person who violates or, being bound thereby, fails to comply with any rule or regulation or order made by virtue of this Act, on being convicted thereof, is liable to a penalty not exceeding $24, and in default of payment to imprisonment for any term not exceeding one month.

### 11. Penalty for hindering any officer or servant of the Corporation

Every person who hinders, molests or interferes with any officer or servant of the Corporation or other person acting under the authority of this Act or of any rule or regulation or order made by virtue of this Act, on conviction, is liable for every such offence to a penalty not exceeding $96 and in default of payment to imprisonment for any term not exceeding 2 months.

### 12. Cemetery to be the only place of burial for persons dying within limits of city

The Cemetery shall, except as hereinafter provided, be the only place of burial for persons who die or whose body shall be brought within the limits of the City of Castries as at present defined or as may hereafter be defined by law or within 2 miles of it. However, it is lawful for the bodies of any such persons to be buried in any cemetery which has not been closed by order of the Governor General.

### 13. Recovery of cost of burial of person buried at public expense

If any person whose body is interred at the public expense is found to have died possessed of any means it is lawful for the Director of Finance and Planning to recover the cost of his or her burial from the estate of such person.

### 14. Penalty for burying body elsewhere than in this cemetery

A person who shall, except as hereinafter provided, bury or assist in burying the body of any person dying within the limits of the City of Castries or within 2 miles of it elsewhere than in the Cemetery established by virtue of this Act, on conviction, is liable to a penalty not exceeding $24, and in default of payment to imprisonment, with or without hard labour, for any period not exceeding 2 months.

However, the Governor General may at any time sanction the re-opening of any vault, tomb or burial place in any existing cemetery on an application in writing for that purpose by any member of the family of or any person interested in the deceased.

In addition, any existing cemetery may continue to be used as a place of burial until closed by order of the Governor General under section 18 of the Cemeteries Act.

### 15. Power to Governor General to dissolve Corporation in certain cases

If the Corporation in the judgment of the Governor General persistently defaults in the performance of the duties imposed upon them by this Act or exceed or abuse their powers hereunder, it is lawful for the Governor General by an order published in the Gazette to dissolve such Corporation; and in case of dissolution the General has all the powers and the same consequences shall follow as are provided by section 68 of the Castries Corporation Act, 1967.

### 16. Recovery of penalties

All penalties imposed by this Act may be sued for and recovered in the name and at the instance of the Town Clerk of Castries by the said Town Clerk or by any police officer and when recovered shall be paid into the Treasury for the use of the Corporation.
