### AN ACT to establish a fund for the benefit of the coconut industry of Saint Lucia and to provide for its administration.

Commencement [1 January 1950]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Coconut Industry Fund Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**coconut produce**” includes coconuts, copra and coconut oil.

### 3. Establishment of fund

(1) For the purpose of this Act there shall be established by the Accountant General in the accounts of Saint Lucia a fund to be called the Coconut Industry Fund (hereinafter referred to as “the fund”).

(2) The fund shall be collected under the following heads—

(a) Head A, from the Coconut Growers Association;

(b) Head B, from other sources.

### 4. Composition of fund

(1) Subject to the provisions of section 7 of the Act every exporter of coconut produce shall pay to the Accountant General an amount which shall be levied at the rate of $3.60 on every ton of copra or its equivalent as fixed by him or her, or other coconut produce exported from Saint Lucia.

(2) The amount to be paid by an exporter of coconut produce under subsection (1) shall be levied and collected within 90 days after the date of exportation or, in the case of coconut produce exported before the date of the publication of this Act, within 90 days after the date of such publication.

(3) In default of payment when due of any amount payable under this section by an exporter of coconut produce, such amount, together with interest at the rate of 6% per year from the date the payment became due under subsection (2) shall be recoverable by the Accountant General from the exporter as a debt due to the Crown.

### 5. Investment of the fund and exemption from income tax

(1) The Accountant General may, with the approval of the Governor General invest or place on deposit account any of the monies lying to the credit of the fund, and any interest earned on monies so invested or deposited shall be placed to the credit of the appropriate head of the fund.

(2) Sums deposited to the credit of the fund including all interest earned on monies invested or deposited shall be exempt from the payment of income tax.

### 6. Disbursement from fund

Any disbursements made by the Accountant General from Head A of the fund shall be made with the authorisation of the Saint Lucia Coconut Growers Association and from Head B of the fund with the authorisation of the Committee hereinafter referred to in section 8.

### 7. Power of Governor General to vary rates and to order a cessation of payments under section 4

(1) The Governor General acting on the advice of the Committee hereinafter referred to in section 8 and the Saint Lucia Coconut Growers Association may, by order published in the Gazette—

(a) at any time and vary the rate specified in section 4(1);

(b) prescribe that the amount specified in section 4 shall cease to be levied, collected and paid in respect of coconut produce exported on or after a date specified in the order.

(2) Where an order is made under subsection (1)(a), the rate as varied shall have effect as if the varied rate was specified in section 4(1).

(3) Where an order is made under subsection (1)(b), the power of the Accountant General under section 4(3) to recover any amount payable under that section but not paid before the date specified in the order shall continue to have effect in the same manner and to the same extent as if the order had not been made.

### 8. Management of fund

(1) Subject to the powers of the Accountant General under section 5 the fund to be collected under Head A shall be controlled and administered by the Saint Lucia Coconut Growers Association and the fund to be collected under Head B by a Committee in accordance with this Act and any regulations made thereunder.

(2) The Committee shall be appointed by the Governor General and the members of the Committee shall be 3 persons not being public officers, the Director of Agricultural Services and the Accountant General.

### 9. Regulations

The Saint Lucia Coconut Growers Association shall in respect of Head A of the fund and the Committee in respect of Head B of the fund shall with the approval of the Governor General make regulations to provide for the following—

(a) the basis and the conditions on which disbursements from the fund shall be allowed and authorised, and the procedure which shall govern the making of such disbursements;

(b) the keeping of proper records and the keeping and rendering of detailed accounts in relation to the fund;

(c) generally for carrying into effect the purposes of this Act and prescribing the powers and duties of the Committee or the Saint Lucia Coconut Growers Association for the control and management of the fund.
