### AN ACT to provide for the incorporation of certain persons as Trustees of the Congregation of the Sisters of St. Joseph De Cluny.

Commencement [26 February 1955]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Congregation of the Sisters of St. Joseph De Cluny (Trustees) Act.

### 2. Appointment and incorporation of Trustees

(1) The Mother Provincial of the Sisters of St. Joseph De Cluny in the British West Indies, of Trinidad, British West Indies, the Mother Superior of St. Joseph's Convent of Castries in Saint Lucia and the Sister Bursar of St. Joseph's Convent of Castries in Saint Lucia and their successors in office are hereby appointed Trustees of the Congregation in the Sisters of St. Joseph De Cluny in Saint Lucia and are hereby created a body corporate by the name of “the St. Joseph de Cluny” (hereinafter called the Trustees) and by that name shall have perpetual succession and shall and may sue and be sued in all Courts of Justice in Saint Lucia and shall have and use a Common Seal provided that such Seal shall only be used in the presence of at least 2 of the Trustees

(2) The names and addresses of the persons holding the several offices referred to in the preceding subsection shall be notified to the Registrar of Companies and registered in his office and be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

### 3. Vesting of property in the Trustees

The property specified in the Schedule to this Act and all other property movable and immovable of whatsoever nature now vested in any person or persons for an on behalf of the Congregation of the Sisters of St. Joseph de Cluny shall be and the same hereby vested in the Trustees.

### 4. Power of Trustees to acquire property

The Trustees shall have full power to acquire for the Congregation of the Sisters of St. Joseph de Cluny by purchase, exchange, demise, gift, bequest or otherwise movable or immovable property in Saint Lucia or any estates or interest therein and also any money, securities for money, goods or chattels whatsoever.

### 5. Power of Trustees to borrow money upon security

The Trustees shall have the power to borrow money upon the security of any property held by them and for that purpose any document relating thereto shall be signed by not less than 2 trustees, provided that any mortgage or hypothecary obligation shall be made under the Common Seal of the Trustees as provided thereunder.

### 6. Power of Trustees to dispose of property vested in them

The Trustees shall have the power from time to time by deed under their Common Seal to sell, demise, grant, convey, exchange, mortgage, hypothecate or otherwise dispose of any property vested or which may hereafter be vested in them.

### 7. Saving rights of the crown and bodies corporate

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty or of any bodies politic or corporate or of any other persons, except such as are mentioned or referred to in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
