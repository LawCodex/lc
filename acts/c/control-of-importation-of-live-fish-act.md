### AN ACT to control the importation into Saint Lucia of live fish.

Commencement [31 May 1952]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Control of Importation of Live Fish Act.

### 2. Restriction on importation of live fish

(1) A person shall not, without the consent of the Governor General, import into Saint Lucia any live fish other than live fish indigenous to Saint Lucia.

(2) Any person who contravenes the provisions of this section is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $100.

### 3. Power of Customs Officers

An officer of customs who finds any live fish other than live fish indigenous to Saint Lucia among any goods or baggage entering Saint Lucia shall, if the consent of the Governor General for the importation of the same has not been obtained, seize such live fish and destroy or cause the same to be destroyed.
