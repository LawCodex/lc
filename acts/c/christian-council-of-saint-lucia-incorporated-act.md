### AN ACT to provide for the incorporation of the Christian Council of Saint Lucia.

Commencement [24 December 1983]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Christian Council of Saint Lucia (Incorporated) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act “**Council**” means the Christian Council of Saint Lucia established by section 3 of this Act.

### 3. Incorporation of the Christian Council of Saint Lucia

With the commencement of this Act, there shall be constituted a body corporate by the name of Christian Council of Saint Lucia (Incorporated) which by that name shall have perpetual succession and may sue and be sued in all Courts of Justice in Saint Lucia and shall have and use a common seal which may from time to time be changed in accordance with the rules of the said body corporate.

### 4. Aims and objects of the Christian Council

The aims and objects of the Council are -

(a) to promote greater understanding and unity among all Christian communities in Saint Lucia;

(b) to bear witness to a common profession of faith in God and in Jesus Christ and to truth, charity, equity and justice in Saint Lucia;

(c) to preserve and protect the rights, powers, privileges and interests of member denominations of the Christian Council, within the framework of the laws of Saint Lucia;

(d) to arrange for the holding of ecumenical services, rallies or other ecumenical meetings and to organize theological, historical or other studies in order to encourage and to promote effectively a better Christian way of life in the community;

(e) to collaborate and co-operate with all religious organisations in the community with the object of bringing about the spiritual, moral, intellectual, social, cultural, economic and technical progress of the people of Saint Lucia, to the glory of God and the dignity of man;

(f) to publish journals or such other publications as the Christian Council may consider necessary; and

(g) to preserve, if necessary in conjunction with any religious or secular bodies or organisations, proper standards of Christian conduct and behaviour in Saint Lucia.

### 5. Officers of the Council

The Council shall consist of a Chairman, Vice Chairman, a Treasurer and Executive Secretary and such other officers as may be appointed by the Council from time to time.

### 6. Notification of names and addresses of officers

(1) The names and addressed of the Officers of the Council shall be notified from time to time to the Registrar of Companies and registered in his or her office and be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

(2) A person last registered in the manner prescribed by the preceding subsection shall be deemed to be an officer authorised to act under the provisions of section 9 of this Act.

### 7. Immunity for acts or proceedings of the Council in certain respects

No act or proceeding of the Council shall be invalidated by reason of any defect in the appointment of any officer to the said Council or by reason of any defect in the notification or in the registration required by subsection (1) of section 6.

### 8. Powers of the Council

The Council shall have the following powers -

(a) to acquire, hold, purchase, lease, possess and enjoy any lands and hereditaments whatsoever, absolutely or for any other estates or interests therein, and all other property, real or personal;

(b) to give, grant, let, charge, improve, manage, develop, exchange, lease, mortgage, sell, convey, assign, dispose of, turn to account or otherwise deal with all or any of the property both present and future, so held or vested or any part thereof;

(c) to borrow, raise, or secure the payment of money in such manner as may be thought fit and in particular by the issue of debenture or script charged upon all or any of the property, both present and future, held or vested in the Council and to redeem and pay off any such securities;

(d) to appoint a person or persons as the attorney or attorneys of the Council either generally or for a limited period and for such purposes and with such powers as may be stated in the power of attorney and to revoke any such appointment.

### 9. Execution of documents

No deed or document purporting to be executed by the Council shall be of any force or validity unless it is properly sealed with its common seal in the presence of the Chairman, Vice Chairman, the Treasurer and the Executive Secretary of the Council for the time being in officer who shall sign every such deed, document or other instrument to which the common seal is affixed and such signing shall be prima facie evidence that the said seal was duly and properly affixed and the lawful seal of the Corporation.

### 10. Power to make rules

The Council may make rules for the purpose of the conduct of its proceedings and the discharge of its duties, powers and functions with power to vary or revoke such rules from time to time as it may think fit.

### 11. Saving of crown and other rights

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or Successors or of any bodies politic or corporate or of any other persons except as are mentioned or referred to in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
