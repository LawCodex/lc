### AN ACT to provide for the protection of documents relating to examinations held by the Caribbean Examinations Council and for connected matters.

Commencement [17 January 1979]

### 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Caribbean Examination Council Act.

### 2. Interpretation

For the purposes of this Act—

“**Council**” means the body founded by Article II of the Agreement establishing the Caribbean Examinations Council done at Georgetown, Guyana on 10 April 1972;

“**Minister**” means the Minister for education.

### 3. Prohibited use of papers relating to examinations

(1) A person shall not, after obtaining possession of a paper containing questions or instructions to candidates in connection with an examination to be held by the Council—

(a) permit that paper to be seen by;

(b) give or permit that paper to be given to;

(c) disclose the contents of that paper to; or

(d) permit that paper or its contents to be used by,

any unauthorised person, or make any unauthorised use of such paper or its contents.

(2) Where a paper containing questions or instructions to candidates in connection with an examination to be held by the Council comes into the possession of a person, he or she shall immediately return that paper to the office of the Council or the officer in charge of the nearest police station, who shall immediately forward the same to the Minister for education.

(3) Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of the preceding subsections commits an offence.

### 4. Offences

(1) A person who is employed—

(a) in the setting, moderating, revising, vetting, printing, collection, storage, distribution or custody of examination papers;

(b) in the making of draft examination papers or of papers containing instructions to candidates;

(c) as a supervisor, an invigilator, an attendant or otherwise,

in connection with an examination held by the Council shall not, prior to or during that examination, knowingly or recklessly disclose to any other person the contents of such examination paper, draft examination paper or paper containing instructions.

(2) A person shall not—

(a) after he or she has acquired prior knowledge of the questions set in connection with an examination held by the Council, present himself or herself as a candidate at that examination;

(b) for the purposes of an examination held by the Council, falsely represent himself or herself to be another person; or

(c) forge or alter, or offer, utter, dispose of or put off, knowing the same to be forged or altered any certificate purporting to show that a person has passed an examination held by the Council.

(3) Any person who contravenes any of the provisions of the preceding subsections commits an offence.

### 5. Summary Trial and punishment

(1) All offences under this Act shall be tried summarily.

(2) A person who is convicted of an offence under this Act is liable to a fine not exceeding $2,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months or to both such fine and imprisonment.

### 6. Restriction on prosecutions

A prosecution for an offence under section 4(1)(b) shall not be instituted except with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions.

### 7. Regulations

The Minister may make regulations for the fulfilment of the purposes of this Act and for connected matters.
