### AN ACT to provide for the regulation of circuses, merry-go-rounds and other shows of a like kind.

Commencement [24 December 1925]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Circus and Similar Shows Act.

### 2. Prohibition of operating circuses, etc., without licence

(1) A person shall not conduct or operate any circus, merry-go-round, or other show of a like kind without a licence granted and issued subject to the conditions hereinafter provided.

(2) Any person who acts in contravention of this section commits an offence.

### 3. Issue of licences

It is lawful for the Commissioner of Police to issue licences under this Act, subject to the conditions next hereinafter mentioned.

### 4. Conditions of licences

(1) Each licence shall specify—

(a) the site on which the show shall be erected;

(b) the days of the week and the hours between which the show shall be operated;

(c) the period during which the show may be operated.

(2) If the holder of a licence or any person employed by him or her infringes any condition subject to which such licence is granted he or she commits an offence against this Act.

### 5. Fee for licence

There shall be paid into the Treasury for every licence issued under this Act a fee of $9.60 for each week or part of a week during which the show is licensed to be operated. However, the Governor General may authorise the grant of a licence for a local show at such smaller fee and on such terms and conditions as he or she may prescribe.

### 6. Penalty

Any person who commits an offence under this Act is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $240.

### 7. Cancellation of licences

The Governor General may, in the event of the infringement by the holder of a licence of any of the conditions subject to which such licence is granted, or of the conviction of such holder or any person employed by him or her of an offence under this Act, without compensation cancel such licence.
