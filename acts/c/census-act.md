### AN ACT to make provision for the taking of a census for Saint Lucia.

Commencement [13 March 1946]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Census Act.

### 2. Power to make census

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act it is lawful for the Governor General by order to direct that a census be taken for Saint Lucia, and any order under this section may prescribe—

(a) the date on which the census is to be taken;

(b) the persons by whom and with respect to whom information for the purpose of the census is to be furnished; and

(c) the particulars to be stated in the returns.

However, no particulars shall be required to be stated other than particulars with respect to such matters as are mentioned in the Schedule.  ***Editor's note:*** *The following Order was made under this section authorising the taking of a census in 2001: Census Order, S.I. 18/2001 in force 10 February 2001*.

(2) Any order made under this section may be revoked, amended or varied by a subsequent order.

### 3. Appointment of Census Officer

It is lawful for the Governor General to appoint in writing under his or her hand a fit and proper person as census officer and such appointment shall be published in the Gazette.

### 4. Duties of Census Officer

(1) It is the duty of the census officer to make arrangements and do all such things as are necessary for the taking of a census in accordance with the provisions of this Act and of any order or regulations made thereunder, and for that purpose to make arrangements for the preparation and issue of the necessary forms and instructions and for the return of the forms when filled up.

(2) The census officer in exercise of his or her powers and in the performance of his or her duties under this Act or under any order or regulations made thereunder, shall be subject to the control of, and shall comply with any directions given by the Governor General.

(3) All expenses incurred with the sanction of the Governor General in connection with the taking of a census or otherwise in connection with the exercise of powers or the performance of duties under this Act shall be defrayed out of the public revenue of Saint Lucia.

### 5. Power of Governor General to make Regulations

For the purpose of enabling any order directing a census to be taken to be carried into effect, the Governor General may make regulations—

(a) providing for the division of Saint Lucia into districts for the purpose of the census and the appointment of persons to act in those districts in connection with the census;

(b) requiring all such persons as may be employed for the purpose of the census to perform such duties in connection with the taking of the census as may be prescribed;

(c) requiring persons employed for the purpose of the census to make a written declaration with respect to the performance of their duties and prescribing the form of such declaration;

(d) requiring the superintendent or other person in charge of every gaol, prison, lock-up or other place of confinement and the officer in charge of any hospital or other public or charitable institution within Saint Lucia to make returns with respect to inmates thereof and to conform to such instructions as may be given to him or her;

(e) requiring information to be given to the persons liable to make returns by the persons with respect to whom the returns are to be made;

(f) with respect to the forms to be used in the taking of a census;

(g) for the allowances to be paid to persons employed in connection with the taking of the census;

(h) making provision with respect to any other matters with respect to which it is necessary to make provision for the purpose of carrying into effect the provisions of the order.

### 6. Census Officer to make report

(1) The census officer shall, as soon as may be after the taking of a census, prepare or cause to be prepared a tabulated report on the census returns, showing the number of persons enumerated in Saint Lucia as a whole and in each district provided in accordance with section 5, and every such report shall be printed and laid before the House of Assembly.

(2) A report, summary of statistics or other publication under this Act shall not contain any of the particulars comprised in any individual return so arranged as to enable any person to identify any particulars so published as being particulars relating to any individual person.

### 7. Enumerators' power to enter

It is lawful for every enumerator appointed under the regulations made under this Act in so far as may be necessary for the purpose of this Act to enter every house within his or her district on the day appointed for taking a census and at any time during the period of 4 weeks immediately preceding the said day, and he or she may repeat his or her visits to and entries into and upon any house in his or her district as often as may be necessary for the purpose of obtaining from any inmate of any such house any further information or explanation necessary to enable him or her to complete, verify or correct any account given to or taken by him or her in respect thereof or of the inmates thereof.

### 8. Access to public records

Every person who has the custody or charge of any public records or documents, or of any records or documents of any corporation, from which information relating to the objects of any census ordered under this Act may be obtained, or which would aid in the completion of such census, shall grant to any census supervisor, enumerator, or person authorised in writing by the census officer, access thereto for the purpose of obtaining such information therefrom.

### 9. Offences and penalties

(1) If any person—

(a) refuses or neglects to comply with or acts in contravention of any of the provisions of this Act or any order or regulations made under this Act;

(b) being a person required under this Act to make a written declaration with respect to the performance of his or her duties makes a false declaration;

(c) being a person required by any order or regulations made under this Act to make, sign, or deliver any document, makes, signs, or delivers, or causes to be made, signed, or delivered a false document; or

(d) being a person required under any such order or Regulations to answer any question, refuses to answer or gives a false answer to that question,

he or she is liable for each offence on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $48.

(2) If any person—

(a) being a person employed in taking a census without lawful authority publishes or communicates to any person otherwise than in the ordinary course of such employment any information acquired by him or her in the course of his or her employment;

(b) having possession of any information which to his or her knowledge has been disclosed in contravention of this Act, publishes or communicates that information to any other person; or

(c) in the pretended performance of duties under this Act obtains, or seeks to obtain, information which he or she is not duly authorised to obtain,

he or she commits a misdemeanour, and on conviction is liable to imprisonment with or without hard labour for a term not exceeding one year, or to a fine, or to both such imprisonment and fine.

## Schedule

(Section 2)

### MATTERS IN RESPECT OF WHICH PARTICULARS MAY BE REQUIRED

1. Names, sex, age, religion.

2. Occupation, profession, trade or employment.

3. Nationality, birthplace, race, language.

4. Place of abode and character of dwelling.

5. Condition as to marriage, relation to head of family, issue born.

6. Any other matters with respect to which it is desirable to obtain statistical information with a view to ascertaining the social or civil condition of the population.

7. Crops, acreage held and form of tenure, livestock and other agricultural matters.

8. Equipment and methods employed in fishing.
