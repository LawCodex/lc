An Act to make provision for the granting of assistance to the fishing industry.

Commencement [12 December 1972.]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Fishing Industry (Assistance) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act —

“**commercial fishing craft**” means a boat propelled by mechanical power which is engaged in the fishing industry;

“**Chief Agricultural Officer**” includes any officer of the Ministry of Agriculture authorised by him to act on his behalf;

“**Co-operative Society**” means a society registered; under section 7 of the Co-operative Society Ordinance;

“**engine**” means an engine used by a registered owner for the purpose of propelling a commercial fishing craft;

“**Minister**” means the Minister charged with the subject of fishing;

“**registered owner**” means a person appearing in the register or list of members kept by any Co-operative Society;

### 3. Authority for payments

(1) Payments may be made from time to time to a Co-operative Society in accordance with the regulations in respect of any engine which the Chief Agricultural Officer is satisfied is used for the purpose of propelling a commercial fishing craft owned by a registered owner.

(2) Such a Co-operative Society may make payments to the registered owner of the engine to which the payment relates.

(3) The amount of any payment under subsection (1) shall be determined in accordance with the regulations.

### 4. Scheduled items to be exempt from Customs duty

(1) Notwithstanding anything enacted in the Customs Act 1967, any item mentioned in the Schedule hereinafter referred to as a “**Scheduled item**” sold to any person shall be wholly exempted from the payment of Customs duty.

(2) For the purposes of this Act “**duty**” carries the same meaning as in section 2 of the Customs Act, 1967.

### 5. Payment to be made by resolution of the House

Payments authorised by section 3 of this Act shall be made out of such moneys as may be provided from time to time for the purpose by resolution of the House of Assembly.

### 6. Power to make Regulations

(1) Cabinet may make regulations —

(a) providing for the registration and cancellation of the registration of engines;

(b) providing for the manner in which, the place at which, and the time limit within which. applications for payments under this Act shall be made;

(c) prescribing a formula, or making other provision, for determining the amount of the payment which may be made in respect of any engine;

(d) prescribing the procedure which shall govern the making of a claim and the refunding of customs duty;

(e) declaring the terms and conditions on which payments under this Act may be made;

(f) declaring the time and place at which payment under this Act will be made;

(g) prescribing the procedure which shall govern the making of any payment under this Act;

(h) prescribing any forms which may be necessary for the purposes of this Act;

(i) providing for appeals from any decision of the Chief Agricultural Officer disallowing wholly or in part any application for a payment under this Act, for the procedure in relation thereto, and for the time limit within which any appeal may be brought;

(j) providing generally for the better carrying into effect of the purposes of this Act.

(2) Regulations made under this section may —

(a) require any person to do any act or thing prescribed therein which is in furtherance of the purposes of this Act;

(b) provide for the imposition of a penalty not exceeding $100 in respect of any breach of, or failure to comply with, any provision thereof.

### 7. Offences

(1) Any person who makes any false statement or gives any false information for the purpose of obtaining any payment or Scheduled item under this Act, shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of $250 or to imprisonment for a term of 6 months.

(2) Any registered owner who is convicted of an offence under subsection (1) of this section or any other offence relating to improperly obtaining or attempting to obtain any payment or scheduled item under this Act shall, in addition to any penalty otherwise prescribed —

(a) be liable, on first conviction, to be disqualified for obtaining any scheduled item or payment under this Act for such period not exceeding 2 years as the Court may order;

(b) on any subsequent conviction, be disqualified obsolutely for obtaining any payment or scheduled item under this Act.

### 8. Performance of duties

Any duties required to be performed for the purpose of administering this Act shall be performed by such officer or officers of the Ministry of Agriculture as may be required by the Chief Agricultural Officer to perform the same.

### 9. Records

The Chief Agricultural Officer shall keep or cause to be kept proper records in the form approved by the Accountant General of all charges to expenditure under section 3*.*

### 10. Accounts

(1) The Chief Agricultural Officer shall not later than the 15th day of March and the 15th day of September in every year furnish to the Minister for the information of the Cabinet, accounts audited by the Director of Audit, of all payments made under section 3. Such accounts shall relate respectively to the half years ending on the 30th day of June and the 31st of December.

Provided that the first account to be rendered under this section shall relate to the period ending on the 30th day of June, 1973.

(2) All accounts furnished under subsection (1) of this section shall be submitted to the Director of Audit to audit at least 28 days before the date on which they are required to be furnished to the Minister.

(3) The Minister shall table the accounts as furnished under subsection (1) of this section in the House of Assembly as soon as possible after receipt thereof.

### 11. Right of payment NOT absolute

Nothing in this Act shall confer or be construed conferring on any person any absolute right to any payment thereunder.

## Schedule

The following items which the Fisheries Officer may certify on signed certificate to that effect and which the Comptroller of Customs is satisfied will be used exclusively in the fishing industry —

(a) artificial bait, spinning bait;

(b) mounted lines and casts;

(c) fishing floats;

(d) sinkers;

(e) fish pot wire;

(f) all other fishing tackle;

(g) inboard and outboard engines;

(h) motor parts to outboard and inboard motors.
