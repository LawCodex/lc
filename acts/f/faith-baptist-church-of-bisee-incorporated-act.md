### AN ACT to provide for the incorporation of the Faith Baptist Church of Bisee.

Commencement [8 September 1983]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Faith Baptist Church of Bisee (Incorporated) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

“Corporation” means the Faith Baptist Church of Bisee established by section 3 of this Act.

### 3. Incorporation of the Faith Baptist Church of Bisee

With the commencement of this Act, there shall be constituted a body corporate by the name of Faith Baptist Church of Bisee Incorporated which by that name shall have perpetual succession and may sue and be sued in all Courts of Justice in Saint Lucia and shall have and use a common seal provided that such seal shall only be used in the presence of at least 2 of the Trustees.

### 4. Trustees of the Corporation

The Trustees of the Corporation shall consist of the Pastor and 2 other members to be chosen annually from amongst the members of the Corporation.

### 5. Notification of names and addresses of officers

(1) The names and addresses of the Trustees of the Corporation shall be notified from time to time to the Registrar of Companies and registered in his or her office and shall be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

(2) A person last registered in the manner prescribed by the preceding subsection shall be deemed to be a trustee authorised to act under the provisions of section 7 of this Act.

### 6. Immunity to act or proceedings of the Corporation in certain respects

No act or proceeding of the Corporation shall be invalidated by reason of any defect in the appointment of any trustee to the Corporation or by reason of any defect in the notification or in the registration required by subsection (1) of section 5.

### 7. Execution of documents

No deed or document purporting to be executed by the Corporation shall be of any force or validity unless it is properly sealed and signed by not less than 2 trustees of the Corporation.

### 8. Powers of the Corporation

The Corporation shall have the following powers

(a) to acquire, hold, purchase, lease, possess and enjoy any lands and hereditaments whatsoever, absolutely or for any other estates or interests therein, and all other property, real or personal;

(b) to give, grant, let, charge, improve, manage, assign, dispose of, turn to account or develop, exchange, lease, mortgage, sell, convey or otherwise deal with all or any of the property both present and future, so held or vested or any part thereof;

(c) to borrow, raise, or secure the payment of money in such manner as may be thought fit and in particular by the issue of debentures or script charged upon all or any of the property, both present and future, held by or vested in the Corporation and to redeem and pay off any such securities;

(d) to appoint a person or persons as the attorney or attorneys of the Corporation either generally or for a limited period and for such purposes and with such powers as may be stated in the power of attorney and to revoke any such appointment.

### 9. Constitution of Corporation

The Constitution and By-laws of the Corporation shall be the Constitution and By-laws which governed the Faith Baptist Church prior to the commencement of this Act and the Corporation may from time to time, in accordance with its rules, amend the said Constitution and By-laws.

### 10. Vesting of property in Corporation

With the commencement of this Act, all the property of the Faith Baptist Church, including the property described in the Schedule shall become vested in the Corporation.

### 11. Saving of the crown and other rights

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or Successors or of any bodies politic or corporate or of any other persons except such as are mentioned or referred to in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.

## Schedule
