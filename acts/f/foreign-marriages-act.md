### AN ACT relating to notice of marriage under the provisions of the Foreign Marriage Act, 1892, of the United Kingdom.

Commencement [10 September 1904]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Foreign Marriages Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In the following sections—

“**district registrar**” means a district registrar appointed under the Civil Status Act;

“**status officer**” means a minister of religion who is authorised to keep records of civil status.

### 3. Notice to Marriage Officer of intended marriage

In case any person residing in Saint Lucia intends entering into bonds of matrimony to be solemnized in any foreign country under the provisions and with the benefit of the Foreign Marriage Act, 1892, of the United Kingdom and the orders in council made thereunder, he or she, on payment of the like fee, if any, as is payable on giving a notice of marriage within Saint Lucia shall give a notice in writing of the said intended marriage either (a) to the district registrar in whose district he or she has, for a period of not less than 3 consecutive weeks immediately preceding the giving of such notice, had his or her usual place of abode, or (b) to the minister of religion (being a status officer) of the congregation to which he or she shall have belonged, or is considered to have been attached, during the period aforesaid, or if not belonging or not considered to be attached to any congregation then, to any minister of religion being a status officer of the parish in which he or she has resided for the said period of not less than 3 weeks immediately preceding the giving of such notice. Such notice shall conform to the requirements of article 97 of the Civil Code.

### 4. Publication of notice by district registrar

(1) On the receipt by the district registrar of such notice as aforesaid he or she being satisfied that the same is in conformity with the requirements of this Act shall cause the same to be published in the manner prescribed for similar publications by the Civil Code.

(2) The district registrar having complied with the requirements of this Act shall, as soon as may be after the said publication, unless he or she is aware of any impediment or valid objection to the solemnization of the marriage, grant a certificate that the said notice has been given and published as aforesaid, which certificate shall be in the Form, as nearly as may be, set out in Schedule 1, and shall therein state whether any opposition to such intended marriage has been served upon the district registrar.

### 5. Publication of notice by minister of religion

(1) In the case of such notice being given to a minister of religion (being a status officer) the said status officer being satisfied on the receipt of such notice that the notice conforms to the requirements of this Act shall publish the same by publication of banns in like manner in every respect as if the party giving the notice were about to be married in Saint Lucia.

(2) The status officer having complied with the requirements of this Act shall, after the publication is complete, unless he or she is aware of any impediment or valid objection to the solemnization of the said marriage, grant a certificate of the due publication of the banns, which certificate shall be in the Form set out in Schedule 2 and shall therein state whether any opposition to the intended marriage has been served upon the said status officer.

### 6. When minister of religion not compelled to accept notice

A status officer who is a minister of religion cannot be compelled to accept any notice or to publish the same or to do anything under this Act to enable any marriage to be celebrated to which any impediment exists according to the doctrine of his or her religion or the discipline of the Church or religious community to which he or she belongs.

### 7. Transmission of certificate

The person, who has been granted a certificate by a district registrar, or by a status officer as aforesaid, shall cause the same to be transmitted to the district in the foreign country or place in which the intended marriage is to be solemnized.
