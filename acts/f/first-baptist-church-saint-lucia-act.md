### AN ACT to provide for the incorporation of the First Baptist Church of Saint Lucia.

Commencement [15 July 1983]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the First Baptist Church (Saint Lucia) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, “**The Church**” means the First Baptist Church established by section 3.

### 3. Incorporation of the First Baptist Church

With the commencement of this Act, the Baptist Church situate at Darling Road in the quarter of Castries shall be created a body corporate by the name of the First Baptist Church, Saint Lucia and by that name shall have perpetual succession and may sue and be sued in all Courts of Justice in the State and shall have and use a Common Seal provided that such seal shall only be used in the presence of at least 2 of the Trustees.

### 4. Officers and trustees of the Church

The Church shall consist of a Council of 4 officers, (hereinafter referred to as the Council) appointed from among the voting members of the church as set out in the Schedule who together with their successors in office shall be the Trustees of the Church.

### 5. Notification of names and addresses of officers

(1) The names and addresses of the Officers of the Council shall be notified from time to time to the Registrar of Companies and registered in his or her office and be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

(2) A person last registered in the manner prescribed by the preceding section shall be deemed to be a trustee authorised to act under the provisions of section 8 of this Act.

### 6. Immunity to acts or proceedings of the council in certain respects

No act or proceedings of the Council shall be invalidated by reason of any defect in the appointment of any officer of the said Council or by reason of any defect in the notification or in the registration required by subsection 1 of section 5.

### 7. Seal of the Church

The Seal of the Church shall be of such design and pattern as the church may determine.

### 8. Execution of documents

No deed or document purporting to be executed by the Church shall be of any force or validity unless it is properly sealed and signed by not less than 2 Trustees of the Church.

### 9. Powers of the Church

The Church shall have the following powers

(a) to acquire, hold, purchase, lease, possess and enjoy any lands and hereditaments whatsoever, absolutely or for any other estates or interests therein, and all other property, real or personal;

(b) to give, grant, let, charge, improve, manage, develop, exchange, lease, mortgage, sell, convey, assign, dispose of, turn to account or otherwise deal with all or any of the property both present and future, so held or vested or any part thereof;

(c) to borrow, raise, or secure the payment of money in such manner as may be thought fit and in particular by the issue of debentures or script charged upon all or any of the property, both present and future, held by or vested in the Church and to redeem and pay off such securities;

(d) to appoint a person or persons as the attorney or attorneys of the Church either generally or for a limited period and for such purposes and with such powers as may be stated in the power of attorney and to revoke any such appointment.

### 10. Saving of crown and other rights

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or Successors or of any bodies politic or corporate or of any other persons except such as are mentioned or referred to in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
