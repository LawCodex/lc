### AN ACT to provide for the increase of pensions payable in respect of public service.

Commencement [1 January 1970]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Pensions (Increase) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

(1) In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

**“enactment”** means any Act applicable to the provisions of this Act;

**“pensions authority”** means the Accountant General.

(2) Any reference in this Act to an enactment shall be construed as a reference to that enactment as amended, extended or applied by or under any such enactment other than this Act.

### 3. Increase of Pensions payable in respect of public service

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, a pension payable to any person under an enactment specified in Schedule 1 shall after 31 December 1969, be increased by the pension authority by an amount equal to a percentage of the annual rate of the pension in accordance with the provisions of Schedule 2.

(2) The said percentage shall be in the case of a pension which began after a period specified in the first column of Schedule 2 be that specified in relation thereto to the second column of Schedule 2.

### 4. Special Pensions and War Pensions

It is lawful for the pension authority to increase any pension in accordance with the provisions of section 3(2) paid to any person entitled—

(a) under any special enactment granting such pension;

(b) to a war pension which has been provided out of sums charged on, and payable out of the Consolidated Fund.

### 5. Legislature to provide sums for an increase

Any additional expenditure incurred by reason of the provisions of this Act in respect of an increase of pension payable to any person entitled thereto shall be defrayed out of monies provided by the House of Assembly.

### 6. False Statements

If for the purpose of obtaining an increase or continuing an increase of a pension under this Act, either for himself or herself or for any other person, or for the purpose of obtaining or continuing such increase at a higher rate than that appropriate to the case, any person knowingly makes a false statement, or false representation, he or she is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $500 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months and, in the case of a pensioner to forfeit any pension or increase of pension payable to him or her.

### 7. Regulations

The pension authority may, where it is deemed expedient make regulations prescribing the manner in which claims for increases in pensions may be made and the procedure to be followed for consideration and determination of claims.
