### AN ACT to make provision with respect to the control and maintenance of public jetties.

Commencement [12 July 1901]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Jetties Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act “**jetties**” means the jetties now existing at Soufrière, Choiseul, Laborie and Vieux-Fort, which have been constructed at the public expense and also any other jetty which may be constructed hereafter at the public expense in any part of this Island or any extension of the said jetties which may be effected hereafter.

### 3. Control and maintenance of jetties

The said jetties shall be subject to the control of the Governor General and shall be maintained at the public expense from the general revenue of Saint Lucia.

### 4. Chief Engineer charged with repair

The Chief Engineer under the direction of the Governor General and subject to the provisions of the Works and Roads Act is charged with the repair and maintenance of jetties.

### 5. Annual report on and estimate of cost of repairs to jetties

(1) The Chief Engineer shall in the month of December in each year, and more often when required by the Governor General, submit to the Governor General, a report on the condition of the jetties accompanied by an estimate of the cost of such repairs as may be necessary to insure their preservation and stability.

(2) Such report and estimate shall be submitted to the House of Assembly.

### 6. Governor General to make rules

It is lawful for the Governor General to make rules in respect of the use of and the prevention of injury to jetties, the landing of goods and passengers, and the regulating of traffic, on them.

### 7. Penalties for offences against rules

A person who offends against any rule made under this Act, on conviction, is liable to a penalty not exceeding $24 without prejudice to any claim for damage that may have been caused by such person to any jetty.

### 8. Ship to be liable for penalty incurred or damage caused by Master

A ship, boat or craft is liable for any penalty incurred, or any damage caused by the Master thereof under the provisions of this Act or of any rule under it, and such ship, boat or craft may, pending legal proceedings, be detained by order of the judge or a district magistrate, unless such penalty or damage is paid or security given for the payment thereof.

### 9. Appointment of special persons to have charge of jetties

To ensure the due observance of any rules made under this Act, the Governor General may in any place where a jetty exists appoint a special person in charge of such jetty.

### 10. Prosecutions for offences against rules

The Chief Engineer or any person authorised by him or her in writing, or the person in charge of any jetty, or any peace officer may prosecute any person before the district court for any offence against the rules.

### 11. Recovery of damage

Any action for the recovery of any damage done to any jetty may be instituted in the name of the Chief Engineer before any court of competent jurisdiction.
