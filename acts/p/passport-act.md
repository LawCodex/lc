# PASSPORT ACT

**(Acts 13 of 1991, 23 of 2003 and 10 of 2011)**

Act 13 of 1991 .. in force 4 January 1992

Amended by Act 23 of 2003 .. in force 15 September 2003

Amended by Act 20 of 2011 .. in force 15 August 2011

### AN ACT to provide for the issue of passports and for related matters.

Commencement [4 January 1992]

### 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Passports Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**an irregular passport**” means a passport that has been obtained by a person who has made a false statement or a false representation for the purpose of obtaining such passport;

“**Minister**” means the Minister responsible for home affairs.

(*Amended by Act 23 of 2003*)

### 3. Citizens entitled to passport

(1) The Minister shall issue a passport on the application of any person who proves that he or she is a citizen of Saint Lucia.

(2) The passport shall be in such form as may be determined by the Minister.

### 4. Cancellation of passport

The Minister may cancel any passport issued to a citizen of Saint Lucia under section 3 on any one or more of the following grounds—

(a) national security;

(b) where the holder of a passport issued under this Act has been convicted of an offence involving—

(i) drug trafficking or possession of illegal drugs,

(ii) trafficking in firearms, their parts and components, ammunition, or explosive materials or derivatives,

(iii) smuggling of persons into, from or through Saint Lucia,

(iv) money laundering.

*(Substituted by Act 23 of 2003)*

### 5. Offences

(1) A person shall not—

(a) make any false statement for the purpose of obtaining a passport;

(b) make any statement on an application for a passport which he or she knows to be false;

(c) intentionally deface, damage or destroy any passport;

(d) forge any passport;

(e) unlawfully produce any passport;

(f) sell forged passports; or

(g) conspire with another person to do any of the acts listed in paragraphs (a) - (f).

(2) A person who contravenes—

(a) subsection (1)(a), (b) or (c) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $5,000; and *(Amended by Act 20 of 2011)*

(b) subsection (1)(d) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $25,000 or to imprisonment for 7 years;

(c) subsection (1)(e) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $50,000 or imprisonment for 15 years;

(d) subsection (1)(f) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $30,000 or to imprisonment for 10 years;

(e) subsection (1)(g) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not less than $500 nor more than $10,000.

(3) Where the offence under subsection (1) involves the use of equipment to make passports, the Court shall, in addition to the penalty specified under subsection (2), declare that the equipment be forfeited to the State.

*(Substituted by Act 23 of 2003)*

### 6. Persons coming from any place outside of State must possess passport or other document

A person other than a person under the age of 16 years coming from any place out of this State shall not land at any port in this State unless he or she has in his or her possession a valid passport issued to him or her not more than 10 years previously by or on behalf of the Government of the country of which he or she is a subject or citizen, or some other document satisfactorily establishing his or her nationality and identity, to which passport or document there must be attached a photograph of the person to whom it relates.

### 7. Power to exempt or prescribe requirements

Cabinet may by order—

(a) exempt any class of persons either unconditionally or subject to such conditions as may be imposed from all or any of the provisions of this Act; and

(b) prescribe any requirement to be fulfilled in respect of their passports by any class or description of aliens coming from any place out of this State as passengers.

### 8. Offences and penalty

(1) If any person acts in contravention of, or fails to comply with, any provision of this Act or any order made or condition imposed under this Act, he or she commits an offence.

(2) A person who, in reply to or in relation to any person lawfully acting in the execution of the provisions of this Act or any order made hereunder—

(a) refuses to answer any questions reasonably put to him or her or to produce any document in his or her possession; or

(b) makes or causes to be made any false statement or false representations; or

(c) obstructs that person in the exercise of his or her powers or duties under this Act or regulations made under it,

commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $1,000 or to imprisonment for 6 months.

(3) A person, whether a citizen of Saint Lucia or not, who without lawful authority uses or has in his or her possession any forged, altered or irregular passport or other document commits an offence.

(4) A person who contravenes subsection (3) is liable on summary conviction to a fine of not less than $10,000 nor more than $15,000 or to imprisonment for 5 years.

*(Amended by Act 23 of 2003)*

### 9. Appeal

A person aggrieved by the decision of the Minister acting under the provisions of section 4, may appeal to an appeal tribunal comprising a judge of the High Court and 2 other persons nominated by Cabinet.

### 10. Regulations

(1) The Minister may by statutory instrument make regulations for—

(a) the form of application for a passport;

(b) one procedure for an appeal where a passport has been withdrawn or cancelled;

(c) the scale of fees for a passport or any renewal or replacement;

(d) the age at which a person may be issued a passport;

(e) any prescribed matter or thing;

(f) for the better administration of the Act.

(2) Provision may be made for infringement of any regulations made under this section by a fine on summary conviction not exceeding $250.
