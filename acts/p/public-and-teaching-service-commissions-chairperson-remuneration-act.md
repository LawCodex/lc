### AN ACT to provide for the remuneration of the Chairperson of the Public and Teaching Service Commissions and related matters.

Commencement [1 October 1997]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public and Teaching Service Commissions (Chairperson) (Remuneration) Act.

### 2. Remuneration

(1) There shall be paid to the Chairperson of the Public and Teaching Service Commissions as remuneration for that Chairperson's services, a salary of $86,400.00 per year.

(2) The Chairperson is entitled to—

(a) an entertainment allowance of $1,200 per year;

(b) a telephone allowance of $2,400 per year; and

(c) a travelling allowance of $4,800 per year.

### 3. Non-pensionable post

The post of Chairperson, Public and Teaching Service Commissions is a non-pensionable post.
