### AN ACT to provide for the payment of remuneration to the Parliamentary Commissioner.

Commencement [7 September 1996]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Commissioner (Remuneration) Act.

### 2. Salary

(1) There shall be paid to the holder of the office of the Parliamentary Commissioner or to any person for the time being lawfully performing the functions of the office a salary of $86,400 per annum.

(2) The salary of the Parliamentary Commissioner shall not be reduced during his or her continuance in office.

### 3. Travelling entertainment and telephone allowances

The Parliamentary Commissioner is entitled to a travelling allowance of $6,000 per year, an entertainment allowance of $1,200 per year and a telephone allowance of $600 per year.

### 4. Gratuity

On the successful completion of a 3-year term of office, the Parliamentary Commissioner is entitled to a gratuity of 25% of the total salary drawn by him or her during his or her period of service.
