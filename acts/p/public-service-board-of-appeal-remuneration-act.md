### AN ACT to provide for the payment of remuneration to the members of the Public Service Board of Appeal and other related matters.

Commencement [1 April 1997]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Public Service Board of Appeal (Remuneration) Act.

### 2. Chairperson

There shall be paid to the chairperson of the Public Service Board of Appeal as remuneration for the chairperson's services—

(a) a retainer fee of $500 per month; and

(b) $150 per meeting.

### 3. Member

There shall be paid to a member of the Public Service Board of Appeal other than the chairperson as remuneration for the services of that member $150 per meeting.
