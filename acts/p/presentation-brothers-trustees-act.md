### AN ACT to provide for the incorporation of certain persons as Trustees of the Presentation Brothers.

Commencement [11 October 1952]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Presentation Brothers (Trustees) Act.

### 2. Incorporation of trustees of the Presentation Brothers

(1) The Superior General of the Presentation Brothers of Mount St. Joseph, Cork, Ireland, the Provincial Superior of the Presentation Brothers in the British West Indies, the local Superior of the Presentation Brothers' Community in the Island of Saint Lucia and the Bursar of the Presentation Brothers' Community in the said Island of Saint Lucia and their Successors in office are hereby created a body corporate by the name of “The Incorporated Trustees of the Presentation Brothers” (hereinafter called the “Trustees”) and by that name shall have perpetual succession, and shall and may sue and be sued in all Courts of Justice in Saint Lucia, and shall have and use a Common Seal provided that such Seal shall only be used in the presence of at least 2 of the Trustees.

(2) The names and addresses of the persons holding the several offices referred to in the preceding subsection shall be notified to the Registrar of Companies and registered in his or her office and be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

### 3. Vesting of property in the Trustees

The property specified in Schedule 1 to this Act and all other property movable and immovable of whatsoever nature now vested in any person or persons for and on behalf of the Presentation Brothers shall be and the same are hereby vested in the Trustees.

### 4. Mortgage in Schedule 2 deemed to have been made by Trustees

The mortgage or hypothecary obligation specified in Schedule 2 to this Act made for an on behalf of the Presentation Brothers shall be deemed to have been made by the Trustees.

### 5. Power of Trustee to acquire property

The Trustees shall have full power to acquire for the Presentation Brothers by purchase, exchange, demise, gift, bequest or otherwise movable or immovable property in Saint Lucia or any estate or interest therein and also any money, securities for money, goods or chattels whatsoever.

### 6. Borrowing power of Trustees

The Trustees shall have the power to borrow money upon the security of any property held by them and for that purpose any document relating thereto shall be signed by not less than 2 Trustees, provided that any mortgage or hypothecary obligation shall be made under the Common Seal of the Trustees as provided hereunder.

### 7. Powers of Trustees to dispose of property

The Trustees shall have the power from time to time by deed under their Common Seal to sell, demise, grant, convey, exchange, mortgage, hypothecate or otherwise dispose of any property vested or which may hereafter be vested in them.

### 8. Saving rights of the crown and bodies corporate

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty or of any bodies politic or corporate or of any other persons, except such as are mentioned or referred to in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.

## Schedule 1

All that area of land being 22.87 acres in extent situate at Vigie in the Quarter of Castries in the Island of Saint Lucia bounded on the North and North-East by the Sea, South-East by lands of the Government of Saint Lucia, South-West by the road leading from the Vigie Airport to the Lighthouse and West by lands of the Government of Saint Lucia, the whole as appears on a Plan of Survey thereof by W.J. Rodrigues, Land Surveyor dated 5 to 10 August 1950 and lodged in the Office of the Commissioner of Crown Lands on 19 August 1950 and recorded under No. 52 of 1950. Together with all the buildings and other structures erected thereon save the wooden dwelling house situate to the North-West of the Rackets Court which is the property of the Government of Saint Lucia and all other the appurtenances and dependencies thereof.

## Schedule 2

Hypothecary Obligation by the Presentation Brothers represented by Brother Canice Collins in favour of the Government of Saint Lucia executed before Maurice Charles Salles-Miquelle, Notary Royal, on 26 November 1951 and registered on 29 November 1951 in Vol. 96a. No. 59082.
