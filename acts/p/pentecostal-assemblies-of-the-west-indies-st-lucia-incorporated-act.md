### AN ACT to make provision for the incorporation of the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies (St. Lucia) Incorporated.

Commencement [8 November 1976]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies (St. Lucia) Incorporated Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act “**Corporation**” means “The Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies (Saint Lucia) Incorporated” established by section 3 of this Act.

### 3. Incorporation of the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies (Saint Lucia) Incorporated

The Presbyter, the Assistant Presbyter and the Secretary of the Corporation and their Successors in office are hereby created a Body Corporate by the name of the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies (Saint Lucia) Incorporated and by that name shall have perpetual succession and may sue and be sued, in all Courts of Justice in the State and shall have and use a Common Seal provide that such seal shall only be used in the presence of at least 2 of the trustees.

### 4. Notification of names and addresses of officers to registrar of companies

(1) The names and addresses of the person holding the several offices referred to in the preceding section shall be notified from time to time to the Registrar of Companies and registered in his or her office and be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

(2) A person last registered in the manner provided by the preceding subsection shall be deemed to be the trustee authorised to act under the provisions of section 7 of this Act.

### 5. Appointment of officers, certification and registration not invalidated by defects

No act or proceeding of the Corporation shall be invalidated by reason of any defect in the appointment of any person to any offices mentioned in section 3 of this Act or by reason of any defect in the notification or in the registration required by subsection (1) of section 4 of this Act.

### 6. Corporation seal

The Seal of the Corporation shall be of such design and pattern as the Corporation may determine.

### 7. Execution of documents

No deed or document purporting to be executed by the Corporation shall be of any force or validity unless it is sealed with the Corporation's Seal and signed by not less than 2 Trustees of the Corporation.

### 8. Powers of corporation

The Corporation shall have the following powers

(a) to acquire, hold, purchase, lease, possess and enjoy any lands and hereditaments whatsoever, absolutely or for leasehold or for any other estate or interest therein and all other property, real or personal;

(b) to give, grant, let, charge, improve, manage, develop, exchange, lease, mortgage, sell, convey, assign, dispose of, turn to account or otherwise deal with all or any of the property, both present and future, so held or vested or any part hereof;

(c) to borrow, raise, or secure the payment of money in such manner as may be thought fit and in particular by the issue of debentures or script charged upon all or any of the property, both present and future, held by or vested in the Corporation, and to redeem and pay off any such securities;

(d) to appoint a person or persons as the attorneys of the Corporation either generally or for a limited period and for such purposes and with such powers as may be stated in the power of attorney, and to revoke any such appointment.

### 9. Saving of crown and other rights

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or Successors, or of any bodies politic or corporate or of any other persons except such as are mentioned or referred to in this Act and those claiming, by, through, from or under them.
