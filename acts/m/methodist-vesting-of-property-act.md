### AN ACT to vest the Property of Methodists in Saint Lucia in the agents of the M.C.C.A. Trust Corporation and for matters incidental thereto and connected therewith.

Commencement [23 October 1993]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Methodist (Vesting of Property) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**agents**” means the Superintendent Minister and 2 Circuit Stewards of the Methodist Church of Saint Lucia appointed to act on behalf of the Corporation for the purposes of this Act;

“**ancillary bodies**” in relation to the Church, to the Parent Church, or to the Missionary Society, means, schools, colleges, seminaries, hospitals, bookshops, charities, associations, institutions and other organizations (other than local bodies) subsidiary or ancillary to or connected with the Church, the Parent Church or the Missionary Society respectively, and it is hereby declared that the provisions of this Act applicable to any such subsidiary or ancillary bodies which are connected with other churches or organizations as well as with the Church, the Parent Church or the Missionary Society shall apply to such subsidiary or ancillary bodies only to the extent of the interest therein of the Church, the Parent Church or the Missionary Society;

“**British Conference**” means the governing body of the Parent Church;

“**Church**” means the Methodist Church in the Caribbean and the Americas constituted and established as before recited, but “the church” and “churches,” have their general meanings;

“**Conference**” means the governing body of the Church and where the context so requires includes the Foundation Conference;

“**Corporation**” means the M.C.C.A. Trust Corporation;

“**Deed of Church Order**” means a Deed dated the 18 day of May 1967 and executed by the Reverend Hugh B. Sherlock and others which declared the establishment of the Church and defined the constitution thereof;

“**Foundation Conference**” means the conference held by representatives of the Parent Church and of certain Methodist synods in the Caribbean and the Americas for the purpose of settling and adopting the Deed of Church Order;

“**land**” or “**lands**” includes land and buildings of any tenure and any interest therein or in the proceeds of sale thereof;

“**local bodies**” in relation to the Church or the Parent Church means districts, circuits and societies or and other local bodies as the work of the Church or the Parent Church may be ordered in for the time being, and the synods and other governing bodies thereof;

“**Methodist Missionary Society**” is also known and called Methodist Church Overseas Division;

“**Missionary Society**” means the Methodist Missionary Society of 25 Marylebone Road, London, N.W.I England, being the Overseas Department or Division of the Parent Church;

“**Model Trusts**” means the model trusts referred to in section 5;

“**Parent Church**” means the Methodist Church of Great Britain formed on the 20 day of September 1932 (upon the execution that date in England of a Deed of Union by John Scott Lidgett and others) through the Union Weslyan Methodist Church, the Primitive Methodist Church and the United Methodist Church;

“**property**” means property of every description whether real or personal, movable or immovable and includes money and choses in action.

### 3. Vesting of real and personal property

(1) All lands in Saint Lucia now held by or vested in the Methodists or held by or vested in any person or persons on behalf of the said Methodists and in particular the lands described in the Schedule and in the Deeds referred to in the Schedule are hereby transferred to and vested in the agents on behalf of the Corporation, without further assurance, for the same estates and interest and to the extent to which the same were, immediately before the coming into operation of this Act, held or vested as aforesaid, subject to the then rights, reservations and charges, if any.

(2) If after the vesting in the agents of any property under subsection (1), any person or organisation divested by this section continues to be under any liability to any third party, being a liability which has been incurred or has arisen in connection with such property, the agents shall keep such person or organisation fully indemnified against such liability.

### 4. Adaptation of trusts

(1) All lands and personal chattels in Saint Lucia held upon trust, for the purposes of the Parent Church, by the Missionary Society or any of their local or ancillary bodies in connection with the work in the Conference area, shall on or after the commencement of this Act be held by the agents upon similar trusts for the purposes of the Church or of its corresponding local or ancillary body and as if references to the Church were substituted for references to the Parent Church or the Missionary Society in any declaration of such first-mentioned trusts but otherwise with and subject to the powers and provisions applicable thereto immediately before the commencement of this Act.

(2) All moneys, bank accounts and choses in action held in Saint Lucia by or on behalf of or in trust for any local body of the Parent Church or the Missionary Society now forming part of the Church shall continue to be held by the agents by or on behalf of the said local body but in its capacity as a local body of the Church rather than of the Parent Church or the Missionary Society.

(3) Whenever the words “Methodist Church” or “Conference” are used in any Act, Deed, Will or other instrument as applying to the Parent Church or in British Conference in connection with work now carried on by the Church such words shall on and after the passing of this Act be construed in their application to property to mean the Church and the Conference respectively.

(4) Any device or bequest contained in a Will or Codicil made by any person living at the commencement of this Act in favour of or directed to be administered by or in connection with the Parent Church, the Missionary Society or any of their local or ancillary bodies in connection with work now carried on by the Church shall (save in so far as such device or bequest is on its true construction intended to be applied for work of the Parent Church or of the Missionary Society carried on outside the area of the Church) take effect in favour of or be administered by the agents or in local or ancillary body and as if references to the Church were substituted for references to the Parent Church or the Missionary Society in such Will or Codicil, but otherwise subject to the powers and provisions set out therein.

### 5. Model trusts

(1) It shall be lawful for the Conference, by resolution passed and confirmed in such manner as may, for the time being, be required for the alteration of the Deed of Church Order, to settle and adopt a form of model trusts (being the Model Trusts) capable of application to any lands held or to be held in connection with the Church or any of its local or ancillary bodies for the purposes of a church, chapel, vestry, minister's or other dwelling-house, day or Sunday school, schoolroom, lecture hall, mission hall or other building or burial ground.

(2) It shall be lawful for the Conference by resolution passed and confirmed as aforesaid, from time to time, to revoke, alter or add to any of the provisions of the Model Trusts other than any such provisions embodying the doctrinal standards of the Church.

(3) The agents, or other owner or owners, for the time being of any lands such as are referred to in subsection (1) may at any time by declaration under seal and forthwith transmitted to the Secretary of the Conference adopt the Model Trusts in relation to such lands and such lands shall thenceforth be held upon the trusts and subject to the powers and provisions set out in the Modal Trusts.

(4) If such a declaration relates only to part of the lands held by the agents on behalf of the Corporation or other such owner or owners subsection (3) shall apply to such part.

(5) If such lands are held jointly by more owners than one then such a declaration shall be valid and effective if executed by a majority of such owners.

(6) Upon any revocation or alteration of or addition to the Model Trusts any lands then already held upon the Trusts thereof shall thenceforth be held upon such trusts as amended by such revocation, alteration or addition and thereafter any reference to the Model Trusts in this Act or in any document whenever executed shall be construed and take effect as a reference to the Model Trusts as varied by such revocation, alteration or addition.

### 6. Evidence of documents

(1) A copy, signed by the President, Vice President or Secretary of the Foundation Conference or of the Conference or by the agents, of any of the documents specified in subsection (2) shall be deemed to be a true copy of such document and shall be received in evidence in all courts and in all proceedings whether civil or criminal as if it were the original documents, unless some error is proved.

(2) The following are the documents referred to in subsection (1), namely:

(i) the Deed of Church Order;

(ii) the Model Trusts;

(iii) any resolution of the Foundation Conference or of the Conference settling, adopting, revoking, altering or adding to the Deed of Church Order or the Model Trusts or any part thereof;

(iv) any resolution of the Corporation amending the objects and powers or the Articles of Association of the Corporation;

(v) any declaration made under section 5(3).

### 7. Legal proceedings

(1) All actions and proceedings by or on behalf of or against the Church may be brought or defended by and in the name of the agents and of “the President of the Methodist Conference in the Caribbean and the Americas” by that description for and on behalf of the Church and the death or incapacity of the agents or the President for the time being of the Conference or the expiration of his or her term of office or any act or thing done or suffered by him or her shall not abate or affect any such action or proceeding.

(2) Any writ, notice or other process in any such action or proceeding shall be sufficiently served on the Church if served personally on the agents or on the President or Secretary for the time being of the Conference or left at the office of the agents or of the Conference.

(3) Any affidavit, statutory or other declaration or other similar document required from or desired by the Church may be made by the agents or the President, Vice President or Secretary for the time being of the Conference for and on behalf of the Church.

(4) Every President, Vice President and Secretary of the Conference and the agents shall be indemnified out of the funds of the Church against all loss, damages, costs and expense which he or she may sustain by reason of anything done to or by him or her in pursuance of this Act.

### 8. Corporation not have alien status

Notwithstanding that the agents of the Corporation may at any time or from time to time for the purposes of the Aliens (Landholding Regulation) Act, or any Act for the time being amending or replacing that Act, be or deemed to be under alien control, the agents of the Corporation shall not be nor be entitled to hold land and mortgages on land in Saint Lucia on behalf of the Corporation without licence and such land and mortgages shall not be liable to forfeiture under the said Act or any other law that may be applicable to aliens.

### 9. Trustees indemnity

Nothing in this Act shall deprive the agents of the Corporation or any other trustee of any rights to which but for this Act he or she would be entitled to be indemnified out of the trust property in respect of any mortgage, charge, incumbrance, lien, bond, covenant or other obligation under which he or she may have incurred personal liability.

### 10. Saving of rights

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the rights of the State or of any body politic or corporate or of any other person or persons and those claiming by, from, through or under them except such as are mentioned in this Act.
