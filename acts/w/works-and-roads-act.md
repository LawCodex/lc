### AN ACT to make provision for the appointment of a Chief Engineer; for the due execution of works; and for the making and preservation of roads.

Commencement [25 May 1905]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Works and Roads Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires—

“**Authority**” means, as regards public highways, and public by-ways, (vested at or after the commencement of this Act), the Works Division of the Ministry of Communications and Works as represented by the Chief Engineer and includes his or her duly appointed officers and agents;

“**cart**” means any cart, wagon, wain, truck, barrow or other carriage on wheels used to carry loads or burdens;

“**Minister**” means the Member charged with the responsibility for the Ministry of Communications and Works;

“**owner**” means the owner or reputed owner or lessee (and in the case of joint owners or lessees or tenants in common of any land, then any one of such joint owners or lessees or tenants in common) of any land, or the attorney or agent of any such owner or lessee, in charge of such land, and if no such owner or lessee or agent be known or be resident in Saint Lucia, then the person in possession or occupation of such land;

“**road**” means a road constructed or maintained at the public expense, and includes all ditches, banks, slopes, causeways, bridges, walls and other works necessary to the construction and maintenance of any road; such term may mean and include either a public highway or a public by-way or both;

“**works**” means work of any kind constructed or maintained from the general revenue of the State.

*(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

*The Chief Engineer*

### 3. Appointment and tenure of office of Chief Engineer

Her Majesty, or the Governor General on Her Majesty's behalf, may appoint an officer, to be called the Chief Engineer, who shall be charged with the duties and obligations by this Act imposed on him or her, and who shall hold office during Her Majesty's pleasure.

### 4. General duties of Chief Engineer

The Chief Engineer shall be charged, subject to the authority, direction and control of the Governor General, with the construction, repair, and supervision of all works, buildings, wharves, public highways and bridges, the cost of which is to be defrayed from the general revenue of Saint Lucia. *(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

### 5. Officers to have no interest in contracts

The Chief Engineer or any clerk, overseer, or road surveyor under him or her, shall not be pecuniarily interested directly or indirectly, in any contract for the execution of any public work, and shall not exact or accept any commission, fee or reward in connection with such work other than the salary and allowances paid to them, and any other salaries or allowances attached to any other office which may be held by them under the Government.

### 6. Contracts for Public Works

The Chief Engineer may with the approval of the Minister enter into contracts on behalf of Saint Lucia for the execution of any public work, or for the repair or keeping in repair or construction of any public roads or bridges. *(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

### 7. Contractor's claim to be certified before being paid

Payment shall not be made on any such contract unless the Chief Engineer has certified that the sum claimed is justly due on such contract.

*Classification of Roads*

### 8. Classification of roads

The several roads mentioned and specified in the Schedule to this Act are hereby declared to be public highways and public by-ways respectively, and shall be kept in repair out of money voted annually by the Legislature from the revenue of Saint Lucia. Public highways shall include any part thereof which now is or at any time hereafter shall be within the boundaries of any town or village except Castries. Public by-ways shall not include any road or part of a road which now is or at any time hereafter shall be within the boundaries of any town or village.

### 9. Roads may be proclaimed to be public highways or public by-ways

It shall be lawful for the Governor General with the consent of the House of Assembly signified by a resolution to that effect to declare by proclamation to be published in the Gazette any road not included in the Schedule to be a public highway or public by-way or to transfer roads from one class to the other, and with the like consent and in the like manner to declare that any public highway or public by-way cease to be a public highway or public by-way under this Act.

*Miscellaneous*

### 10. Width of roads

(1) The maximum width to which a public highway may be repaired and kept in repair shall be 30 feet and a public by-way 16 feet, exclusive of ditches and side drains. However, compensation shall be payable in accordance with the next subsection to the owner lessee or occupier for the value of any soil encroached over 24 feet and 16 feet respectively with allowances being made for ditches and side drains.

(2) Where for any special reason any road or part thereof shall require to be widened beyond the limits specified in the last preceding subsection or the course of a road shall require to be altered, the same shall be widened or altered in such manner as the Authority shall direct, and there shall be payable to the owner for the value of the soil encroached on for the purpose of such widening or alteration, as well as to the lessee or other occupier of such soil for the value of any crop standing thereon at the time, such compensation as shall be agreed upon between the Authority and the owner or lessee or other occupier as aforesaid; and in case of difference between the Authority and such owner or lessee or other occupier with regard to such compensation, either party may give to the other 7 days' notice in writing of his or her intention to refer the matter to the magistrate within whose district the land is situate, who shall hear, settle and determine the amount of such compensation, and the costs attending the hearing and determining the same. The award of such magistrate shall be final.

*(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

### 11. Duties of the magistrate when making award

The magistrate shall before making his or her award estimate the advantage as well as disadvantage, if any, accruing to the adjacent land from the widening or alteration of the course of any road, and if in his or her opinion the value of such advantage exceeds the value of the land encroached upon or more than compensates for the material taken or the damage suffered by the owner or occupier of the adjacent land, the compensation shall be nominal.

### 12. Tender of compensation

At any time previous to an arbitration the Authority may by notice served upon the owner, lessee or other occupier tender to such owner, lessee or other occupier a sum by way of compensation, and if on arbitration the amount of the award is not greater than the sum so tendered, the costs of such arbitration shall be borne by the owner, lessee or other occupier, but in any other case the said costs shall be payable as regards public highways and public by-ways out of the general revenue of the State. *(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

### 13. Authority may make temporary road

(1) Where a road becomes impassable or dangerous the Authority may make a road through lands (not being the ground whereon any house stands, nor being a garden, yard, court, plantation, provision ground, or avenue to any house) to be made use of as a public road, whilst the old road is being repaired, widened, or altered, and the Authority shall make compensation to the owner or occupier of such lands for the damage he or she may sustain thereby, and in case of difference between them, the amount shall be assessed as provided in the foregoing sections 10, 11 and 12.

(2) In such case the magistrate shall estimate the advantage and disadvantage, if any, accruing to the adjacent land from the making of such a road.

### 14. Authority may regulate rivers

The Authority may alter or regulate the course of any river, stream or watercourse in such manner as the Authority may deem necessary for the preservation and maintenance of any road; and any claim for compensation in consequence of any such alteration or regulation shall be rendered to and may be fixed by agreement with the Authority, or may be assessed in like manner as any compensation with reference to any widened or altered road as hereinbefore directed.

### 15. Power of Authority to take material

The Authority or any road surveyor, contractor or overseer or other person authorised by the Authority may take any material required for the making or repairing of any road or bridge from any river, stream, or ravine, or from any land in any place in which any part of such road may be or may be required, and for that purpose may open quarries on such land and may carry or convey such material so taken through any such land from which such material is taken or through which such material is carried or conveyed, not being a garden, yard, park, planted walk, or avenue to a house, or any land planted in canes, cocoa, or spices. However—

(a) the land from which such material may be taken shall not be a garden, yard, park, planted walk or avenue to a house, or any land planted in canes, cocoa, or spices, and that the digging or taking of material therefrom shall not affect the foundations of any house or building thereon or the grounds immediately surrounding such house or building;

(b) when the land from which such material is taken or through which such material is carried or conveyed, shall be waste land, payment shall not be made to the owner of such land in respect of such material so taken or conveyed;

(c) where the land from which such material is taken or through which such material is carried or conveyed, shall be enclosed land, there shall be payable to the owner of such land such compensation for the material so taken, or for any damage done to such land by the passage of such material through such land as shall be agreed upon between the Authority and the owner of such land, and in case of difference between the Authority and such owner, such compensation may be assessed in like manner as any compensation with reference to any widened or altered road as hereinbefore directed. However, where the material taken is not of any marketable value, the compensation to the owner of the land from which the same is taken shall be nominal;

(d) the Authority or the road surveyor, contractor or other persons authorised as aforesaid, shall cause to be levelled or sloped all quarries, pits or banks fenced, whence such material is taken or rail or fence off such quarries or pits and maintain such rails or fences, so that the same shall not be dangerous to persons or cattle.

### 16. Notice to be given by Authority

Whenever the Authority or any person authorised by the Authority intends to take from any land, materials for the making, widening or altering of any road, the Authority shall give 7 days notice in writing of such intention to the owner, lessee or occupier of such land.

### 17. Planting near precipitous sides of road prohibited

(1) A person commits an offence who

(a) plants or cultivates or, having been previously convicted of planting or cultivating, continues to plant or cultivate any steep, precipitous, or sloping land within 10 feet from the top of any cutting or bank of any road so as to cause such cutting or bank to slide or to cause obstruction of the drains or otherwise endanger such road;

(b) undermines, digs, excavates the ground or burns wood for charcoal or cuts down any trees or shrubs at the base of or under the precipitous part or side of any road;

(c) injures any road by drawing on it logs, stones or things of weight otherwise than upon a wheeled carriage; or

(d) uses any instrument for the purpose of retarding the descent of any wheeled carriage down any hill in such manner as unnecessarily and wilfully to injure the surface.

### 18. Canals kept in repair by owners

If any canal or conduit for the conveyance of water shall run along or across a road, the parts of such road crossed or intersected by such canal or conduit shall be repaired and kept in repair to the satisfaction of the Authority at the sole cost of the owner, and the part of such canal or conduit which runs along such road shall be embanked, maintained, and repaired by the owner as aforesaid to the satisfaction of the Authority; and if the owner shall fail after 7 days' notice given by the Authority to comply with the provisions of this section he or she commits an offence against this Act for every such case of non-compliance, and it shall be lawful for the Authority to execute the necessary work and to recover the cost thereof from the defaulting owner together with the penalty aforesaid.

### 19. Authority may give notice to remedy encroachments

(1) If any fence, hedge or tree encroaches upon any road, or if any person neglects to trim the fences, hedges, trees or canes growing on any land owned, leased or occupied by him or her so that any road is encroached upon, the Authority may give notice in writing to the owner of the land to remove or remedy such encroachment.

(2) If such owner fails, within 3 days after receiving such notice, to remove or remedy such encroachment he or she commits an offence against this Act. However, it shall be lawful for the Authority after the expiration of 3 days from the service of any such notice to cause the removal of or to remedy the encroachment referred to in such notice and to recover the cost of such removal.

### 20. Owners to provide bridges and drains

(1)

(a) A person shall not without the consent in writing first had and obtained of the Chief Engineer, open, construct, raise, lower, or otherwise alter a road that crosses or enters the route of any public road.

(b) The Chief Engineer shall issue such instructions to the owner or party proposing to perform or performing any of such acts in respect of the road as regards the execution of the work for the prevention of surface water falling or flowing into or over the footway of the highway.

(c) If any person shall fail to obtain such consent before executing such works or shall fail to comply with a requirement of the Chief Engineer he or she commits an offence and is liable in respect thereof to a fine of $100, and if the offence in respect of which he or she is convicted is continued after the conviction he or she commits a further offence and is liable in respect thereof to a fine not exceeding $50 for each day on which the offence is so continued.

(2) For preventing damage to roads at their junction with private roads or paths by crossing the drains of roads with carts, it shall be lawful for the Authority to require any owner of land through which such private roads or paths may be, to make provision for the erection of wooden bridges or covered drains for the crossing of the drains of the road or for keeping open or clean the drains at such junction aforesaid.

(3) If within 14 days from the date of such requisition the owner shall fail to comply therewith or shall continue to allow water from his or her land to run on to the road, the Authority may provide such necessary bridges or drains or open and repair such drains, and shall recover the cost and expense of all such work on complaint before a Court of Summary Jurisdiction.

*(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

### 21. Offence of placing dead animal on a road

A person who places or causes to be placed on a road a dead animal or other filth, and a person in possession of land on a part of which, within 100 yards of a road, the carcase of any dead animal is left exposed, commits an offence.

### 22. Offence concerning water on highway

(1) The Chief Engineer may by notice to the occupier or owner of the premises adjoining the highway, require him or her within 3 months from the date of the service to construct or erect and thereafter to maintain such gutters, down pipes, channels or covered watercourse of suitable material so constructed and laid as may be necessary to prevent—

(a) water from the roof and any other part of the premises falling upon persons using the highway; or

(b) so far as reasonably practical, surface water from the premises flowing on to or over the footway of the highway.

(2) The notice may be served on the owner of the premises in question instead of the occupier or may be served on both the owner and the occupier of the premises.

(3) If a person, on whom a notice is served fails to comply with a requirement of the notice within the period specified in subsection (1), he or she commits an offence and is liable in respect thereof to a fine not exceeding $100, and if the offence in respect of which he or she is convicted is continued after the conviction he or she commits a further offence and is liable in respect thereof to a fine not exceeding $10 for each day in which the offence is so continued.

### 23. Certain offences

(1) A person commits an offence who impedes the free flow of water in, or dam or fills up or otherwise obstruct—

(a) any ditch or drain adjoining any road;

(b) any ditch, drain or watercourse from any road which shall be connected with any ditch, drain or watercourse being in or upon any land;

(c) any ditch, drain, or watercourse on any land into or through which the water of any ditch, drain, or watercourse in the last preceding subsection mentioned shall flow; or

(d) any ditch, drain or watercourse under any road.

(2) It is lawful for the Authority to do all things necessary for effectually removing every such obstruction at the cost of the person so offending.

### 24. Molesting Authority, etc.

A person who obstructs, disturbs or molests the Authority in the execution of any act or duty done or being done under this Act commits an offence against this Act. *(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

### 25. Action by or against Saint Lucia

In any action brought by or against Saint Lucia in respect of any contract or of any public work the words “Saint Lucia” shall be a sufficient description of Saint Lucia, and service of process at the office of the Chief Engineer shall be deemed good and sufficient service in any such action.

### 26. Protection of Public Officers

(1) An action shall not be brought against Saint Lucia or any road surveyor or Authority in respect of any act, matter, or thing done under this Act, unless such action is commenced within 3 months of the time when the cause of action arose, and unless notice in writing is given to the defendant in any such action of the intention to bring such action; and such notice shall set out the cause of action and the sum if any demanded by way of damages. *(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

(2) If the plaintiff fails to prove the service of the said notice at least one month before action brought he or she shall be non-suited with costs. However, if the defendant at any time before such action is brought, tenders to the plaintiff or pays into court to the credit of the plaintiff sufficient compensation for the wrong or injury in respect of which the action is brought, and the plaintiff refuses to accept such compensation, the court shall, upon proof of the circumstances, give judgment for the plaintiff only for the amount so tendered or paid into court, subject to deduction therefrom of the costs of the action.

### 27. Limitation of powers of Authority

The Authority shall not have power to cut down cocoa or fruit bearing trees, except as expressly authorised by this Act, or to purchase or take possession of lands, or otherwise to involve the Government in claims for compensation without first obtaining the permission of Cabinet. *(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

*Penalties and Procedure*

### 28. Onus of proof of ownership

(1) In any proceedings under this Act, where any question arises as to the ownership of any land or tree or part thereof, the onus of proof shall lie on the defendant and not on the prosecutor.

(2) Any notice given or left at any premises under any provision of this Act or any regulation made under this Act may be addressed by the description of the “owner” of the land or ground (describing it) without any further name or description.

(3) On the trial of any person for an offence against this Act, the production to the magistrate of any notice purporting to be certified as a true copy by the Chief Engineer or such person duly appointed by him or her shall be *prima facie* evidence of the contents of such notice and of the service thereof.

### 29. Penalty for offences

A person who commits an offence against this Act and a person who contravenes any regulation made hereunder shall, on summary conviction, be liable to a penalty not exceeding $96.

### 30. Procedure

(1) All fines and penalties incurred under this Act or under any regulation made hereunder may be proceeded for by the Commissioner of Police or by the officer in charge of any police station.

(2) Where any expenses, fines, or penalties are alleged to have been incurred in relation to a public highway and to have arisen under any provision of this Act or of any regulation affecting the same, the proceedings for the recovery thereof may be taken by the Chief Engineer.

(3) Where any expenses, fines, or penalties are alleged to have been incurred in relation to a public by-way, and to have arisen under any provision of this Act or of any regulation affecting the same, the proceedings for the recovery of such expenses, fines, or penalties may be taken in the name of the Chief Engineer or such person duly appointed by him or her.

*(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

### 31. Procedure

(1) All proceedings for the recovery of any expenses or penalties under this Act, or under any regulation made hereunder, shall be heard and determined by the magistrate of the magisterial district in which the matter or offence shall occur; the mode of procedure shall be in accordance with the procedure prescribed by any Act regulating the duties of and procedure before magistrates.

(2) Court fees or stamp duties shall not be payable in the first instance in respect of such proceedings. However, the Court may order the defendant to pay full costs in cases in which judgment is given against the defendant.

### 32. Fines to be paid into the Treasury

Where in any proceedings for offences committed on, or in relation to, any public by-way, any fines or penalties are imposed and recovered, the same shall be paid into the Treasury for the credit of the General Revenue of the State. *(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*

### 33. Regulations

(1) The Cabinet may make regulations for giving full force and effect to the general scope and intention of this Act and to protect roads from injury and for giving fuller and better effect to any of the provisions hereof.

(2) Without prejudice to the foregoing general power, which shall not be deemed to be limited by the following enumeration of particular powers, the Cabinet may make regulations—

(a) for the alteration, discontinuance, or stopping up of any road or bridge;

(b) for declaring or limiting the use by the public of any road or bridge, either as to the time of such public use, or as to the character of the traffic on such road or bridge, or for both purposes;

(c) for the erection of telegraph or telephone posts and wires;

(d) for laying down railways, tramways or crossings of the same respectively on any road;

(e) for the general regulation of the traffic on roads, and for the survey, control, use, management, construction and repair thereof and of all bridges, and for the prevention and removal of obstruction thereon, and otherwise for the general good rule and government thereof;

(f) for the prevention of any nuisance on any road;

(g) for prohibiting the use upon any road of any cart, and for regulating and declaring the manner and conditions under which the same may be used or driven over any road;

(h) to declare offences against this Act, and any regulations made hereunder.

*(Amended by Act 20 of 1969)*
