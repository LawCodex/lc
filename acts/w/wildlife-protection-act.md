### AN ACT to make provision for the protection, conservation and management of wildlife in Saint Lucia.

Commencement [19 September 1980]

## PRELIMINARY

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Wildlife Protection Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires—

“**alien wildlife**” means any species of wildlife not indigenous to Saint Lucia;

“**close season**” means any period declared to be a close season under section 9;

“**court**” means a court of summary jurisdiction;

“**deleterious substance**” means any substance which if added to water would endanger the subsistence and maintenance of fish and other aquatic life residing in water;

“**forest officer**” means any person appointed as such under the Forest, Soil and Water Conservation Act, and includes a person so appointed by virtue of his or her office;

“**gun**” includes an airgun, pellet gun, sling shot and any device capable of propelling shots, bullets or missiles;

“**hunt**” means to seek, stalk, shoot, pursue or to attempt to seek, stalk, shoot or pursue;

“**Minister**” means the Minister responsible for matters relating to wildlife;

“**officer**” means a Wildlife Protection Officer appointed by or under this Act;

“**open season**” means any period during which a specified species or group of species of wildlife may be hunted or taken;

“**partially protected wildlife**” means any species of wildlife specified in Schedule 2;

“**protected wildlife**” means any species of wildlife specified in Schedule 1;

“**take**” means to kill, capture, snare, trap, net, carry away, or to attempt to kill, capture, snare, trap, net, carry away or by any means other than a means specified in section 16;

“**territorial sea**” means a distance of 12 nautical miles round the shores of Saint Lucia;

“**unprotected wildlife**” means any species of wildlife which is specified in Schedule 3;

“**wildlife**” means any species of the following groups living beyond the control of man—

(a) mammals;

(b) birds and the eggs thereof;

(c) frogs and the eggs thereof;

(d) reptiles;

(e) fishes, their fry and eggs; and

(f) crustaceans;

“**wildlife reserve**” means any area declared as such under this Act.

## PART 1 ADMINISTRATION

### 3. Appointment of Chief and other wildlife protection officers

(1) There shall be appointed a Chief Wildlife Protection Officer whose office shall be a public officer and who shall be charged with the duties and possess the powers conferred by or under this Act. He or she shall act under the authority of the Minister and under the general direction of the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry. He or she shall have supervision of the other wildlife protection officers and may delegate to any of them any of his or her duties and powers under this Act. In the absence of any other appointment the Director of Agricultural Services shall be the Chief Wildlife Protection Officer.

(2) There shall be appointed such other public officers to be known as wildlife protection officers as are necessary for the purposes of this Act. In the absence of any other appointment all forest officers are hereby appointed wildlife protection officers. Every wildlife protection officer shall perform such duties and possess such powers under this Act as may be assigned to him or her by the Chief Wildlife Protection Officer.

### 4. Duties of Chief Wildlife Protection Officer

(1) The duties and powers of the Chief Wildlife Protection Officer shall be—

(a) to administer and enforce the provisions of this Act and the Rules thereunder;

(b) to carry out research or surveys for the protection, control or disposition of wildlife or wildlife products including the establishment of laboratories, zoos, and other related services;

(c) to gather, print and circulate information and reports pertaining to wildlife;

(d) to enter into contracts with individuals, agencies or companies for the construction of trails, roads, boundary marks, building hatcheries and other works for wildlife;

(e) to provide facilities for visitors to wildlife reserves, zoos and other related establishments;

(f) to engage in all other activities necessary for the purposes of this Act.

### 5. Rights and privileges of wildlife protection officers

For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this Act, every wildlife protection officer has the authority, privileges and protection of a police constable conferred or granted by any law in force in Saint Lucia.

## PART 2 WILDLIFE MANAGEMENT PROTECTION AND CONTROL

### 6. Wildlife property of Crown

Wildlife, resident or migratory, indigenous or alien, found in Saint Lucia, except fish, frogs or crustaceans in private ponds, are the property of the Crown and may be taken or hunted only at such times, in such places and in such manner as is provided by this Act.

### 7. Minister may declare wildlife reserve

The Minister may—

(a) by order declare any area of land or water or the territorial sea to be a wildlife reserve;  ***Editor's note:*** *By Gazette notice 1016/1982 the following was declared a Wildlife Reserve with effect from 30 September 1988: the Group of Islands known as Maria Islands situated off the southern Coast of Saint Lucia near the town of Vieux-Fort.*

(b) lease, exchange, buy or sell any property for use as a wildlife reserve.

### 8. Classification of wildlife

Wildlife is hereby divided into 3 categories, namely, protected wildlife as specified in Schedule 1, partially protected wildlife as specified in Schedule 2 and Unprotected Wildlife as specified in Schedule 3.

### 9. Power of Minister to vary Schedules

(1) The Minister may by statutory instrument—

(a) add to, vary or delete from any of the species of wildlife specified in any of the said Schedules;

(b) make provision for the protection or control of any of the species of wildlife listed in any of the said Schedules;

(c) declare a close season for any of the species of wildlife specified in Schedule 2.

(2) The powers of the Minister under this section shall include the power by statutory instrument as aforesaid to declare, for any period not exceeding 3 years, a close season in respect of any or all of the species of wildlife specified in Schedule 2.

## PART 3 LICENCES AND PERMITS

### 10. Power of Minister to issue licences and permits for certain purposes

(1) The Minister may, on application made to him or her, issue licences for any of the following purposes, namely—

(a) to hunt or to take wildlife in a wildlife reserve;

(b) to import or export any wildlife into or from Saint Lucia;

(c) to keep any wildlife in captivity.

(2) A licence issued under the preceding subsection may contain such conditions as the Minister may think fit.

(3) The Minister may issue permits to any recognized scientific, cultural or educational organization to hunt or to take wildlife. Such permits may specify the wildlife to be hunted or taken, the person authorised to do so and any other condition which the Minister considers necessary or appropriate.

(4) Except by the authority of a licence or permit issued by the Minister under this section, it is an offence for any person to hunt or to take any wildlife in a wildlife reserve.

### 11. Validity and duration of licence

(1) A licence issued under section 10(1) by this Act shall be valid only for the period during which it is issued and shall in any event expire on 31 December following the date of issue.

(2) Every such licence shall be valid only in respect of the person to whom it is issued and shall not be transferable.

(3) Every holder of such licence shall carry it with him or her when engaged in any activity authorised by it; and he or she shall show such licence upon the request of any wildlife protection officer.

### 12. Cancellation and duplicate licences and permits

(1) The Minister may at any time in his or her discretion cancel any licence or permit issued under this part.

(2) Where any licence or permit has been lost, stolen or destroyed a duplicate may be issued by the Minister upon payment of the prescribed fee.

### 13. Licence does not confer rights to enter upon private property

A licence or permit issued under the provisions of this Act shall not confer upon the holder thereof any right to enter upon any private lands or waters.

## PART 4 OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

### 14. Hunting or taking protected wildlife

Except by authority of a licence or permit issued under section 10, a person commits an offence who—

(a) hunts or takes any protected wildlife or the eggs, fry or young thereof;

(b) damages or destroys the nest, fry or young of any protected wildlife;

(c) permits any dog to enter or to be in wildlife reserve;

(d) carries in a wildlife reserve any gun or weapon or device capable of being used to hunt or to take any protected wildlife.

### 15. Taking or hunting partially protected wildlife

A person commits an offence who during a close season—

(a) takes or hunts any partially protected wildlife; or

(b) damages or destroys the nest or egg of any partially protected wildlife.

### 16. Using devices or substances to take wildlife

A person commits an offence who—

(a) sets in any position any electrical device designed to frighten any protected or partially protected wildlife;

(b) sets in any position any article of any nature and which is likely to cause or is calculated to cause injury to any protected wildlife;

(c) sets in any place any noxious substance for the purpose of killing, taking, injuring or stupefying or which is likely or calculated to kill, injure or stupefy or is otherwise injurious to any protected or partially protected wildlife;

(d) for the purpose of killing or capturing any protected or partially protected wildlife, uses any live animal or any decoy or appliance whatsoever;

(e) uses any form of artificial light for the purpose of killing or capturing any protected or partially protected wildlife;

(f) deposits or permits the deposit of any deleterious substance of any type in water frequented by fish, shrimps or crabs or in any place where such deleterious substance may enter such water;

(g) constructs or excavates or maintains any pit for the purpose of catching or killing wildlife;

(h) engaged in logging, lumbering, land clearing or other operation puts or permits to be put or to flow any slash, stumps or other debris into any water frequented by fish, shrimps, or crabs.

### 17. Possession of wildlife

A person commits an offence who—

(a) except by authority of a licence issued under section 10, has in his or her possession or control any protected wildlife;

(b) exposes or offers for sale or purchases any protected wildlife or any part of such wildlife or the eggs, fry or young thereof;

(c) has in his or her possession or control any partially protected wildlife or any part of any such wildlife or the eggs, fry or young thereof during the close season;

(d) exposes or offers for sale or purchases any partially protected wildlife during the close season or the eggs, fry or young thereof during the close season; or

(e) imports or exports any wildlife into or from Saint Lucia without first having obtained a licence.

### 18. Licence to keep in captivity

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act and of any other law, it shall be lawful to hunt or to take any species of wildlife specified in Schedule 3; but no such species of wildlife shall be kept in captivity without a licence issued by the Minister under section 10(1).

(2) Any person who keeps in captivity any species of wildlife listed in Schedule 3 without having obtained a licence commits an offence under this Act.

### 19. Action to protect private property

Where protected wildlife is alleged to be causing damage to any crops, land or other private property, the owner or occupier thereof may report the same to the Minister who shall expeditiously take such action as may be considered necessary, including the granting of any licence, to kill any such wildlife.

### 20. Powers of officers to search and seize

(1) If any officer has reasonable grounds for suspecting that any person has contravened any of the provisions of this Act he or she may—

(a) stop and search any such person and any vehicle, boat or other conveyance in the possession of such person or in which such person happens to be, and open and search any baggage or other thing in his or her possession;

(b) enter and search any tent or land in the occupation of any such person;

(c) under the authority of a warrant issued by a magistrate, enter and search any building by the occupation of any such person, and seize any protected or partially protected wildlife in the possession of any such person;

(d) seize all guns, dogs, boats, vehicles or other equipment which he or she may have cause reasonably to suspect to have been used by such person in connection with any such contravention.

(2) When any person is convicted of an offence under this Act, any protected or partially protected wildlife or the nest or eggs thereof or any gun, weapons, dog, boat, vehicle or other article in respect of or by means of which the offence was committed shall be forfeited to the Crown. Such forfeiture may be in addition to any other penalty to which such person is liable under this Act.

### 21. Arrest of person found committing an offence

(1) Where any person is found committing an offence against this Act, any officer may require any such person to give his or her name and place of abode; and may arrest any such person if—

(a) such person refuses to give his or her name or place of abode to the satisfaction of the officer; or

(b) gives a name or place of abode which is false.

(2) Any person required as aforesaid to give his or her name or place of abode who refuses to do so or who gives a name or place of abode which is false commits an offence.

### 22. Assault or resisting officer

Any person who assaults, hinders, obstructs or resists an officer in the execution of his or her duty under this Act commits an offence.

### 23. Complaints procedure

(1) All complaints for offences against this Act shall be laid in the name of an officer.

(2) Where an officer makes a complaint against another person for an offence under this Act any other officer may appear on his or her behalf before the court hearing the said complaint; and shall have the same privileges as to addressing the court and as to examining witnesses as if he or she were the complainant.

(3) Any police officer may conduct the prosecution of any complaint under this Act although such complaint was not laid by a police officer.

(4) In any proceedings before the court the statement of an officer that the species in issue is one of the species of wildlife listed in any of the Schedules shall be prima facie evidence of that fact.

### 24. Jurisdiction

(1) All offences committed against this Act shall be tried and punishable by a court.

(2) Where an offence against this Act is committed on the sea coast or at sea or within the territorial waters of Saint Lucia, it may be tried by a court in any district abutting such sea coast or adjoining such sea.

(3) Where any offence against this Act is committed in or, from any water forming the boundary between any 2 courts it may be tried in either of such courts.

### 25. Penalties

Any person who commits an offence under this Act is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $5,000 or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding 12 months.

### 26. Provisions to make statutory instruments

(1) The Minister may by statutory instrument—

(a) prescribe the scale of fees payable for licenses or permits issued under this Act;

(b) make provision for the control and administration of any wildlife reserve;

(c) make provision for the protection or control of any species of wildlife listed in any of the Schedules to this Act;

(d) do all such other things as may be necessary for the fulfilment of the purposes of this Act.

(2) A statutory instrument made under the provisions of the preceding subsection may provide for the imposition of a fine not exceeding $250 or imprisonment for 3 months for contravention of any of the provisions thereof on summary conviction therefor.

### 27. Powers of Minister

(1) The Minister may, by writing under his or her hand, empower an officer—

(a) to accept from any person who has admitted in writing or in the presence of at least one witness not being an Officer that he or she has committed an offence against this Act, a sum of money not exceeding $500 by way of compensation for such offence;

(b) when any property has been seized under this Act, to release the same on payment of the value thereof as estimated by such officer.

(2) On payment of such sum of money or such value, or both, as the case may be, to such officer, the person so admitting the commission of such offence, if in custody, shall be discharged, the property, if any, shall be released, and no further proceedings shall be taken against such person or property in respect of such offence.

(3) All monies received by such officer under this section shall be paid promptly into the Treasury by the officer receiving the same.
