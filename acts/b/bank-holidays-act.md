### AN ACT to make provision for bank holidays, and with respect to obligations to do certain acts on such days.

Commencement [1 April 1874]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Bank Holidays Act.

### 2. Bank Holidays

The several days mentioned in the Schedule, and which days are in this Act referred to as bank holidays shall in every year be kept as close holidays in all banks in Saint Lucia.

### 3. Bills of exchange due on Bank Holiday – when payable

All bills of exchange and promissory notes which are due and payable on any bank holiday are payable, and when necessary, in case of non-payment may be noted and protested, on the day next following such bank holiday; and any such noting or protest are as valid as if made on the day on which the bill or note was made due and payable.

### 4. Notice of dishonour or presentment of bill when Bank Holiday intervenes

When the day on which any notice of dishonour of an unpaid bill of exchange or promissory note should be given, or when the day on which a bill of exchange or promissory note should be presented or received for acceptance, or accepted, or forwarded to any referee or referees, is a bank holiday, such notice of dishonour shall be given, and such bill of exchange or promissory note shall be presented or forwarded, on the day next following such bank holiday.

### 5. Provisions where payment due on Bank Holiday

A person is not required to make any payment or to do any act in connection with any bank upon any bank holiday; and the obligation to make such payment or to do such act shall apply to the day next following such bank holiday; and the making of such payment or the doing of such act on such following day is equivalent to payment of the money or performance of the act on the holiday.

### 6. Provisions where day after Bank Holiday is a Sunday

When the day following any bank holiday is a Sunday, the Monday next following is, for the purposes of this Act, considered the next following day to such bank holiday.

### 7. Governor may alter Bank Holiday

The Governor General may, whenever it appears to him or her that in any year it is inexpedient that a day by this Act appointed for a bank holiday should be a bank holiday, declare by proclamation that such day shall not in such year be a bank holiday, and appoint such other day as to the Governor General may seem fit to be a bank holiday instead of such day, and thereupon the day so appointed shall in such year be substituted for the day so appointed by this Act.

### 8. Holidays to be observed at Public Offices

All bank holidays shall be kept as public holidays at the several public offices.
