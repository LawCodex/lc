### AN ACT to repeal and replace the Mid-Missions Incorporation Ordinance, to give effect to a change in the name of the Mid-Missions and to clarify the relationship between the Mid-Missions and indigenous New Testament Baptist Churches in Saint Lucia.

Commencement [16 March 1983]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Baptist Mid-Missions Incorporation Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires –

“**Baptist Mid-Missions**” means the Baptist Mid-Missions Incorporated, comprising the General Council of Co-operating Baptist Missions of North America, a non-profit, religious, educational and charitable organisation, composed of the New Testament Baptist Churches in Saint Lucia and its Dependencies;

“**The General Council**” means the General Council of Baptist Mid-Missions.

### 3. Incorporation of Trustees

(1) The Body Corporate which was created by subsection (1) of section 3 of the Mid-Missions Incorporation Ordinance by the name of “The Incorporated Trustees of the Mid-Missions in Saint Lucia” shall continue to exist and from and after the commencement of this Act, shall bear the name “The Incorporated Trustees of Baptist Mid-Missions in Saint Lucia.”

(2) The persons who at the commencement of this Act are registered under subsection (2) of section 3 of the said Ordinance as trustees of the Mid-Missions in Saint Lucia shall be deemed to be known as “The Incorporated Trustees of the Baptist Mid-Missions in Saint Lucia” (hereinafter referred to as “the Trustees”) and by that name, they and their successors in office shall have perpetual succession and shall be capable to sue and be sued in all Courts of Justice in Saint Lucia and shall have and use a common seal which may from time to time be altered by the General Council. Any 2 of the persons registered as trustees may act on behalf of the Trustees in the event of the death, incapacity or absence from the State of others.

(3) The names and addresses of the persons appointed from time to time to be trustees of Baptist Mid-Missions in Saint Lucia shall be notified to the Registrar of Companies and registered in his office.

### 4. Trustees may acquire property

The Trustees shall, subject to the rules, regulations and control of the General Council, have power to acquire for Baptist Mid-Missions by purchase, exchange, git, bequest, lease or otherwise all property movable or immovable as owners or for any other estate or interest therein: and, subject to such rules, regulations and control as aforesaid to take hold and enjoy the same and subject also to the performance of any trust upon which the same may have been acquired.

### 5. Vesting of property

All property movable and immovable now vested in the Incorporated Trustees of the Mid-Missions in Saint Lucia shall be and the same is hereby vested in the Incorporated Trustees of Baptist Mid-Missions in Saint Lucia.

### 6. Trustees may dispose of property

It shall be lawful for the Trustees, subject to such rules, regulations and control as aforesaid and with the consent of Cabinet from time to time by deed under their common seal to sell, lease, grant, convey, mortgage, exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or any other property by this Act vested, or which may hereafter be vested in the Trustees upon such terms as the Trustees with consent of the General Council may see fit.

### 7. Transfer of property to indigenous churches

Where an indigenous New Testament Baptist Church has been established in Saint Lucia before or after the commencement of this Act and the said Church has fulfilled the requirements prescribed by Baptist Mid-Missions for becoming independent and the Baptist Mid-Missions has formally accepted the said fellowship, the Trustees shall at the request of the said Church forthwith transfer convey and deliver to the said Church all property movable and immovable which the Trustees may have acquired in their name in trust for the said church or may otherwise hold as trustees for the said Church.

### 8. Borrowing power of trustees

The Trustees shall have power to borrow money upon the security of any property held by them.

Provided that any mortgage or hypothecary obligation shall be made under the common seal of the Trustees.

### 9. Saving of crown and other rights

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or Successors or of any body politic or corporate or of any other persons except such are mentioned or referred to in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
