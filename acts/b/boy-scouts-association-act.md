### AN ACT to further and protect the activities and interests of the Boy Scouts Association in Saint Lucia.

Commencement [15 November 1919]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Boy Scouts Association Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**Association**” means the Boy Scouts Association incorporated under the Royal Charter granted on the 4 July 1912;

“**Boy Scout**” means a Boy Scout recognized as such under the constitution, by-laws, or rules of the Association, and includes also all officers of the Association.

### 3. Restriction on use of uniform and Boy Scouts Association

It shall not be lawful for any person, not being under the by-laws or rules of the Association duly authorised and entitled so to do, publicly to wear, carry or bear any uniform, badge, token or emblem which under the said by-laws or rules is specifically adopted for use under the authority of the Association in such style or manner as to convey an impression that such person is under the said by-laws or rules entitled so to wear, carry, or bear such uniform, badge, token or emblem.

### 4. Boy scout not to enforce or exercise undue authority

No Boy Scout shall seek or attempt by virtue of his wearing any uniform, badge, token or emblem of the Association, or uniform, badge, token, or emblem purporting or appearing to be such, to enforce or exercise authority otherwise than in accordance with and as authorised by the by-laws or rules of the Association.

### 5. No person falsely to claim connection with Boy Scouts Association

It shall not be lawful for any person to form, organise, or work in connection with, or be concerned in forming, organising, or working in connection with any corps or body which without due authority granted under the Royal Charter of the Association claims or purports to be Boy Scouts or otherwise to be connected with the Association or which holds itself out as, or passes itself off as Boy Scouts or as otherwise connected with the Association.

### 6. Penalties

Any person contravening any of the provisions of this Act commits an offence under this Act and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $50 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months.
