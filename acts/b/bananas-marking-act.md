### AN ACT to provide for the marking of bananas for the purposes of identification and for other connected matters.

Commencement [11 April 1959]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Bananas (Marking) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**Association**” means the Saint Lucia Banana Growers Association Limited;

“**bananas**” means bananas of the varieties known as “Puerto Rique” “Gros Michel”, “Lacatan” and “Poyo” – or any other variety of bananas declared by the Governor General;

“**identification mark**” means an identification mark approved as part of a scheme under the Act;

“**Minister**” means the Minister for trade and production;

“**registered grower**” means a person registered as a banana grower under the Articles of the Association.

### 3. Preparation of Scheme for the allocation of identification marks

(1) The Association may, by resolution of its Board of Directors, and with the approval of the Minister, prepare a scheme for the allocation to registered growers of such marks (in this Act referred to as “identification marks”) as it may consider appropriate for the identification of bunches of bananas.

(2) When the Minister has approved a Scheme under this section, the Association shall cause particulars of the scheme and a notification of its approval to be published in the Gazette and at least one newspaper circulating in Saint Lucia.

### 4. Allocation of identification marks

(1) The Association shall, on the application of any registered grower, allocate to him or her an identification mark for use on bananas grown by him or her.

(2) Any registered grower applying for an identification mark shall furnish to the Association such information as to his or her banana cultivation as the Association may require.

### 5. Register of identification marks

The Association shall keep a register of identification marks and shall cause to be entered therein the following particulars in respect of every identification mark allocated to a registered grower—

(a) the name and residence of the registered grower to whom the identification mark is allocated;

(b) the description of the identification mark;

(c) the date of allocation;

(d) the situation of every portion of land whereon such registered grower grows bananas.

### 6. Penalty for unauthorised use of identification mark

(1) After the publication of a scheme under this Act, no person shall mark or cause to be marked any bananas with an identification mark unless he or she is authorised to do so by the Association.

(2) Any person who acts in contravention of this section commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $120 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months, or to both such fine and imprisonment.

### 7. Penalty for forging identification mark

Any person who—

(a) forges any identification mark;

(b) makes, disposes of, or has in his or her possession, any die, block, machine, or other instrument, for the purpose of forging an identification mark; or

(c) uses in connection with any article whatsoever, any mark of such a character or in such a manner as to be calculated, by reason of the resemblance of that mark to an identification mark, to deceive,

is liable on summary conviction, unless he or she proves that he or she acted without, intent to deceive, to a fine not exceeding $200 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 6 months, or to both such fine and imprisonment.

### 8. Power to make orders

The Governor General may by order to be published in the Gazette declare such other varieties of bananas to be bananas to which this Act applies. *(Substituted by Act 22 of 1959)*
