### AN ACT to provide for the protection and control of beaches and the seashore and for connected purposes.

Commencement [27 May 1967]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Beach Protection Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**authorised officer**” means any member of the Royal Saint Lucia Police Force established by the Police Act or other person duly appointed as such by the Chief Engineer by a notification published in the Gazette and in one or more newspapers circulating in Saint Lucia;

“**Chief Engineer**” means the person performing the duties of Chief Engineer of Saint Lucia;

“**the floor of the sea**” means the soil and subsoil of the sea off the coast of Saint Lucia between low water mark and so far out to sea as is deemed by international law to be within the territorial sovereignty of the Crown.

*(Amended by Act 9 of 1984)*

### 3. Appointment of authorised officers

The Chief Engineer may appoint authorised officers for and in respect of any quarter in Saint Lucia for the purpose of issuing permits under section 4.

### 4. Removal and unlawful possession of sand from the seashore prohibited

(1) A person shall not—

(a) dig, stockpile, take or carry away or aid or assist in digging, stockpiling, taking, or carrying away, any sand, stone, shingle or gravel from any beach, seashore or floor of the sea in Saint Lucia;

(b) convey or move any such sand, stone, shingle or gravel along any road; or

(c) possess, keep on his or her premises or under his or her control on any premises, any such sand, stone, shingle or gravel,

except under a written permit from the Chief Engineer or an authorised officer and subject to such terms and conditions as are specified in the permit.

(2) A person shall not receive or in any manner dispose of or assist in the disposal of any sand, stone, shingles or gravel, which he or she knows or reasonably suspects to have been obtained in contravention of subsection (1).

(3) Every permit granted under subsection (1) shall be in the form in the Schedule to this Act and shall have effect for a period not exceeding 3 months.

(4) A permit shall not be granted unless the Chief Engineer or an authorised officer is satisfied that the removal of such sand, stone, shingle or gravel is not likely to affect adversely the beach, the seashore or the floor of the sea.

(5) Any person who contravenes the provisions of subsection (1) and (2) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction—

(a) to a fine of $1,000 or treble the value of the sand, stone, shingle or gravel, whichever is greater, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one month or to both; and

(b) for a second or subsequent offence to a fine of $5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or to both; and in addition, that sand, stone, shingle or gravel shall be forfeited.

*(Substituted by 9 of 1984)*

### 5. Time when sand etc., may be conveyed on public road

(1) Sand, stone, shingle or gravel the digging or removal of which has been authorised by a permit as provided in section 4, shall not be conveyed or moved along any public road between the hours of 6:00 in the evening and 5:00 in the morning.

(2) Any person who contravenes the provisions of subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $50 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months.

### 6. Power of arrest

It is lawful for any person with or without a warrant to apprehend any person who contrary to the provisions of this Act is found—

(a) digging, stockpiling, taking or carrying away or aiding or assisting in digging stockpiling taking or carrying away any sand, stone, shingle or gravel in contravention of section 4(1)(a); or

(b) conveying or moving any sand, stone, shingle or gravel in contravention of section 4(1)(b),

and with all convenient speed to take him or her to the nearest police station to be dealt with.

*(Amended by Act 9 of 1984)*

### 7. Seizure and forfeiture of boats conveyances and animals

(1) All boats, conveyances and animals used for the removal of any sand, stone, shingle or gravel in contravention of section 4(1)(b) shall be forfeited.

(2) All boats, conveyances and animals liable to forfeiture under subsection (1) may at any time be seized by an authorised officer and shall be taken and delivered to the Chief Engineer who shall secure the same and after the expiration of 21 days cause the same to be sold by public auction to the highest bidder.

### 8. Owner may inform Chief Engineer that thing seized not liable to forfeiture

Where anything is seized as forfeited under this Act, the owner may within 14 days of the seizure by notice in writing inform the Chief Engineer that such thing is not liable to forfeiture and upon such written notice being given, the Chief Engineer shall postpone the sale until the matter is adjudicated upon by a magistrate.

### 9. Adjudication by Magistrate

In all cases where a notice is given to the Chief Engineer as aforesaid, the Chief Engineer within 7 days after such notice shall cause a complaint to be preferred for the adjudication of such forfeiture before a magistrate, and if it is made to appear by the owner to the satisfaction of the magistrate that such thing was not liable to forfeiture, the magistrate shall adjudge such thing to be restored, but if the magistrate is not satisfied, or if the owner fails to prove that such thing was not liable to forfeiture, the magistrate shall confirm the seizure and thereupon the same shall be sold as hereinbefore directed.

### 10. Authorised officer may enter into and search any premises, boat or conveyance

An authorised officer may enter into and search any premises, boat or conveyance which he or she reasonably suspects that sand, stone, shingle or gravel is kept in contravention of section 4(1)(b). *(Amended by Act 9 of 1984)*

### 11. Fouling of seashore prohibited

If any person fouls any part of the seashore of Saint Lucia by the deposit of offal, garbage or other waste, or in any other manner whatsoever, the person commits an offence and, on summary conviction thereof, is liable to a fine not exceeding $1,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months. *(Amended by Act 9 of 1984)*

### 12. Act binding on Crown

This Act is binding on the Crown.

### 13. The Queen's Chain not affected by this Act

It is hereby expressly declared that this Act and any permit issued or granted thereunder shall not in any way whatsoever affect and shall in all respects be subject to the exercise by Her Majesty, Her Heirs and Successors of their rights title and interest in and over that part of the domain of the Crown known as the Queen's Chain.
