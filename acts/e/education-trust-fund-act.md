### AN ACT to establish an Education Trust Fund for the purpose of providing financial assistance to students, schools and other educational institutions and for connected matters.

Commencement [14 March 1998]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Education Trust Fund Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

**“Board”** means the Board of Trustees appointed under section 4;

**“Fund”** means the Education Trust Fund;

“**Minister**” means the Minister responsible for education.

### 3. Establishment of Education Trust Fund

(1) There is hereby established an Education Trust Fund into which is to be paid all moneys received for and on behalf of the Fund.

(2) The Fund shall consist of—

(a) such moneys as may be provided by the Government for the use of the Fund;

(b) such moneys as may be raised by the Fund through contributions or donations by persons or groups— whether local, regional or international—or by fundraising activities;

(c) such other moneys from any other source as may be granted, loaned or allocated to the Fund.

### 4. Board of Trustees

(1) The Fund shall be administered by a Board of Trustees consisting of—

(a) a person representing the Saint Lucia Teachers Union;

(b) a person representing the Saint Lucia Principals Association;

(c) a person representing the National Youth Council and not less than 2 and not more than 4 other members, to be appointed by the Minister by instrument in writing.

(2) The chairperson shall be designated by the Minister.

(3) A member shall be appointed for such term, not exceeding 3 years as may be determined by the Minister and shall be eligible for re-appointment.

(4) A member may be removed by the Minister for inability or unwillingness to act or for impropriety in the discharge of his or her function as a member of the Board.

(5) Where a member of the Board appointed under subsection 1(a), (1)(b) or (1)(c) is removed in accordance with subsection (4), the Minister shall request that an alternative representative be nominated by the organisation which the member removed represented.

(6) A member, other than the chairperson, may at any time resign his or her office by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister and transmitted through the chairperson and from the date of receipt of the instrument by the Minister that person shall cease to be a member of the Board.

(7) Where a vacancy occurs in the membership of the Board, the Minister may where necessary, after consultation with the organisation which nominated the person for membership, appoint a person to act for the remainder of the term specified in the instrument of appointment of the person whose membership became vacant.

### 5. Power to grant assistance to students and others

The Board may, subject to the provisions of the Act and Regulations made thereunder, grant financial assistance to—

(a) a student, if in the opinion of the Board such student would be unable to commence or complete his or her education in the absence of such financial assistance; or

(b) a school or other educational institution approved by the Ministry of Education, where the Minister is satisfied that as a result of exceptional circumstances a grant should be made.

### 6. Patron of Trust Fund

The patron of the Trust Fund is the Governor General of Saint Lucia.

### 7. Audited financial statement to be submitted to the House of Assembly

(1) An account of the financial transactions of the Fund shall be prepared by the Board in respect of the period ending on the last day of the financial year of the Government of St. Lucia and transmitted to the Director of Audit for certification and report.

(2) Every such account after certification shall be submitted to the Minister who shall report thereon to the House of Assembly.

### 8. Deposit moneys belong to the Fund

All moneys received for and on behalf of the Fund shall be deposited with a bank approved by the Board in the name of the Fund and shall not be paid into the general revenue of the State.

### 9. Regulations

The Minister may make regulations for —

(a) the discharge by the Board of its functions and duties;

(b) the terms, conditions and circumstances under which financial assistance may be granted; and

(c) generally, for carrying out the objects and purposes of the Act.
