### AN ACT to impose an excise equalisation duty on certain goods and commodities produced or manufactured in the territories (other than Saint Lucia) comprising the East Caribbean Common Market and imported into Saint Lucia and for other related matters.

Commencement [16 June 1972]

### Whereas it is provided in effect by paragraph 1(a) of Article 8 of the Agreement establishing the East Caribbean Common Market that Member States may apply fiscal charges directly or indirectly to imported goods provided the said charges are not in excess of those applied directly or indirectly to like domestic goods, nor are such charges applied so as to afford effective protection to like domestic goods;

### A nd W hereas under the provision of section 3 of the Excise Act as amended duties of excise are charged, levied and collected on certain goods and commodities produced or manufactured in St. Lucia;

### A nd W hereas it is expedient to make provision for the imposition of an Excise Equalisation Duty in the case of certain goods and commodities produced or manufactured in, and imported into Saint Lucia from the territories of the East Caribbean Common Market.

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Excise Equalisation Duty Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Associated States**” means those territories which have assumed and which maintain a status of Association with the United Kingdom in accordance with the provisions of the West Indies Act, 1967 (Imperial) and includes the territories of Saint Vincent, and Montserrat;

“**Comptroller**” means the Comptroller of Customs and Excise;

“**market area**” means the Associated States;

“**Minister**” means the Minister to whom responsibility for Customs and Excise has been assigned.

### 3. Equalisation duty to be charged

(1) There shall be charged, collected and paid to the Accountant General on all goods and commodities enumerated in the Schedule being goods and commodities produced or manufactured in, and imported into Saint Lucia from, a territory in the market area an excise equalisation duty equivalent in amount to the aggregate of any duties of excise, and other charges levied and collected on goods and commodities of the like description produced or manufactured in Saint Lucia.

(2) Where goods and commodities to which this Act applies are imported into St. Lucia, or taken out of bond for consumption in Saint Lucia, the duty made payable under subsection (1) shall be paid by the importer of such goods and commodities at the time of their importation or, as the case may be, at the time they are taken out of bond for consumption.

### 4. Collection of duty

(1) The Comptroller is responsible for collecting the duty, and the officers of the Customs and Excise Department, other than the Comptroller, shall perform such duties in connection with the collection of the duty as may be assigned to them by the Comptroller.

(2) All money and securities for money collected for or on account of the duty shall, as soon as practicable be paid by the Comptroller to the Accountant General for the benefit of the general revenue of the State.

### 5. Power to amend Schedule

The Cabinet may by order amend the Schedule by adding or deleting any goods or commodities or by imposing any conditions as to the Cabinet seem fit.

However, any order made by the Cabinet under this section shall be submitted to the House of Assembly, and the House of Assembly may confirm, amend or revoke such order.

### 6. Power to remit or refund duty

(1) The Cabinet may, upon application made by the importer of any goods or commodities to which this Act applies, remit or refund in whole or in part any duty payable or paid under this Act if the Cabinet is satisfied that it is just and equitable so to do.

(2) The remission or refund authorised to be made under subsection (1) may apply to specific instances or generally.

### 7. Goods entered for transhipment or intransit exempt

Goods and commodities which are entered for transhipment or intransit through Saint Lucia in accordance with the provisions of the Customs (Control and Management) Act as amended, or any rules or regulations made thereunder shall be exempt from the payment of any duty made payable under this Act.

### 8. Value of goods and commodities

Where the duty made payable under this Act is payable by reference to the value of goods and commodities, the provisions of section 34 of the Customs (Control and Management) Act, shall have effect, with the necessary modifications, for ascertaining the value of such goods and commodities.

### 9. Recovery by action

The duty authorised to be collected under section 3 may be sued for and recovered as debt due to the Crown and may be recovered with full costs of suit from the person liable to make payment.

### 10. Offences and penalties

(1) Any person who with intent to deceive or to evade any of the provisions of this Act makes, produces, or furnishes, or causes to be made, produced or furnished or otherwise makes use of any book, account, estimate, return or other document which is false in a material particular is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $500.

(2) If any person in furnishing any information for the purposes of this Act or any rules made thereunder makes any statement which he knows to be false in a material particular, or recklessly makes any statement which is false in a material particular, he or she is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $500.

### 11. Power to make rules

The Minister may make rules for the purpose of giving effect to the provisions of this Act.

## Schedule

Alcoholic spirits of any description, whether completely distilled or otherwise, including all liquors mixed with or containing spirits, and all mixtures and preparations made with and containing spirits.
