### AN ACT to make provision for the licensing of yachts plying for hire in the territorial waters of Saint Lucia and for connected matters.

Commencement [1 January 1971]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Yachts Licence Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act “**territorial waters**” mean the waters surrounding the island of Saint Lucia and within 3 miles of any part of it.

### 3. Licence fee payable in respect of yacht plying for hire

(1) A licence fee in accordance with the provisions of section 4 is payable in respect of any yacht which may at any time ply for hire in the territorial waters.

(2) Subject to the provisions of this Act a yacht plies for hire in the territorial waters where it—

(a) calls at any part of Saint Lucia in the course of a journey;

(b) takes up or puts down passengers at any part in Saint Lucia; or

(c) where there is any carriage for reward or any other use on hire or charter into or out of any part of Saint Lucia.

(3) The licence fee is payable to the port officer by the owner or charterer or hirer or any other person in charge or command of the yacht, and it is the duty of the port officer to pay all licence fees received into the Consolidated Fund.

### 4. Licence fee

(1) Subject to subsection (2), the licence fees payable under section 3 are as prescribed in Schedule 1.

(2) The licence fees listed in Schedule 1 do not apply to fees charged by the Soufrière Marine Management Authority.

*(Substituted by Act 33 of 2001)*

### 5. Liability for payment of fee on demand

Any owner, charterer, hirer, or the master or other person in command or control, of a yacht in respect of which licence fee is not paid, who fails to pay the same on demand either orally or in writing by the port officer or by any other person duly authorised in writing by the port officer commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $500 and to be ordered to pay the licence fee.

### 6. Power for Port Officer to seize and detain

The port officer or any person acting under authority given by him or her may with such assistance and force as may reasonably be considered necessary, seize any yacht to be found anywhere in Saint Lucia including its territorial waters, if he or she has reasonable suspicion that any licence fee is payable under this Act and remains unpaid in respect of such yacht, and he or she may detain such yacht until he or she is satisfied either that no licence fee is payable or that any licence fee payable has been paid.

### 7. Yacht not to leave place at which it is stationary without certificate of compliance

A yacht to which this Act applies shall not leave any place at which it is anchored or otherwise stationary, unless the master or person in charge or command is in possession of a certificate of compliance issued by a port officer.

### 8. Breach of section 7 an offence

The master or other person in command or in charge of any yacht which leaves the place of which it is anchored or otherwise stationary in contravention of section 7 commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $500.

### 9. Certificate of Compliance to be in form set out in Schedule

The certificate of compliance referred to in section 7 which a port officer shall issue on application, and on being satisfied that licence fee in respect of the period then current has been paid in accordance with this Act, shall be in the form set out in Schedule 2.

### 10. Burden of proof

This Act does not apply to any pleasure yacht in relation to its use solely for the personal or domestic purposes of the owner, but in any proceedings under this Act, the burden of proof that this Act does not apply to a yacht, shall lie upon the owner, charterer or hirer of that yacht, or upon the master or other person in command or charge of the same.
