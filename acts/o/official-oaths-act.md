### AN ACT to make provision with respect to official oaths.

Commencement [29 August 1879]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Official Oaths Act.

### 2. Forms of Oaths

(1) The several oaths required to be taken under this Act shall be in the Forms set out in Schedule 1.

(2) Cabinet may, by order in the Gazette, amend Schedules 1 and 2.

*(Amended by Act 22 of 2003)*

### 3. Persons to take and tender Oaths

The several oaths set out in the first column of Schedule 2 shall be taken by the respective persons named in the second column of the said Schedule as soon as may be after their appointment to or acceptance of office, and shall be tendered by the respective persons named in the third column of the said Schedule.

### 4. Consequence of Officer refusing or neglecting to take Oath

If any officer mentioned in the second column of Schedule 2 refuses or neglects, when any oath required to be taken by him or her under this Act is duly tendered, to take such oath, he or she shall, if he or she has already entered on his or her office, vacate the same, and if he or she has not entered on his or her office, be disqualified from entering on the same; but a person shall not be compelled, in respect of the same appointment to the same office, to take such oath more times than one.

### 5. Cases in which Affirmation is allowed in lieu of Oath

Where an oath is required to be taken under this Act, every person by law permitted to make a solemn affirmation or declaration instead of taking an oath may, instead of taking such oath, make and subscribe a solemn affirmation in the form of the oath hereby appointed, substituting the words “solemnly and sincerely, declare and affirm” for the word “swear” and omitting the words “So help me God.”

### 6. Saving

This Act does not affect any other oath required or authorised by law to be taken or made, except that the form of Oath of Allegiance prescribed by this Act shall be used in Saint Lucia to the exclusion of all other forms.

## Schedule 1

(Section 2)

### FORMS OF OATHS

### OATH OF ALLEGIANCE

I, ..................., do swear that I will be faithful and bear true allegiance to Saint Lucia that I will uphold and defend the Constitution and the laws of Saint Lucia and that I will conscientiously and impartially discharge my responsibilities to the people of Saint Lucia. So help me God.

### OATH OF OFFICE

I, ..................., do swear that I will well and truly serve the people of Saint Lucia in the office of ................ So help me God.

### JUDICIAL OATH

I, ..................., do swear that I will be faithful and bear true allegiance to Saint Lucia that I will uphold and defend the Constitution of Saint Lucia and that I will administer justice to all persons alike in accordance with the laws and usages of Saint Lucia without fear or favour, affection or ill will. So help me God.

### OATH OF CABINET MINISTERS

I, ..................., do swear that I will be faithful and bear true allegiance to Saint Lucia, that as a Minister of the Government of Saint Lucia I will discharge my duties and perform my functions honestly, to the best of my ability, faithfully in accordance with the law, and always in the interest of the sovereignty, integrity, solidarity, well-being and prosperity of Saint Lucia and that I will not directly or indirectly communicate or reveal the business or proceedings of the Cabinet or the nature or contents of any document communicated to me as a Member of the Cabinet or any matter coming to my knowledge in my capacity as a Member of the Cabinet except as may be required for the due discharge of my duties as a Minister. So help me God.

### OATH OF CABINET SECRETARY

I, ..................., being called upon to exercise the functions of Cabinet Secretary in Saint Lucia, do swear that I will not directly or indirectly reveal the business or proceedings of or the nature or contents of any document communicated to me as Cabinet Secretary or any matter coming to my knowledge in my capacity as Cabinet Secretary, except as may be required for the due discharge of my duties as Cabinet Secretary. So help me God.

*(Substituted by Act 22 of 2003)*
