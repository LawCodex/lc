### AN ACT to enable the Governor General to make regulations with respect to the landing and storage of oil fuel.

Commencement [29 June 1926]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Oil Fuel (Landing and Storage) Act.

### 2. Definition

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**oil fuel**” includes all mineral oils, whether refined or unrefined, imported into Saint Lucia which have a flash point of not less than 150 degrees by Fahrenheit's thermometer when tested in the manner prescribed by the Petroleum Act for testing petroleum as defined by that Act; and

“**a store for oil fuel**” means any area specially appointed by the Governor General for the storage of oil fuel in bulk and includes all tanks, sheds and other buildings erected on the area in accordance with plans to be submitted and approved under any regulations made.

### 3. Regulations

(1) It is lawful for the Governor General to make regulations to control the landing and storage of oil fuel imported into Saint Lucia.

(2) All regulations purporting to be made under this Act shall be published in the Gazette and shall come into operation on such publication or at such other time as may be named in such regulations.

(3) A breach of any regulation made under this Act may on summary conviction be punished by a fine not exceeding $480 and in default of payment by imprisonment with or without hard labour for any period not exceeding 6 months.

(4) Regulations made under this Act shall be of the same effect as if they were contained in this Act and shall be judicially noticed.

(5) Any regulations made under this Act shall be laid before the House of Assembly as soon as may be after the making thereof; and if the House of Assembly by resolution disapprove of such regulations they shall thereupon be rescinded, but without prejudice to the validity of anything previously done thereunder or the making of new regulations.

### 4. Power to prevent discrimination

Without prejudice to the generality of the powers conferred on him or her by this Act, it is lawful for the Governor General in any regulations made under this Act to provide that permission to erect or to use a store for oil fuel shall be conditional on a sufficient guarantee being given that no discrimination will be exercised by the owner or occupier of such store for oil fuel or by any of his or her servants in the supply of oil fuel in favour or to the prejudice of any particular person, firm, company or corporation.
