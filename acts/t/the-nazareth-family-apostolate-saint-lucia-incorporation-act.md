### AN ACT to provide for the Incorporation of The Nazareth Family Apostolate (Saint Lucia).

Commencement [23 October 1993]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as The Nazareth Family Apostolate (Saint Lucia) (Incorporation) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Corporation**” or “**Apostolate**” means the Nazareth Family Apostolate (Saint Lucia);

“**Registrar**” means the Registrar of Companies.

### 3. Incorporation of The Nazareth Family Apostolate (Saint Lucia)

The following members of the Apostolate are hereby declared and appointed a corporation or body corporate with perpetual succession by the name of The Nazareth Family Apostolate (Saint Lucia) and possessed of a corporate seal and by that name may sue and be sued in all Courts in Saint Lucia—

(a) the Co-ordinator;

(b) two Directors;

their successors-in-office whose names and addresses shall be notified to the Registrar in the manner provided by section 4.

### 4. Notification of names and addresses of the officers to registrar

(1) The names and addresses of the persons holding offices referred to in section 3 shall be notified from time to time by the Co-ordinator to the Registrar and registered in his or her office and be subject to the provisions of Article 236 of the Commercial Code.

(2) A person last registered in the manner provided by subsection (1) shall be deemed to be a member of the Apostolate authorised to execute any document under the provisions of section 7.

### 5. Acts not invalidated by defects in appointment of officers, notification and registration

No act or proceeding of the Corporation shall be invalidated by reason of any defect in the appointment of any of the offices mentioned in section 3 or by reason of any defect in the notification or in the registration required by subsection (1) of section 4.

### 6. Corporate seal

The Seal of the Corporation shall be of such design and pattern as the Corporation may determine.

### 7. Execution of documents

No deed or document purporting to be executed by the Corporation shall be of any force or validity unless it is sealed with the Corporate Seal and signed by not fewer than 2 members of the Corporation described in section 3.

### 8. Powers of Corporation

The Corporation shall have the power—

(a) to acquire, hold, purchase, lease, possess and enjoy any lands and hereditaments whatsoever absolutely or for leasehold or for any other estate or interest therein and all other property real or personal;

(b) to give, grant, let, charge, improve, manage, develop, cultivate, farm, breed, and raise livestock, exchange, lease, mortgage, sell, convey, assign, dispose of, turn to account or otherwise deal with all or any property both present and future, or any part thereof held by or vested in the Corporation;

(c) to borrow, raise or secure the payment of money in such manner as may be thought fit and in particular by the issue of debentures or script charged upon all or any of the property, both present and future, held by or vested in the Corporation, and to redeem and pay off any such securities; and

(d) to appoint a person as the Attorney of the Corporation either generally or for a limited period and for such purposes and with such powers as may be stated in a power of attorney, and to revoke any such appointment.

### 9. Exemption

The Corporation shall be exempt from the provisions of the Aliens (Landholding Regulation) as amended and from the payment of stamp duty in respect of the acquisition by gift, purchase or lease of lands and hereditaments to be used by the Apostolate or the disposal thereof.

### 10. Savings

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect or be deemed to prejudice or affect the right of Her Majesty The Queen, Her Heirs and Successors, or of any body politic or corporate or of any other person and those claiming by, from or under them, except such as are mentioned in this Act.
