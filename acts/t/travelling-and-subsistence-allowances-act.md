### AN ACT to authorise the grant of subsistence allowance to public officers when travelling on duty and to persons other than public officers travelling on Government business, and to make provision for the payment of their transport expenses.

Commencement [1 January 1946]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Travelling and Subsistence Allowances Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“**Officer**” means every person permanently or temporarily employed in the public service of Saint Lucia and includes any officer of the Windward Islands;

“**salary**” includes all pensionable emoluments;

“**station**” means any place, district or parish in which an officer is for the time being resident in the performance of the duties of his or her office;

“travelling” includes an officer's stay at any place other than his or her station, but only when such stay has been unavoidable or otherwise necessary in the interest of the public service; and if any question shall arise as to whether such stay was unavoidable or necessary the decision of the Governor General thereon shall be final.

### 3. Transport

(1) Officers when travelling on duty may be furnished with reasonable transport at the public expense.

(2) Cabinet may make regulations prescribing the nature and conditions of the transport to be allowed under this Act.

(3) Subject to the provisions of this Act and to any regulations made thereunder, if any question arises as to the reasonableness of the transport claimed by any officer under this section, the Governor General shall decide upon such claim and his or her decision shall be final.

### 4. Transport allowance

(1) The Governor General, whenever he or she may deem it necessary, may require sufficient means of transport to be kept by any officer for the performance of his or her duties, and shall prescribe the allowance to which any such officer shall be entitled for the upkeep of such means of transport: Provided always, that whenever such means of transport shall not be available for travelling in and for the performance of such duties, such allowance shall cease to be payable.

(2) Every officer entitled to such allowance shall, before payment thereof, furnish a certificate that he or she has kept the requisite means of transport for use in the performance of his or her duties.

### 5. Subsistence allowance

Officers whilst properly travelling and absent from their stations on the public service shall, subject to the provisions of this Act and any regulations made thereunder, when the Governor General is satisfied that they have used due diligence and despatch in the performance of such service, be entitled to receive for such travelling and absence a subsistence allowance in accordance with regulations to be made by Cabinet: Provided that 1/3 only of such allowance shall be payable to any officer for any period exceeding one day spent travelling on duty on any ship or plane when the officer's passage, inclusive of his or her board, shall have been provided from public funds.

### 6. Transfer expenses

When an Officer is transferred, whether permanently or temporarily, from one station to another or from one country of the Windward Islands to another and the circumstances are such that no allowance is payable to such officer, it shall be lawful for the Governor General to authorise the repayment to such officer of any reasonable out-of-pocket expenses which he or she may necessarily have incurred in connection with such transfer.

### 7. Special allowance

When an officer is employed in travelling on any service in which it is necessary for him or her to incur more than ordinary expenses it shall be lawful for the Governor General in his or her discretion, and if he or she considers it reasonable in the circumstances, to authorise the payment to such officer of such expenses.

### 8. Allowances not to count for pension

No allowance made under this Act, or any Act amending the same, shall count for pension under the Pensions Act, in the case of any present or future officer.

### 9. Chief Justice and Puisne Judges

The Chief Justice and the Puisne Judges of the Supreme Court of the Windward Islands and Leeward Islands shall receive such travelling and subsistence allowances when travelling from their stations on duty as the Governor General, in consultation with the Governor General of the Leeward Islands, shall determine.

### 10. Subsistence allowance to person other than public officers

(1) Any person other than an officer within the meaning of this Act, travelling on the public service on the written authority of the Governor General and absent from his or her normal place of residence on such service, shall be entitled to receive for such travelling and absence, a subsistence allowance in accordance with the Regulations to be made by Cabinet under this Act.

(2) Cabinet shall decide, for the purpose of this section, the place to be regarded as the normal place of residence of any person claiming subsistence allowance by virtue of this section.
