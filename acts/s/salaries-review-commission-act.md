### AN ACT to establish a Salaries Review Commission to review the salaries and other conditions of service of the holders of offices mentioned in the Schedule.

Commencement [27 April 1998]

### 1. Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Salaries Review Commission Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Commission**” means the Salaries Review Commission established under section 4;

“**incumbent**” means the holder of an office mentioned in the Schedule;

“**Minister**” means the Minister to whom responsibility for Finance has been assigned;

“**recommendations**” means the recommendations of the Commission contained in the report;

“**report**” means the report of the Commission concerning any review of salaries and other conditions of service of incumbents.

### 3. Establishment of Commission

There is hereby established for the purposes of this Act a Salaries Review Commission which shall consist of a Chairperson and 5 other members all of whom shall be appointed by the Governor General.

### 4. Qualifications of members

(1) The Chairperson shall be appointed from among persons qualified and experienced in the fields of industrial relations, business management, economics, accountancy, law, medicine or any other professional field, of not less than 10 years standing.

(2) The other members of the Commission shall comprise the following persons—

(a) a representative of the Government nominated by the Prime Minister;

(b) a representative of the opposition nominated by the Leader of the Opposition;

(c) a representative of the private sector nominated by the Prime Minister after consultation with the appropriate bodies in the private sector;

(d) a person who in the opinion of the Governor General is qualified to represent the general public; and

(e) a representative of a Public Service Union appointed under section 5(2).

### 5. Tenure of office

(1) Subject to subsection (2), a member shall be appointed for a term, not exceeding 3 years, and on such conditions, as may be determined by the Governor General at the time of his or her appointment and shall be eligible for re-appointment.

(2) A member representing a Public Service Union shall be appointed for 2 years in the first instance on the basis of rotation and shall be eligible for re-appointment if there is no representative of another Public Service Union who is willing to serve as a member of the Commission for that period.

### 6. Temporary appointment

Where a member is, for any reason, unable to act, the Governor General may, after consultation with the person who nominated that member, appoint another person, qualified under section 4(1), to act temporarily in that member's place.

### 7. Resignation of members

(1) A member, other than the Chairperson, may at any time resign his or her office by instrument in writing addressed to the Governor General and transmitted through the Chairperson and from the date of receipt of the instrument by the Governor General that person ceases to be a member of the Commission.

(2) The Chairperson may at any time resign his or her office by instrument in writing addressed to the Governor General and from the date of receipt of the instrument by the Governor General that person ceases to be Chairperson and a member of the Commission.

### 8. Filling of vacancies

(1) Where a vacancy occurs in the membership of the Commission, the Governor General may where necessary, after consultation with the person who nominated that member appoint a person, qualified under section 4(1), to act for the remainder of the term specified in the instrument of appointment of the member whose membership became vacant.

(2) A vacancy in the membership of the Commission occurs—

(a) on the death, resignation or revocation of the appointment, of a member;

(b) on the expiration of the term specified in a member's instrument of appointment; or

(c) on the failure of a member to attend 3 consecutive meetings of the Commission without reasonable cause.

### 9. Revocation of appointment

The Governor General may, at the request of the person who nominated a representative for appointment as a member of the Commission, at any time by instrument in writing, revoke the appointment of that member.

### 10. Allowances

Members of the Commission shall be eligible for such remuneration whether by way of fees, travelling or other allowances as the Governor General may determine.

### 11. Names of members to be published in Gazette

The names of all members of the Commission as at first constituted and every change in the membership thereof shall be published in the Gazette.

### 12. Procedure and meetings of Commission

(1) The Commission shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient for the transaction of its business and each meeting shall be held on the days and at the place and time that the Commission determines.

(2) The Chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the Commission and in the absence of the Chairperson the members present and constituting a quorum shall elect a temporary Chairperson from among their members who shall preside at that meeting.

(3) The quorum of the Commission at any meeting shall be the Chairperson and 3 other members.

(4) Subject to this section, the Commission shall regulate its own procedure.

### 13. Secretary to the Commission

The Secretariat to the Commission shall be the Ministry of the Public Service, Personnel Division, and the Chief Personnel Officer shall act as Secretary to the Commission.

### 14. Functions of the Commission

(1) The Commission shall with the approval of the Governor General review the salaries and other conditions of service of the holders of the offices mentioned in the Schedule.

(2) Despite subsection (1), the Commission may review the salaries and other conditions of service of incumbents—

(a) immediately following the review of salaries and other conditions of service of officers in the public sector;

(b) at any such time at the request of the Prime Minister.

(3) The Minister may on the recommendation of the Cabinet by order amend the Schedule.

### 15. Criteria for reviewing salaries, etc.

(1) The Commission in its review shall consider the following guidelines—

(a) the necessity to attract, recruit and retain persons of suitable competence, experience, knowledge, skills and personal attributes in administration, management and entrepreneurship to fill positions of the highest responsibility and trust in the State;

(b) a fair comparison with current levels of remuneration paid outside of the public sector for broadly comparable jobs, taking into account differences in other conditions of employment;

(c) the need to ensure appropriate differentials in compensation to take account of significant differences in the levels of responsibility between one officer and another; and

(d) the implications of salary revisions for those office-holders on the pay structures of the Public Service and on compensation levels in the economy as a whole.

(2) The Commission in its review shall take into consideration the following—

(a) percentage increases granted to public officers, public servants and other Crown servants;

(b) general responsibility of members of Parliament in relation to their constituencies and the portfolios assigned to them;

(c) the levels of salaries and other terms and conditions of service of persons holding senior managerial positions in the private sector and statutory corporations;

(d) levels of salaries and other terms and conditions of service of members of Parliament in other Commonwealth countries within the Caribbean;

(e) the rise in the cost of living since the last review; and

(f) the period of time which elapsed between the last review and the current review.

(3) In addition to subsection (2), the Commission may invite incumbents to articulate their views either in writing or orally with respect to the salaries and other terms and conditions of service which should apply to them and to give consideration to such views.

### 16. Report of Commission

(1) Where the Commission reviews the salaries and other conditions of service of incumbents, the Commission shall embody its recommendation in a report.

(2) The report, in the case of a review under section 15(1), shall be submitted to the Governor General who shall forward a copy thereof to the Prime Minister, and, in the case of a review under section 15(2), the report shall be submitted to the Prime Minister, and in both these cases the Prime Minister shall present the report to the Cabinet and, subject to subsection (3), the report shall be laid, as soon as possible thereafter, on the table of the House of Assembly and of the Senate.

(3) When a report is presented to the Cabinet, the Cabinet may accept it, or refer it back to the Commission for further consideration.

(4) Any recommendations accepted by Cabinet shall be construed as an amendment to any existing contract or legislation with respect to salaries and other conditions of service of the holders of the offices mentioned in the report and any such existing contract or legislation shall be deemed to be amended accordingly.

### 17. Expenses of the Commission to be a charge on Consolidated Fund

All expenses incurred by the Commission for the purposes of this Act shall be a charge on the Consolidated Fund.
