### AN ACT respecting the statutes of Saint Lucia.

Commencement [6 May 1967]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Statutes Act.

### 2. Style of statutes

This Act and all statutes enacted by the Legislature of Saint Lucia and passed after the commencement of this Act shall be styled “Acts”.

### 3. Words of enactment and other clauses

(1) In every bill presented to the Governor General for assent, the words of enactment shall be as follows—

“BE IT ENACTED by The Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the House of Assembly of Saint Lucia, and by the authority of the same, as follows—”

(2) The words of enactment shall follow the preamble, if any, and the various clauses within the body of the statute shall be consecutively numbered and follow in a concise and enunciative form.

(3) To every clause in the Bill, there shall be annexed as a head note a short summary of its contents.

(4) The statutes of each year shall be distinguished by consecutive numbers, commencing in each year with the number one.

(5) As far as practicable, matters having no proper relation to each other shall not be provided for by the same statute; no statute shall contain anything foreign to what the title of the statute imports; and no provision having indefinite duration shall be included in any statute expressed to have limited duration.

*(Amended by Act 23 of 2000)*

### 4. Date of passing of statutes

(1) The date of the passing of every statute is the date on which the bill for that statute receives the assent of the Governor General.

(2) The clerk of the House of Assembly shall inscribe on every statute, immediately after its long title, the day, month and year when the statute comes into force or operation, or where the statute provides that it is to come into force or operation by proclamation, or that it is not to come into force or operation until a day or date to be so fixed, the words “On Proclamation”; and such inscription shall be taken to be part of the statute.

### 5. Operation of statutes

Where a statute provides that it is to come into force or operation on a day or date to be fixed by the Governor by proclamation, or that it is not to come into force or operation until a day or date to be so fixed, any such proclamation—

(a) may apply to the whole or any part or parts or portion or portions or section or sections of the statute; and

(b) may be issued at different times as to any part or parts or portion or portions or section or sections of the statute.

### 6. Printing and distribution

(1) The statutes shall be printed, published and distributed by the Government Printer.

(2) The clerk of the House of Assembly shall furnish the Government Printer with a certified copy of every statute of Parliament as soon as it has received the assent of the Governor General.

(3) Every copy of a statute printed by the Government Printer is evidence of that statute and its contents, and every copy purporting to be printed by the Government Printer is considered to have been so printed, unless the contrary is shown.

(4) As soon as practicable after the commencement of each year, the clerk of the House of Assembly shall cause a complete collection of all the statutes enacted in Saint Lucia during the preceding year to be bound and published for general information.

### 7. Recording of statutes and certification

(1) This Act and all statutes passed after the commencement of this Act shall be of record in the custody of the Clerk of the House of Assembly.

(2) Where the Clerk of the House of Assembly is required to certify a copy of a statute for any purpose he or she shall insert at the foot of each copy required to be certified a written certificate duly signed and authenticated by him or her to the effect that such copy is a true copy.

(3) Copies of statutes certified under subsection (2) by the clerk of the House of Assembly shall be held to be duplicate originals and also to be evidence of the statutes and of their contents as if printed by authority of law.
