# SAINT LUCIA DEVELOPMENT BANK (REPEAL) ACT

**(Act No. 10 of 2009)**

Act 10 of 2009 .. in force 12 October 2009 (Repeal of Act No. 22 of 1980)

ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| 1. | Short title |
| 2. | Repeal |

An Act to repeal the Saint Lucia Development Bank Act, Cap. 12.02.

Commencement [12 October 2009]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Saint Lucia Development Bank (Repeal) Act.

### 2. Repeal

The Saint Lucia Development Bank Act, Cap.12.02 is repealed.
