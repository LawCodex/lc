### AN ACT for the suppression of voluntary and extra-judicial oaths and affidavits.

Commencement [14 October 1887]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Statutory Declarations Act.

### 2. Restriction on administration of solemn affirmations

It is not lawful for any magistrate, justice of the peace, or other person to administer or cause or allow to be administered, or to receive or cause or allow to be received, any oath, affidavit, or solemn affirmation touching any matter or thing whereof such magistrate, justice, or other person has not jurisdiction or cognizance by some statute in force. However, nothing herein contained shall be construed to extend to any oath, affidavit, or solemn affirmation before any magistrate or justice in any matter or thing touching the preservation of the peace or the prosecution, trial, or punishment of offences, nor to any oath, affidavit, or affirmation which may be required by the laws of any country or place beyond Saint Lucia, to give validity to any instrument in writing designed to be used in such country or place.

### 3. Power to take voluntary declarations

In any case where confirmation of any written instrument or allegation, or the proof of any debt, or the execution of any deed or other matter is required, it is lawful for the judge, the Governor General, and any magistrate, justice of the peace, notary royal or other officer now by law authorised to administer an oath, to take the declaration of any person voluntarily making the same before him or her, in the Form contained in the Schedule.

### 4. Penalty for making false declaration

A person who makes a declaration according to this Act, containing any statement false in fact, which the person knows or believes to be false or does not believe to be true, commits a misdemeanour, and on conviction is liable to imprisonment, with or without hard labour, for any term not exceeding one year.

### 5. Mode of referring to declaration

A declaration made under this Act may be referred to in any Act, or in any official or legal document, as a statutory declaration.

### 6. Fee payable by declarant

Whenever any declaration is made and subscribed by any person according to this Act, a fee which would have been due and payable on the taking or making a legal oath, solemn affirmation, or affidavit is similarly due and payable upon making and subscribing such declaration.

### 7. Savings

(1) This Act shall not extend or apply to the oath of allegiance in any case in which the same is or may be required to be taken by any person; but such oath of allegiance shall continue to be required, and shall be administered and taken in the same manner as if this Act had not come into force.

(2) This Act shall not extend or apply to any oath, solemn affirmation, or affidavit which now is or hereafter may be made or taken, or be required to be made or taken, to verify and prove the signing, sealing, or delivering of any will, codicil, or other testamentary disposition or writing, or of any deed or other instrument in writing, before any person, in order to the due recording thereof in the office of the Registrar of Deeds and Mortgages or otherwise, nor to any oath, solemn affirmation, or affidavit which now is or hereafter may be made or taken, or be required to be made or taken, in any judicial proceeding in any court of justice, or in any proceeding before any magistrate or justice of the peace; but all such oaths, affirmations, and affidavits shall continue to be required, and shall be administered, taken, and made, in the same manner as if this Act had not come into force.

(3) This Act shall not extend or apply to any oath or solemn affirmation which now is or hereafter may be required to be made or taken under or by virtue of any commission or instruction of Her Majesty; but every such oath or affirmation shall continue to be required, and shall be administered and taken in the same manner as if this Act had not come into force.

(4) This Act shall not extend or apply to any oath for the due execution of any office or employment, or be deemed to interfere with the operation of the Statutory Declarations Act of the United Kingdom.
