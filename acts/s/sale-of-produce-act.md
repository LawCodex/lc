### An Act to amend and consolidate the law relating to the sale of certain agricultural produce.

Commencement [1 January 1947]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Sale of Produce Act.

## 2. Interpretation

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires—

“**cocoa**” means cocoa beans, whether in the pod or pulp or separated from the pulp, or whether cured or uncured, ripe or unripe;

“**coconuts**” means ripe or dry coconuts, husked or not husked, the kernel or copra and includes coconut oils;

“**coffee**” means coffee in pulp or cherry and raw coffee in the parchment but not raw, cleaned coffee;

“**district**” means a magisterial district;

“**licensable produce**” includes the agricultural produce set out in Schedule 1 and such other agricultural produce as the Governor General, by proclamation, may direct to be included in such term;

“**licensed dealer**” means a person licensed under this Act to deal in licensable produce;

“**licensed huckster**” means a person licensed under this Act to export licensable produce to Guyana and West Indian Islands other than Bermuda;

“**licensed pedlar**” means a person licensed under this Act to peddle licensable produce;

“**licensed seller**” means an owner of any plantation or land licensed under this Act to sell and export the licensable produce derived from such plantation or land;

“**limes**” means lime fruit whether ripe, green or pickled, lime juice and lime oils;

“**logwood**” means logwood deprived of bark and sapwood as prepared for commercial purposes;

“**owner**” includes the tenant or person in actual possession of, and the manager or other person having chief authority on, any plantation or land;

“**peddle**” means selling otherwise than in a building, but shall be deemed to include selling in any parlour or market. For the purpose of this section “parlour” means a small shop carrying a stock not exceeding $144 in value;

“**personal representative**” means the heir, executor, or other person duly authorised by law to administer the estate of a deceased person;

“**produce inspector**” means an officer appointed under the provisions of section 45;

“**purchase**” includes barter;

“**sell**” includes barter and offering or exposing for sale;

“**weigh**” includes measuring, counting and otherwise ascertaining quantity.

## PART 1 PRODUCE SELLERS

### 3. Licence to sell produce derived from plantation or land

(1) The owner of any plantation or land desiring to sell or export the licensable produce derived from such plantation or land shall first obtain a Produce Seller's Licence as hereinafter provided.

(2) Any such owner who sells or exports licensable produce derived from his or her plantation or land without having first obtained a Produce Seller's Licence in respect of such plantation or land commits an offence against this Act.

### 4. Application for Produce Seller's Licence

(1) Every person desiring to obtain a Produce Seller's Licence shall apply to the magistrate of the district in which the plantation or land is situate whereon the licensable produce which the applicant proposes to sell or export is growing.

(2) Such application shall—

(a) be in Form A set out in Schedule 2;

(b) state in full the name and address of the applicant and the locality and name if any of his or her plantation or land;

(c) state as near as shall be practicable the number of trees bearing the licensable produce which he or she seeks to sell;

(d) state whether the applicant holds such land as sole owner, part owner, lessee, manager or otherwise.

(3) An applicant whose application contains a statement false in any particular commits an offence.

### 5. Verification of application for Produce Seller's Licence

Upon receipt of such application the magistrate may refer the same to a produce inspector for verification of the statement therein contained.

### 6. Issue and form of Produce Seller's Licence

(1) Subject to the provisions of section 9 the magistrate shall, when satisfied that the statements contained in the application are correct, and upon receipt of the licence fee set out in schedule 3, issue to the applicant a Produce Seller's Licence in Form B set out in Schedule 2.

(2) Upon payment of a fee for each licence a Produce Seller's Licence may be granted to the applicant in respect of each of the items of licensable produce derived from his or her plantation or land.

(3) Such licence fee shall be paid by means of postage stamps which shall be affixed to the application and shall be cancelled, on grant of the licence, by the magistrate.

(4) A Produce Seller's Licence shall not be issued to an applicant in respect of licensable produce derived from the working of any plantation or land on the metayer or share system except with the written consent of the person who put him or her in possession.

### 7. Extent of Produce Seller's Licence

All or any of the applicant's plantations or lands situate in the district wherein the application is made may be included in a single application, and a single licence may be issued in respect of such plantations or lands, but no licence shall extend to plantations or lands situate in any district other than that in which such licence is issued. However, if any plantation or lot of land lies in 2 or more districts, a licence in respect of the whole of such plantation or lot of land may be issued in any one of such districts.

### 8. Effect of Produce Seller's Licence

(1) A Produce Seller's Licence shall be only in respect of the plantation or land stated thereon and shall entitle the licensee to—

(a) sell to a licensed dealer, licensed huckster or licensed pedlar licensable produce derived from such plantation or land; and

(b) export licensable produce derived from such plantation or land.

(2) Subject to section 42, and to the provisions of this Act relating to the sale and export of licensable produce by licensed pedlars, licensed hucksters and licensed dealers, any licensed seller who sells or exports licensable produce derived otherwise than from the plantation or land endorsed upon his or her Produce Seller's Licence commits an offence.

### 9. Persons under 16 year's of age

(1) A Produce Seller's Licence shall not be issued to any person who, in the opinion of the magistrate, is under the age of 16 years.

(2) A licence may be issued on his or her behalf to the guardian of such person under the age of 16 years, in which case—

(a) the name of the guardian shall be entered on the licence in addition to that of the person under the age of 16 years;

(b) all dealings in licensable produce under such licence shall be conducted by such guardian;

(c) such guardian is liable in respect of any offence committed against the provisions of this Act in connection with such licence as if he or she were the licensee.

### 10. Transfer of Produce Seller's Licence

If a licensed seller dies or parts with his or her property in the plantation or land set out in his or her licence, the magistrate shall, by endorsement thereon, transfer such licence to the person who is proved to his or her satisfaction to have acquired the property in such plantation or land.

### 11. Endorsement of sales on Produce Seller's Licence

(1) Immediately upon the disposal of any licensable produce the licensed seller shall cause to be entered upon his or her licence in a legible manner in Form C set out in Schedule 2 the following particulars—

(a) in the case of sale

(i) the date of delivery,

(ii) the name and address of the purchaser,

(iii) the description of the licensable produce sold,

(iv) the weight, measure or quantity of such licensable produce,

(v) the amount received by the licensed seller in respect of such sale;

(b) in the case of export

(i) the date of export,

(ii) the place to which, and the ship by which, the licensable produce is exported,

(iii) the description of licensable produce exported,

(iv) the weight, measure or quantity of such licensable produce.

(2) Every such entry made upon sale to a licensed dealer or a licensed huckster shall be checked and signed by the purchaser or his or her agent and every such entry made upon export shall be checked and signed by a Treasury officer.

In the case of export such check may be made at any time within 14 days of the date of export and the licensed seller shall produce his or her licence to the proper Treasury officer for such check to be made within the period aforesaid.

(3) A licensed seller commits an offence—

(a) who fails or neglects to make or cause to be made the entries required by this section;

(b) who fails or neglects to produce his or her licence to a Treasury officer as required by this section;

(c) on whose licence there shall be found any entry false in any particular;

(d) who enters or permits any entries to be made on his or her licence in respect of any licensable produce derived otherwise than from the plantation or land stated on such licence; or

(e) who sells or exports or has in his or her possession such quantity of licensable produce as, having regard to the quantity already disposed of and entered on such seller's licence, could not reasonably be supposed to be derived from the plantation or land stated on such licence.

(4) Any person who fails or neglects to check or sign an entry in a Produce Seller's Licence as required by this section commits an offence.

### 12. Power to inspect licences and search plantation of produce seller

(1) The Director of Finance and Planning or any person authorised by him or her or by any magistrate, any police officer, a produce inspector or any rural constable may—

(a) call for and inspect the licence of any licensed seller;

(b) take a copy of any entry in such licence;

(c) enter and examine the plantation or land of a licensed seller;

(d) examine and weigh any licensable produce in the possession of a licensed seller.

(2) A licensed seller commits an offence who—

(a) fails or neglects to produce such licence;

(b) refuses to allow a copy of any entry in any such licence to be taken;

(c) obstructs or resists the taking of such copy;

(d) obstructs or resists the entry and examination of such plantation or land; or

(e) fails or neglects to give and provide such assistance as may be necessary for the purpose of examining and weighing such licensable produce.

## PART 2 PRODUCE PEDLARS

### 13. Licence to peddle produce

(1) Any person not being a licensed dealer, desiring to peddle for domestic use any licensable produce shall first obtain a Produce Pedlar's Licence as hereinafter provided.

(2) Any person peddling licensable produce without having first obtained a Produce Pedlar's Licence commits an offence.

### 14. Application for Produce Pedlar's Licence

(1) Every person desiring to obtain a Produce Pedlar's Licence shall make application to the magistrate of the district wherein he or she desires to peddle licensable produce.

(2) Such application shall be in Form D set out in Schedule 2 and shall state in full the name and address of the applicant, and, if the applicant is a licensed seller or a licensed huckster, shall state the number of his or her Produce Seller's or Produce Huckster's Licence as the case may be.

### 15. Issue and Form of produce Pedlar's Licence

(1) Subject to the provisions of this section the magistrate shall, upon receipt of such application together with the licence fee set out in schedule 3 hereto, issue to the applicant a Produce Pedlar's Licence in Form E set out in Schedule 2. However, if the applicant is a licensed seller or a licensed huckster, such licence shall, upon production of his or her Produce Seller's or Produce Huckster's Licence as the case may be, be issued to the applicant free of charge.

(2) Such licence fee shall be paid by means of postage stamps which shall be affixed to the application and shall be cancelled, on grant of the licence, by the magistrate.

(3) No such licence shall be issued unless the applicant, in the opinion of the magistrate, has attained the age of 16 years.

(4) The magistrate may refuse to grant a licence if the applicant has within the previous 5 years suffered cancellation of his or her licence under the provisions of section 44.

### 16. Effect of Produce Pedlar's Licence

(1) A Produce Pedlar's Licence shall entitle the Licensee to—

(a) peddle licensable produce for domestic use only; and

(b) purchase licensable produce from a licensed seller or a licensed dealer for the purpose of peddling the same as aforesaid.

(2) A licensed pedlar who is also a licensed seller may peddle for domestic use licensable produce derived from the plantation or land stated on his or her Produce Seller's Licence, but shall, in such case, enter upon his or her Produce Seller's Licence the particulars required by section 11.

(3) Any licensed pedlar who purchases or sells licensable produce otherwise than in accordance with the provisions of this section commits an offence against this Act.

(4) Any person purchasing licensable produce from a licensed pedlar and using or attempting to use the same for any purpose other than domestic use commits an offence against this Act.

### 17. Licensed Pedlar to carry Licence

(1) Every licensed pedlar shall, while peddling or transporting any licensable produce, carry with him or her at all times his or her Produce Pedlar's Licence and shall expose the same to any person to whom he or she offers any such licensable produce for sale.

(2) Any licensed pedlar who fails or neglects to carry and expose such licence as aforesaid commits an offence against this Act.

### 18. Restrictions as to purchase

Any person purchasing licensable produce that is being peddled from any person other than a licensed pedlar commits an offence against this Act.

### 19. Power to Inspect licence and licensable produce carried by licensed pedlar

(1) The Director of Finance and Planning or any person authorised by him or her or by any magistrate, any police officer, a produce inspector or any rural constable may—

(a) call for and inspect the licence of any licensed pedlar; and

(b) examine and weigh any licensable produce in the possession of a licensed pedlar.

(2) Any licensed pedlar who—

(a) fails or neglects to produce such licence; or

(b) obstructs or resists the examination of any such licensable produce,

commits an offence against this Act.

## PART 3 PRODUCE HUCKSTERS

### 20. Produce Hucksters to be licensed

(1) Any person, not being a licensed dealer who desires to purchase and export licensable produce to Guyana and West Indian Islands other than Bermuda shall first obtain a Produce Huckster's Licence as hereinafter provided.

(2) Subject to section 42 and to the provisions of this Act relating to the export of licensable produce by licensed sellers and licensed dealers, any person who exports licensable produce as aforesaid without having first obtained a Produce Huckster's Licence commits an offence against this Act.

### 21. Application for produce Huckster's Licence

(1) Every person desiring to obtain a Produce Huckster's Licence shall make application to the magistrate of the district wherein the applicant normally resides.

(2) Such application shall be in Form F set out in Schedule 2 and shall state in full the name and address of the applicant.

### 22. Produce Huckster's Licence

(1) Subject to the provisions of this section the magistrate shall, upon receipt of such application together with the licence fee set out in Schedule 3, issue to the applicant a Produce Huckster's Licence in Form G set out in Schedule 2.

(2) Such licence fee shall be paid by means of postage stamps which shall be affixed to the application and shall be cancelled, on grant of the licence, by the magistrate.

(3) No such licence shall be issued unless the applicant, in the opinion of the magistrate, has attained the age of 16 years.

(4) The magistrate may refuse to grant such licence if the applicant has within the previous 5 years suffered cancellation of his or her licence under the provisions of section 44.

### 23. Effect of Produce Huckster's Licence

(1) A Produce Huckster's Licence shall entitle the licensee—

(a) to export licensable produce to Guyana and West Indian Islands other than Bermuda; and

(b) to purchase licensable produce from a licensed seller or licensed dealer for the purposes of such export only.

(2) Any licensed huckster who—

(a) purchases licensable produce otherwise than from a licensed seller or licensed dealer; or

(b) disposes of any licensable produce purchased by him or her otherwise than by export to Guyana or West Indian Islands other than Bermuda,

commits an offence against this Act.

However a licensed huckster who is also a licensed pedlar may peddle licensable produce in accordance with the provisions of Part 2.

(3) For the purposes of this section licensable produce shipped to Guyana or West Indian Islands other than Bermuda and reshipped to any port outside such territories shall be presumed to have been exported to such last mentioned port.

The burden of proving that any licensable produce shipped by a licensed huckster to Guyana or any West Indian Island other than Bermuda has not been reshipped with the privity of the huckster to any port outside such territories shall be on the huckster exporting the same.

### 24. Entries in Produce Huckster's Licence

(1) Immediately upon the receipt or disposal of any licensable produce the licensed huckster shall cause to be entered upon his or her licence in a legible manner in Form H set out in Schedule 2 the following particulars—

(a) upon receipt—

(i) the date and time of receipt,

(ii) the name and address of the person from whom the produce is actually received,

(iii) the name of the vendor,

(iv) the number and description of the vendor's licence,

(v) the description of the licensable produce received,

(vi) the weight, measure or quantity of such licensable produce,

(vii) the amount paid for such licensable produce;

(b) upon export—

(i) the date of shipment,

(ii) the place to which, and the ship by which, licensable produce is consigned,

(iii) the description of the licensable produce exported,

(iv) the weight, measure or quantity of such licensable produce.

(2) Every such entry made upon export shall be checked and signed by a Treasury officer.

(3) Any licensed huckster—

(a) who fails or neglects to make or cause to be made, or to be made and checked, as the case may be, the entries required by this section;

(b) in whose licence there shall be found any entry false in any particular; or

(c) whose stock of licensable produce does not correspond with the amount entered in such licence,

commits an offence against this Act.

### 25. Power to inspect licence and examine licensable produce in possession of or shipped by licensed huckster

(1) The Director of Finance and Planning or any person authorised by him or her or by any magistrate, any police officer or a produce inspector may—

(a) call for and inspect the licence of any licensed huckster;

(b) take a copy of any entry in such licence;

(c) examine the licensable produce in the possession of a licensed huckster and for this purpose enter and inspect the premises of a licensed huckster;

(d) open and examine any crate, barrel or other container shipped by a licensed huckster which contains or is suspected to contain licensable produce and for this purpose may go on board and search any ship and may require the master to produce any documents relating to any cargo which is being carried on such ship.

(2) Any licensed huckster who—

(a) fails or neglects to produce any such licence;

(b) refuses to allow a copy of any entry in any such licence to be taken;

(c) obstructs or resists the taking of any such copy;

(d) obstructs or resists any such examination of licensable produce in his or her possession, or the opening and examination of any such crate, barrel or other container shipped by him or her; or

(e) fails or neglects to give and provide such assistance as may be necessary for the purpose of checking licensable produce in his or her possession or shipped by him or her,

commits an offence against this Act.

(3) The master of any ship who—

(a) obstructs or resists the boarding or search of such ship; or

(b) refuses or neglects to produce any documents relating to the cargo in such ship when called upon so to do,

commits an offence against this Act.

## PART 4 PRODUCE DEALERS

### 26. Produce dealers to be licensed

(1) Subject to section 42 and to the foregoing provisions of this Act no person, whether as owner, manager or otherwise shall carry on the business of purchasing, selling, exporting or otherwise dealing in licensable produce without having first obtained a Produce Dealer's Licence as hereinafter provided.

(2) Any person so doing commits an offence against this Act.

### 27. Application for Produce Dealer's Licence

(1) Every person desiring to obtain a Produce Dealer's Licence shall make application to the magistrate of the district within which the premises where he or she intends to carry on business are situate.

(2) Such application shall be in Form I set out in Schedule 2, and shall state in full the name and address of the applicant and the situation and description of the premises in respect of which the application is made.

(3) Subject to the provisions of section 34 the magistrate shall grant the licence within 7 days after the receipt of the application.

### 28. Form and effect of Produce Dealer's Licence

(1) A Produce Dealer's Licence shall be in Form J set out in Schedule 2.

(2) Such licence shall be in respect of one set of premises only, and, subject to the provisions of section 29, shall entitle the licensee to deal in licensable produce upon the premises stated in his or her licence.

### 29. Restrictions as to dealing

(1) Any licensed dealer who purchases licensable produce from any person other than a licensed dealer or another licensed dealer commits an offence against this Act.

(2) Any person who sells or offers for sale licensable produce to a licensed dealer; and any licensed dealer who purchases—

(a) at any place other than his or her licensed premises; or

(b) at any time except between the hours of 6:00 a.m. and 6:00p.m. of any day,

subject to the provisions of subsection (3), commits an offence.

(3) This section shall not be construed to prevent—

(a) the delivery of licensable produce at any time to a ship receiving the same as cargo; or

(b) the sale or delivery of licensable produce to another licensed dealer at the licensed premises of such first mentioned dealer; or

(c) the purchase or delivery of licensable produce from another licensed dealer at the licensed premises of such other dealer.

### 30. Partners, Agents, &c

(1) Two or more persons carrying on business in partnership shall not be obliged to take out more than one licence in respect of the same premises.

(2) A licence to 2 or more partners shall not be determined by the death or retirement of any one or more of them.

### 31. Produce Dealer's Licence not assignable

A Produce Dealer's Licence shall not be assignable or shall authorise any person to carry on the business of a produce dealer by reason of his or her being the personal representative of the person to whom a licence has been granted.

### 32. Personal representatives and transferees

(1) The personal representative of a deceased licensed dealer or the transferee of the business of a licensed dealer may apply to the magistrate in writing for a free licence for the unexpired term of such dealer's licence.

(2) Subject to the provisions of section 34 such magistrate shall, upon satisfactory proof of the facts and upon surrender of such dealer's licence, cancel such dealer's licence and issue a free licence to the applicant for the unexpired term.

### 33. Substitution of premises

A magistrate may, on the application of the licensee, by endorsement on the licence authorise a licensed dealer to transfer his or her licensed business to premises other than those specified in his or her licence, but situate within the same district.

### 34. Conditions precedent to issue of Produce Dealer's Licence

(1) No Produce Dealer's Licence shall be issued unless—

(a) the applicant is resident in Saint Lucia or being an incorporated company has a registered office in Saint Lucia;

(b) the applicant has first paid into the Treasury the licence fee set out in schedule 3;

(c) the applicant in the opinion of the magistrate, has attained the age of 16 years.

(2) The magistrate may refuse to grant a Produce Dealer's Licence if the applicant has within the previous 5 years suffered cancellation of his or her licence under the provisions of section 35.

### 35. Cancellation of Produce Dealer's Licence

(1) If any licensed dealer is convicted of larceny or of receiving stolen goods or of any offence against this Act, the convicting judge or magistrate may in addition to any other penalty, order such dealer's licence to be cancelled.

(2) The Registrar or magistrate as the case may be shall cause notice of such order and of the date thereof to be published in the Gazette.

### 36. Publication of list of licensed dealers

It is the duty of each magistrate to cause—

(a) a list of the names and places of business of all licensed dealers in his or her district to be posted at each Police Station in such district;

(b) such list to be corrected as may be necessary;

(c) such list to be published in the Gazette as soon as convenient after 31 January in each year.

### 37. Sign Boards to be kept by licensed dealers

(1) Every licensed dealer shall cause to be printed in letters at least 3 inches in length and one inch in width, in white upon a black ground or in black upon a white ground publicly visible and legible upon a board placed over the entrance door of his or her licensed premises, his or her name or trade name together with the words “Licensed to deal in licensable produce”.

(2) Every such dealer shall preserve and keep up such name or trade name and words painted visible and legible during all the time that he or she continues licensed.

(3) Any person contravening the provisions of this section, and any person who puts or has any such letters as aforesaid upon his or her premises falsely importing that he or she is a licensed dealer, commits an offence against this Act.

### 38. Produce Dealer's Book

Every licensed dealer shall keep at his or her licensed premises a book to be called a Produce Dealer's Book, in Form K set out in Schedule 2.

### 39. Entries in Produce Dealer's Book

(1) Immediately upon the receipt or disposal of any licensable produce the licensed dealer shall cause the following entries to be made in such book—

(a) Upon receipt—

(i) the date and time of receipt,

(ii) the name and address of the person from whom the licensable produce is actually received,

(iii) the name of the vendor,

(iv) the number and description of the vendor's licence,

(v) the description of the licensable produce received,

(vi) the weight, measure or quantity of such licensable produce,

(vii) the amount paid for such licensable produce;

(b) Upon disposal—

(i) the date of disposal,

(ii) the name and address of the purchaser or the place to which, and the ship by which, exported as the case may be,

(iii) the description of the licensable produce disposed of,

(iv) the weight, measure or quantity of such licensable produce.

(2) Any licensed dealer—

(a) who fails or neglects to keep on his or her premises a Produce Dealer's Book; or

(b) who fails or neglects to cause to be made the entries required by this section; or

(c) in whose book there shall be found any entry false in any particular; or

(d) whose stock of licensable produce does not correspond with the amount entered in such book, commits an offence against this Act.

(3) Where an offence for which a licensed dealer is liable under this section has in fact been committed by some agent or servant of, employee or other person acting for, such licensed dealer, such agent or servant, employee or other person is liable to the same penalty as if he or she were the licensed dealer.

### 40. Licensed dealer using licensable produce grown by him or her for the purposes of his or her business

A licensed dealer who is also the owner of any plantation or land may use licensable produce grown by him or her for the purposes of his or her business provided—

(a) he or she is the holder of a Produce Seller's Licence issued in respect of such plantation or land; and

(b) he or she causes the proper entries to be made in accordance with the provisions of this Act in his or her Produce Seller's Licence and his or her Produce Dealer's Book respectively showing the particulars of any licensable produce transferred and used for the purposes of such business.

### 41. Power to inspect licence and search premises of produce dealer

(1) The Director of Finance and Planning or any person authorised by him or her or by any magistrate, any police officer or a Produce inspector may enter the licensed premises of any licensed dealer or any house or building part of or contiguous thereto in the occupation of such dealer and—

(a) call for and inspect such dealer's licence;

(b) call for and inspect any book required to be kept under this Act;

(c) take a copy of any entry in such book;

(d) examine the licensable produce in the possession of such dealer and the place where it is stored;

(e) weigh and compare all such licensable produce as may be in stock with the entries in such book.

(2) Any licensed dealer who—

(a) obstructs or resists any such entry;

(b) fails or neglects to produce any such licence or book;

(c) refuses to allow a copy of any entry in such book to be taken;

(d) obstructs or resists the taking of any such copy; or

(e) fails or neglects to give and provide such assistance as may be necessary for the purpose of checking the licensable produce in his or her possession,

commits an offence against this Act.

## PART 5 MISCELLANEOUS

### 42. Purchase or export of licensable produce for domestic or private purposes

(1) This Act shall not be construed to prevent—

(a) the purchase from any licensed pedlar or licensed dealer of licensable produce by any person requiring the same for domestic or private purposes and not trading or dealing in the same; or

(b) the export for private purposes of licensable produce by any person not trading or dealing in the same.

(2) Any person not licensed under this Act who purchases licensable produce from any person not being a licensed pedlar or a licensed dealer commits an offence against this Act.

### 43. Director of Finance and Planning may refuse to permit export

The Director of Finance and Planning may refuse to permit the export of any licensable produce unless satisfied either—

(a) that the exporter holds a licence issued under this Act which entitles him or her to export such licensable produce; or

(b) that such licensable produce is *bona fide* intended for private purposes only.

### 44. Cancellation of Produce Huckster's or Produce Pedlar's License

(1) If any licensed huckster or licensed pedlar is convicted of larceny, or of receiving stolen goods or of any offence against this Act the convicting Judge or magistrate may, in addition to any other penalty, order such huckster's or pedlar's licence to be cancelled and to be delivered to the Registrar or magistrate as the case may be within the time specified in such order.

(2) Any person failing to deliver any such licence in accordance with any such order commits an offence against this Act.

### 45. Appointment of Produce Inspectors

The Governor General may appoint any person to be a Produce inspector.

### 46. Fees for Licences

Except when otherwise provided a licence fee in accordance with the scale set out in Schedule 3 shall be payable in respect of every licence issued under this Act.

### 47. Duration of Licences

A licence issued under this Act shall have effect until 31 December next after it is granted.

### 48. Government officials

Licences under this Act shall be issued free to any officer of the Agricultural Department or other Government official who requires such licence in his or her official capacity.

### 49. Power to arrest person conveying produce

(1) It is lawful for—

(a) any owner, manager, overseer, or agent of any plantation whereon any kind of licensable produce is cultivated, without warrant, to apprehend and detain any person having in his or her possession or carrying or conveying any such kind of produce in and upon any such plantation, or in or upon any plantation, road, or way immediately adjoining such first-named plantation;

(b) any police officer or rural constable—

(i) without warrant, to apprehend and detain any person, not being the owner of any plantation whereon any kind of licensable produce is cultivated, having in his or her possession, or found any where carrying or conveying any such kind of produce, and

(ii) to stop and examine any horse or other beast of burden, or any cart, wagon, or other conveyance, in order to ascertain whether any such kind of produce is being conveyed thereon or therein,

which such owner, manager, overseer, agent, police officer or rural constable may have reasonable cause to suspect to be stolen or unlawfully obtained.

(2) If any person liable to arrest under the provisions of this section escapes from any person attempting to arrest him or her or after he or she has been arrested, or lets fall or throws away such produce from his or her possession, or abandons the possession of any such produce, it shall be lawful for any magistrate, upon application, to issue his or her warrant for the arrest of such person, and upon his or her arrest such person shall be deemed to be a person apprehended and detained within the meaning of this section.

(3) The application for a warrant under the last preceding subsection may be made either by any of the persons named in subsection (1) hereof who has actually made or attempted to make the arrest or by a police officer or by a rural constable.

(4) On the hearing of any case under this section, it shall not be incumbent on the prosecutor to prove that the person having in his or her possession or found carrying or conveying any such produce as aforesaid is not the owner, but the onus of proving ownership shall be on the accused.

### 50. Procedure on arrest

(1) Upon the apprehension or detention of any person under the powers contained in section 49, such person shall, together with all produce so suspected to be stolen or unlawfully obtained, and all beasts of burden and conveyances used in carrying and conveying the same be taken before a magistrate, or to the nearest justice of the peace or police station, to be dealt with according to law.

(2) In the latter case such justice of the peace or the constable in charge of such station may take bail with one sufficient surety from such person by recognizance to any amount not exceeding 20 pounds conditioned for his or her appearance for examination before a magistrate in the usual course.

### 51. Not satisfying magistrate as to possession

If any person so taken before a magistrate does not, within such time as may be fixed by such magistrate, make it appear to his or her satisfaction by what lawful means he or she came into possession of any such produce, he or she is liable to imprisonment for 6 months.

### 52. Records to be handed to magistrate

(1) Every person to whom a licence has been issued under this Act shall, upon the expiration of such licence, cause such expired licence together with all books and records required to be kept by this Act to be delivered to the magistrate.

(2) Any person failing or neglecting to cause any expired licence, book or record to be delivered to the magistrate as aforesaid within 14 days from the date of the expiry of such licence and who shall not account for such failure to the satisfaction of the magistrate commits an offence against this Act.

(3) It shall be the duty of the magistrate to preserve all such expired licences, books and records for a period of 2 years from the date of the expiry.

### 53. Penalty

Any person who commits an offence against this Act for which no special penalty is provided by this Act is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $240 or to imprisonment with or without hard labour for a period not exceeding 6 months.

### 54. Prosecution of offences

All offences under this Act may be prosecuted, heard, and determined, and all penalties incurred may be imposed or recovered in the manner provided by the Criminal Code.
