### AN ACT to provide police officers with additional powers for the suppression of escalated crime and for related matters.

Commencement [17 March 2023]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Suppression of Escalated Crime (Police Powers) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act —

“**ammunition**” has the meaning assigned under section 2 of the Firearms Act;

“**escalated crime**” means an action or omission that constitutes an offence and is punishable by law that —

(a) causes substantial deaths, widespread fear and panic and interrupts business activity in a particular area;

(b) constitutes a serious offence in a particular area; and

(c) disrupts the peace, protection of life and property of individuals;

“**escalated crime area**” mean an area designated under section 5;

“**firearm**” has the meaning assigned under section 2 of the Firearms Act;

“**Force**” has the meaning assigned under the Police Act;

“**Minister**” means the Minister responsible for national security;

“**police officer**” has the meaning assigned under the Police Act;

“**serious offence**” means —

(a) an offence under the Anti-Gang Act;

(b) an offence under the Drugs (Prevention of Misuse) Act;

(c) an offence under the Firearms Act;

(d) an offence against the person under Chapter 2 of the Criminal Code;

(e) an offence under the Public Order Act;

“**vehicle**” includes any description of carriage or other artificial contrivance used, or capable of being used, as transportation on land, sea or air.

### 3. Application of this Act

Without prejudice to the Police Act and the Criminal Code this Act applies to a police officer in the execution of his or her functions for the suppression of escalated crime in an escalated crime area.

### 4. Purpose

The purposes of this Act are to —

(a) establish a framework to protect the public order, public safety and economic stability of Saint Lucia;

(b) suppress or otherwise deal effectively with escalated crime within an area in Saint Lucia;

(c) strengthen the capacity of the Force to deal with escalated crime in Saint Lucia.
