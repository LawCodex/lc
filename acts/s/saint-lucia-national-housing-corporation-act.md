### AN ACT to create a national housing corporation and for related matters.

Commencement [5 May 2001]

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY

### 45. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Saint Lucia National Housing Corporation Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**building**” includes any temporary or moveable form of shelter which is used for human habitation;

“**Corporation**” means the Saint Lucia National Housing Corporation established by section 3;

“**dwelling house**” means any premises (including a flat) used as a separate dwelling and any part of a building which is occupied or intended to be occupied as a separate dwelling or being of a type suitable for such use;

“**flat**” means a separate and self-contained set of premises constructed for use for the purpose of a dwelling house and forming part of a building from some other part of which it is divided;

“**local authority**” means any authority recognised as such under any law in force in Saint Lucia;

“**low income household**” means a household (including a person who lives alone) that receives a total income that, in the opinion of the Corporation, is insufficient to permit it to rent housing accommodation adequate for its needs;

“**Minister**” means the Minister responsible for Housing;

“**owner**” in relation to any building or land, means a person, other than a mortgagee not in possession, who is for the time being entitled to dispose of the absolute property of the building or land, whether in possession or in reversion, and includes also a person holding or entitled to the rents and profits of the building or land under a lease or agreement, the unexpired term of which exceeds 3 years.

## PART 2 ESTABLISHMENT, CONSTITUTION, MANAGEMENT AND STAFF

### 3. Establishment of the Corporation

(1) There is hereby established for the purposes of this Act, the Saint Lucia National Housing Corporation.

(2) The Corporation shall be a body corporate to which, subject to this Act, section 19 of the Interpretation Act applies.

### 4. Board of Directors

(1) The Minister shall appoint a Board of Directors (in this Act referred to as “the Board”) of the Corporation which, subject to this Act shall be responsible for the general administration and implementation of the policy of the Corporation.

(2) The Board shall consist of 7 persons having such qualifications and skills as the Minister thinks fit including but not limited to, one person with—

(a) legal qualifications;

(b) expertise in the field of housing and urban development;

I     expertise in land economics;

(d) expertise in estate management;

I     expertise in architecture or building construction.

(3) The Minister shall designate from among the members of the Corporation one member as chairperson.

(4) The chief executive of the Corporation shall be an ex officio member with no voting rights and shall serve as secretary to the Corporation.

(5) A member of the Board shall hold office for a period not exceeding 3 years and is eligible for reappointment.

(6) A member who absents himself or herself without the permission of the chairperson from 3 consecutive meetings of the Board shall cease to be a member.

(7) Where a member is absent on account of illness or other temporary cause or is temporarily absent from the State, the Minister may appoint another person as a temporary member for the period of such absence.

### 5. Procedure and meetings of Board

(1) The Board shall at its first meeting appoint a Deputy chairperson from among its members and in the absence of the chairperson the deputy chairperson shall preside over the meetings of the Board.

(2) The Board shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient for the transaction of business, and such meetings shall be held at such place and time and on such days as the chairperson or in his or her absence the deputy chairperson may determine.

(3) The chairperson or in his or her absence the deputy chairperson, may at any time call a special meeting of the Board and shall call a special meeting within 7 days of a requisition for that purpose addressed to him by any 4 members to consider a matter contained in such requisition.

(4) The chairperson shall call a special meeting of the Board if directed by the Minister to do so.

(5) The chairperson, or in his or her absence the deputy chairperson, shall preside at all meetings of the Board.

(6) The chairperson or in his or her absence the deputy chairperson, and 3 other members shall form a quorum but where a member is disqualified from taking part in any deliberation or decision of the Board he or she shall be disregarded for the purpose of constituting a quorum for deliberating on or deciding that matter.

(7) The decision of the Board with regard to any question shall be determined by a majority vote of the members present at the meeting of the Board and in any case when the votes of the members present in regard to any question shall be equally divided, the chairperson, deputy chairperson or the person presiding at the meeting shall have a casting vote in addition to his or her own vote.

(8) Minutes of the proceedings of each meeting of the Board shall be kept in such manner as the Board may determine and shall be confirmed by the chairperson or the deputy chairperson (as the case may be) as soon as practicable thereafter at a subsequent meeting.

(9) The Board may co-opt any one or more persons to attend any particular meeting of the Board for the purpose of assisting or advising it, but no such co-opted person shall have any right to vote.

(10) Subject to this section, the Board shall have power to regulate its own proceedings.

### 6. Conflict of interest

(1) A member of the Board shall not act as a delegate of any commercial, financial, agricultural, industrial or other interests, or receive or accept directions therefrom in respect of duties to be performed under this Act.

(2) A member of the Board, or any officer or employee of the Corporation shall not, in the course of his or her duties, solicit or accept any gift or advantage for himself or herself or for any person with whom he or she may have family, business or financial connections.

### 7. Disclosure of interest and abstention from voting

(1) Subject to this section, if a member is in any way, whether directly or indirectly, interested in a contract or proposed contract with the Corporation, he or she shall declare the nature of his or her interest at a meeting of the Corporation.

(2) In the case of a proposed contract the declaration required by this section shall be made—

(a) at the meeting of the Board at which the contract is first taken into consideration; or

(b) if such member was not at the date of the meeting interested in the proposed contract, at the next meeting of the Board held after he or she became so interested.

(3) Where a member becomes interested in a contract with the Corporation after it is made, the declaration shall be made at the first meeting of the Board held after the member acquires such interest.

(4) A member who has interest in any contract or arrangement with the Corporation shall vacate the meeting room upon the matter coming up for discussion and his or her departure from the meeting room shall be recorded in the minutes of the meeting.

(5) A person who contravenes this section commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $10,000 or imprisonment for 2 years, or to both.

(6) Where a person convicted under subsection (5) is a member of the Board, that conviction disqualifies him or her from holding office as a member of the Board.

### 8. Remuneration of members

(1) The Corporation shall pay to the chairperson and each member of the Board in respect of his or her membership of the Board such remuneration and allowances, if any, as the Minister determines.

(2) The Corporation may pay different rates of remuneration and allowances to the chairperson, deputy chairperson and other members of the Board.

### 9. Appointment of Managing Director

(1) The Board shall, with the approval of the Minister, appoint a suitably qualified person to be the Managing Director of the Corporation at such salary and upon such terms and conditions of service as it may determine.

(2) Subject to section 4, the Managing Director shall be the chief executive officer of the Corporation and shall be present at all meetings of the Board unless he or she has obtained leave of absence from the chairperson or is incapacitated by illness or other cause from attending such meetings.

(3) In the event of the absence of the Managing Director, the Board may, by instrument in writing and after consultation with the Minister, appoint any person to act as Managing Director during the period of such absence upon such terms and conditions as it may determine.

(4) The person so appointed as Managing Director under subsection (3) may exercise all the powers and perform all the duties of the Managing Director or as many of such powers and duties as may be specified in the instrument of appointment.

### 10. Appointment of staff

(1) The Board may appoint and employ at such remuneration and on such terms and conditions as it thinks fit such officers and employees as it deems necessary for the proper carrying out of its functions under this Act.

(2) The persons appointed and employed under subsection (1) shall perform such duties as may be assigned to them by the Managing Director.

### 11. Establishment and maintenance of pension scheme

(1) Subject to the National Insurance Act the Corporation may with the approval of and subject to general policy directions by the Minister, provide for the establishment and maintenance of a pension scheme for the benefit of its officers and staff.

(2) Despite subsection (1), the pension scheme may empower the Corporation to—

(a) grant gratuities, pensions or superannuation allowances to the widows, families or dependants of its officers and staff;

(b) establish contributory and superannuation funds for the benefit of its officers and staff; and

I     enter into and carry into effect agreements with any general insurance company or other institution for the purpose of effecting a pension scheme.

### 12. Appointment of committees

(1) The Board may appoint a committee of the Board to examine and report to it on any matter arising out of or connected with any of its functions under this Act.

(2) Any such committee shall consist of at least one member of the Board together with such other persons, whether members or not, whose assistance or service the Board may desire.

(3) The Board may by resolution reject or adopt a report of a committee—

(a) either in whole or in part; or

(b) with such modifications or additions as the Board considers appropriate.

(4) Where a person, other than a member of the Board, is appointed to a committee under this section the Board may, with the approval of the Minister, declare the remuneration and allowance of such persons, and such sums shall properly be payable out of the funds and resources of the Corporation.

### 13. Service of notices

(1) Any notice, summons, writ or other proceedings at law or otherwise required to be served on the Corporation for any of the purposes of this Act may be served upon it by delivering it to its secretary, or by sending it by post in a registered letter addressed to the secretary of the Corporation at its registered office.

(2) Subject to subsection (1), any notice, order, or other document required or authorised to be served by the Corporation under this Act may be served either—

(a) by delivering it to the person on whom it is to be served;

(b) by leaving it at the usual or last known place of abode of that person;

I     by sending it in a prepaid letter addressed to that person at his or her usual or last known place of abode; or

(d) in the case of an incorporated company or body, by delivering it to the secretary or clerk of the company or body at their registered or principal office or sending it in a prepaid letter addressed to the secretary of the company or body at that office.

(3) Where it is not practicable after reasonable inquiry to ascertain the name or address of any person on whom a document under subsection (2) is to be served, the secretary of the Corporation may—

(a) address the document to him or her by the description of “owner” or “lessee” or “occupier” as the case may be, and by delivering it to such person on the premises; or

(b) if there is no person on the premises to whom the document can be delivered, by affixing it or a copy of it, to some conspicuous part of the premises.

### 14. Protection of Corporation and its officers

An act done or proceedings taken under this Act shall not be questioned on the grounds of—

(a) the existence of any vacancy in the membership of the Board, or any defect in the constitution of the corporation;

(b) the contravention by a member of any of the provisions of section 7; or

I     any omission, defect or irregularity not affecting the merits of the case.

### 15. Power of Minister to issue directions

(1) The Minister may, after consultation with the chairperson, give the Board such directions of a general character as to the policy to be followed by the Board in the performance of its functions, and the Board shall give effect to those directions.

(2) The Minister shall give such directions to the Corporation in respect of the disposal of land vested in it and in respect of the development by it of the land as appears to him or her to be necessary or expedient to ensure, so far as practicable, the preservation of any features of special architectural or historic interest.

## PART 3 FUNCTIONS AND POWERS

### 16. Functions of Corporation

The functions of the Corporation are—

(a) to acquire, hold and manage land and other property of any kind and sell, let, lease or otherwise dispose of any land or other property on such terms and conditions as the Corporation determines, after consultation with the Minister;

(b) to ensure the development, building, maintenance, repair, and improvement of operations relating to housing and settlement development schemes;

I     to ensure that water, electricity, sewage and other services are adequately provided in housing and development schemes;

(d) to carry on any business or undertaking in, or for the purposes of, any housing development;

I     to ensure the implementation of plans for settlement clearance and redevelopment; and

(f) to carry out such other functions as may be conferred upon it by the Minister for the purposes of this Act.

### 17. Powers of Corporation in relation to unfit building

(1) Where in the opinion of the Corporation a building to which this Act applies—

(a) is derelict;

(b) is unfit for human habitation;

I     creates a nuisance; or

(d) is injurious or likely to be injurious to health,

the Corporation may serve on the owner of the building notice in writing requiring him or her to demolish, secure, repair or rebuild the building to the satisfaction of the Corporation within a time to be specified in the notice.

(2) If an owner on whom a notice is served under subsection (1) fails to comply with the requirements of the notice, the Corporation may carry out the works required to be done and in the case of a demolition may sell or otherwise dispose of any materials resulting from such demolition.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), the expenses incurred by the Corporation under subsection (2) may be recovered as a civil debt due to the Corporation.

(4) Where the Corporation carries out demolition under subsection (2) it may sell or otherwise dispose of any materials resulting from the demolition and the amount realised from the sale shall be used to defray any expenses incurred by the Corporation in carrying out the demolition.

(5) In the event that there is a surplus in the hands of the Corporation from the amount realised by such sale after deducting any expenses, the Corporation shall pay the surplus to the owner or his or her representative.

(6) In determining for the purpose of this section whether a house is fit for human habitation, regard may be had to the extent, if any, to which it suffers from sanitary defects.

(7) Where in consequence of any notice issued under this section persons will be displaced, the Corporation shall first satisfy itself that suitable alternative accommodation is available for such persons.

### 18. Powers of Corporation in relation to abandoned objects

The Corporation shall have the power to remove or otherwise dispose of any object abandoned on its lands.

### 19. Power to make arrangements

(1) The Corporation may, after consultation with the Minister, make arrangements with a company, firm, partnership or local government authority for the purpose of enabling such body to—

(a) provide housing accommodation for low income households;

(b) provide housing accommodation to relocate persons displaced—

(i) by government action under any law,

(ii) by a settlement clearance or redevelopment scheme,

(iii) by the demolition of a building under section 17, or

(iv) in response to a natural disaster or other emergency;

I     alter, repair or improve a house or building which the Corporation has acquired or has an interest in, with a view to providing or improving housing accommodation for low income households.

(2) The Corporation may, after consultation with the Minister, create a subsidiary company to undertake any of the functions of the Corporation.

## PART 4 SETTLEMENT CLEARANCE, UPGRADING AND REDEVELOPMENT SCHEME

### 20. Power to secure settlement clearance

Where the Minister, as a result of such enquiries as he or she considers necessary, is satisfied that housing accommodation in an area is overcrowded, insanitary, severely congested or unfit for human habitation and that these conditions can be remedied—

(a) by ordering the demolition, reconstruction or repair of the accommodation;

(b) by the acquisition of the land and other property in the area and undertaking the demolition, reconstruction or repair of the housing accommodation;

I     by the acquisition of land surrounding or surrounded by the area, the acquisition of which is reasonably necessary for the satisfactory reconstruction and development of the area,

the Minister shall cause the Corporation to prepare and submit for his or her consideration a plan defining the area.

### 21. Duty to secure redevelopment

Where the Minister, as a result of such enquiries as he or she considers necessary, is satisfied that—

(a) an area contains 20 or more houses occupied by low income households;

(b) at least 50% of the houses referred to under paragraph (a) is overcrowded or so arranged as to be congested or unfit for human habitation and not capable at a reasonable expense of being rendered so fit;

I     it is expedient in relation to the provision of housing accommodation for lower income persons that the area as a whole be redeveloped,

the Minister shall cause the Corporation to prepare and submit for his or her consideration a plan defining the area.

### 22. Plans to be sent to the Minister

(1) Within 3 months after the Corporation produces a plan under sections 20 and 21, or within such extended period as the Minister may allow, the Corporation shall prepare and submit to the Minister a plan of any scheme intended to be undertaken, accompanied by a statement containing appropriate particulars of the scheme indicating—

(a) the manner in which it is intended that the defined area shall be laid out and the land to be used, and in particular the land intended to be used for the provision of roads, open spaces, community facilities and recreational grounds;

(b) the approximate area of the land to be acquired;

I     the approximate number and the nature of the houses to be provided by the Corporation;

(d) the average number of houses to be constructed per acre;

I     if the demolition of existing houses and the erection of new houses are proposed, the nature of the proposed new houses;

(f) if the total demolition of existing houses is not proposed, the nature of repairs, improvements and reconstruction intended to be made;

(g) the time within which the scheme or any part of the scheme is to be carried into effect;

(h) the estimated cost of the scheme and the proposed method for distribution of the houses provided under the scheme;

(i) such incidental, consequential and supplementary provisions (including provisions as to the subsequent variation of the scheme) as may appear necessary or proper for the purposes of the scheme;

(j) objections made by persons affected by the scheme where such objections have not been withdrawn or met.

(2) The Corporation shall before submitting such scheme to the Minister—

(a) publish in at least 2 local newspapers a notice stating that the scheme is about to be submitted to the Minister for approval, naming a place where the plan and particulars of the scheme may be inspected and specifying the time within which and the manner in which objections may be made;

(b) serve a notice to like effect on every owner and on every person who to the knowledge of the Corporation has an interest in land in the defined area, except persons holding a monthly tenancy or any tenancy for a lesser period.

### 23. Local body affected by scheme to be notified

(1) In any case where a scheme is wholly or in part within an area under the jurisdiction of a local authority, the Corporation shall, before submitting the draft scheme to the Minister for approval, furnish particulars and a copy of the scheme to the local authority for its consideration and representations.

(2) Where the local authority is desirous of making any objections or representations in respect of the said scheme, it shall within the prescribed time and manner submit the objections to the Corporation.

(3) The Corporation shall consider any objections and representations received by it under this section and shall give full opportunity for a local authority to be heard by the Corporation and in submitting the scheme to the Minister for approval shall forward copies of all the objections or representations which have not been met.

### 24. Approval of scheme by Minister

(1) The Minister may, if he or she thinks fit, after consultation with Cabinet and after considering any objections to the scheme properly made, approve the scheme submitted by the Corporation or part thereof, either with or without modification, and the scheme or the part of it approved by the Minister shall be binding on the Corporation.

(2) Where the Minister considers the scheme inadequate he or she may refuse to approve it and may require the Corporation to prepare and submit to him or her a revised scheme within such time as the Minister may fix.

(3) The Minister shall not approve of any scheme unless he or she is satisfied that—

(a) the size of the area is such that the housing condition in the area can be remedied within a reasonable period;

(b) the financial resources of the Corporation are or will be sufficient for the purpose of carrying into effect such scheme;

I     the Corporation has provided, or secured alternative accommodation for low income households which will be displaced by the scheme.

(4) The modification referred to in subsection (1) may include the alteration of the defined area so as to exclude land from the area but not so as to add land thereto.

### 25. Notification of approval of scheme

(1) Upon notification to the Corporation of the approval by the Minister of any scheme, the Corporation shall immediately publish in the official Gazette and in at least 2 local newspapers a notice stating that the scheme has been approved and naming a place where a copy of the scheme and its particulars may be inspected.

(2) Where, it appears to the Corporation after a scheme has been approved, that any land in the area comprised in the approved scheme ought to be re-developed or used otherwise than as indicated in the scheme, the Corporation shall prepare and submit for the approval of the Minister a new scheme in respect of that land.

### 26. Corporation to implement scheme

(1) The Corporation shall take steps to execute a scheme after the scheme has been approved by the Minister and, within the time specified in the scheme or within such further time as may be allowed by the Minister.

(2) The Corporation may in like manner and for the purposes of this scheme lay out, pave, and complete all roads upon the land acquired by it, and all roads so laid out and completed shall in accordance with existing law be public roads.

(3) The Corporation may engage the services of any person to carry the whole or any part of the scheme into effect upon such terms as the Corporation may think expedient.

(4) When a scheme has been substantially completed by the Corporation in accordance with this Act, the Corporation shall immediately certify that fact to the Minister and specify the date upon which the buildings within the area of the scheme or any part thereof shall be ready for habitation.

### 27. Slum clearance and re-development by owner of land and buildings

(1) Despite the publication of a notice by the Corporation in accordance with section 25, the owner of any land or building included in the relevant scheme may, with the permission of the Corporation, undertake the clearance and reconstruction of the land and buildings so specified or the re-development thereof subject to this Act.

(2) An application for such permission shall be made by the owner in writing addressed to the Corporation within 8 weeks of the date of publication of the notice by the Corporation and shall contain full particulars accompanied by plans.

(3) The Corporation shall, within 4 weeks after receipt of such application, consider the application at a meeting of the Corporation and shall, subject to subsection (4), by resolution passed at such meeting, either grant the application or refer the matter to the Minister.

(4) The Minister may either reject any application so referred to him or her or grant the application with such modifications, if any, as he or she thinks fit.

(5) Where the application is granted, the owner shall within 4 weeks of the date of his or her being notified of the granting of his or her application enter into a bond with one or more sureties approved by the Corporation for a sum not less than the estimated cost of clearance and redevelopment of the area as specified in the application approved by the Minister.

(6) It shall be a condition of the bond required under subsection (5) that the owner shall pay the sum of the bond or part thereof to the Corporation upon failure to complete the clearance and redevelopment within the period specified in the bond or in accordance with the scheme.

(7) Where the owner of any land and building fails to complete the clearance and redevelopment, in accordance with the scheme and to the satisfaction of the Corporation or within the period specified in the bond, subject to any variation or extensions approved by the sureties, the Corporation may, despite the enforcement of the bond, acquire the land and building and clear and re-develop the land and building in accordance with this Act.

(8) Upon the completion by the owner of the clearance and the reconstruction of the land or buildings or the re-development thereof, as the case may be, to the satisfaction of the Corporation, the Corporation shall cause the notice published by the Corporation in accordance with section 25 to be amended accordingly by the publication of an amending notice.

### 28. Demolition order

(1) Where, in respect of an area included in an approved settlement clearance scheme, the Corporation determines that a building should be demolished, it shall make, and serve on the owner or his or her representative a demolition order ordering the demolition of the building within a time specified in the order.

(2) Where the owner of a building subject to a demolition order, or his or her representative, fails to give effect to the order within the time specified or within such extended time permitted by the Corporation, the Corporation shall carry out the demolition.

(3) Where the Corporation carries out demolition under subsection (2) it may sell or otherwise dispose of any materials resulting from the demolition and the amount realised from the sale shall be applied towards any expenses incurred by the Corporation in carrying out the demolition.

(4) In the event that there is a surplus in the hands of the Corporation from the amount realised by the sale after deducting any expenses, the Corporation shall pay the surplus to the owner or his or her representative.

### 29. Use of land under demolition order

(1) Where a demolition order has become operative, no land to which the order applies shall be used for building purposes, or otherwise developed, except subject to such restrictions and conditions as the Corporation may impose.

(2) An owner who is aggrieved by a restriction or condition imposed on the use of the land or by a subsequent refusal by the Corporation to cancel or modify such restriction or condition may appeal by notice in writing to a judge in chambers who shall determine the matter and make such order as he or she thinks fit, and the judge's decision shall be final.

(3) A person who commences or causes the commencement of any work in contravention of a restriction or condition imposed under subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine of $100 for each day that the work continues to exist.

(4) A person convicted under subsection (3) is liable to pay any costs incurred in the demolition of the work in contravention of the restriction or condition imposed.

### 30. Power of Corporation to seek information

(1) The Corporation may, by notice in writing, require an owner or occupier of land or a building in the area of an approved scheme or a person receiving, whether for himself or herself or another, rent out of such land or building, to state to the Corporation—

(a) particulars of the interest or right by virtue of which he or she owns or occupies such land or building; or

(b) particulars of the interest or right by virtue of which he or she receives such rent; and

I     the name, address and interest or right of every person who to his or her knowledge has an interest or right over or in respect of such land or building.

(2) Every person who is required under this section to state in writing any matter or thing to the Corporation and who—

(a) fails so to state any such matter or thing within the time appointed under subsection (1); or

(b) when so stating any such matter or thing, makes a statement in writing which is false or misleading in a material particular,

commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $1,000.

## PART 5 ACQUISITION AND COMPENSATION

### 31. Acquisition of property by Corporation

(1) Where by this Act the Corporation is authorised to acquire land or buildings or any interest therein, such acquisition may, subject to this Act, be by way of gift, by private treaty or, subject to this section, may be compulsorily acquired in accordance with the Land Acquisition Act.

(2) Any land or building compulsorily acquired under the Land Acquisition Act for any of the purposes of this Act shall be deemed to have been acquired for a public purpose.

(3) For the purposes of an acquisition of a building for the purpose of this Act references in the Land Acquisition Act to land shall be construed as including references to buildings.

### 32. Assessment of compensation

(1) Where land or buildings or any interest therein is acquired compulsorily under the Land Acquisition Act for the purposes of this Act, compensation shall be payable to the owner of such land or buildings in accordance with the Land Acquisition Act.

(2) The Corporation shall, out of its own funds, pay into the Consolidated Fund any compensation paid under the Land Acquisition Act in respect of any compulsory acquisition.

(3) In assessing the amount of compensation payable under the Land Acquisition Act to the owners of land or buildings or any interest therein acquired compulsorily by the Corporation for the purposes of this Act—

(a) in the case of land with buildings which are unfit for human habitation or are dangerous or injurious or likely to be injurious to the health of the inhabitants of the area, the compensation payable shall be the value of the site as a cleared site available for development without regard to any buildings existing thereon;

(b) in the case of land with buildings in respect of which sanitary defects exist but which are not otherwise unfit for human habitation or dangerous or injurious or likely to be injurious to the health of the inhabitants of the area, the compensation payable shall be the value of the site as a cleared site together with the value of the buildings, after deducting such amount as would be required to abate the sanitary defects;

I     in the case of any other land and building, the compensation payable shall be the value thereof.

(4) In assessing the amount of compensation payable to the owners of land and buildings acquired for a re-development scheme, there may be taken into consideration any increased value which will be given to other premises of the same owner.

(5) The owner of any building which is regarded as unfit for human habitation is entitled, on making a request in writing, to be furnished by the Corporation with a statement in writing of its reason for deciding that the building is unfit.

### 33. Power to pay allowances to displaced persons

(1) The Corporation may pay to any person displaced from any dwelling-house or other building in a settlement clearance area or a re-development area, which has been acquired by the Corporation under this Act, such reasonable allowance as may be required to meet the expenses of moving.

(2) The Corporation may also pay to a person carrying on a trade or business in any such dwelling-house such reasonable allowance as it thinks fit towards the loss which the person will sustain by reason of the disturbance of his or her trade or business and in estimating that loss the Corporation shall have regard to the period for which the premises occupied by that person might reasonably have been expected to be available for the purpose of the trade or business and the availability of other premises suitable for that purpose.

## PART 6 FINANCIAL PROVISIONS

### 34. Funds and resources of Corporation

The funds and resources of the Corporation shall consist of—

(a) such sums as may be paid to its account out of moneys voted by Parliament;

(b) such sums as may accrue from the operations of the Corporation;

I     such sums or property as may in any manner become payable to or vested in the Corporation in respect of any matter incidental to its functions;

(d) such sums as may be borrowed by the Corporation;

I     such sums as may become available from any other source approved by the Minister.

### 35. Application and investment of funds

(1) The funds of the Corporation shall be applied towards discharging the obligations and performing any of the functions of the Corporation under this Act and for connected purposes.

(2) The Corporation may, with the approval of the Minister, invest any of its funds not required to be expended in meeting its obligations or in the performance of its functions.

(3) The Minister may require that any balance standing to the credit of the Corporation at the end of the financial year, after full allowance has been made for the matters under subsection (1), be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

### 36. Accounts and audit

(1) The Corporation shall keep account of its transactions in such form and manner as the Minister determines and its accounts shall be audited annually by an auditor appointed by the Corporation with the approval of the Minister.

(2) The Director of Audit shall, on the direction of the Minister, carry out at any time an investigation into, or audit of, such accounts.

(3) The members, officers and other staff of the Corporation shall give the Director of Audit or other person appointed to audit the accounts of the Corporation under subsections (1) and (2), as the case may be, access to all books, documents, cash and securities of the Corporation and shall give him or her, on request, all information as may be within their knowledge in relation to the operations of the Corporation.

(4) A person who fails to comply with subsection (3) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $10,000 or to imprisonment for 2 years.

### 37. Annual report

(1) The Corporation shall not later than 4 months after the expiration of each financial year, submit to the Minister a report containing—

(a) an account of its transactions throughout the preceding financial year in such detail as the Minister directs; and

(b) a statement of the accounts of the Corporation audited under section 36.

(2) A copy of the report together with a copy of the auditor's report shall be laid before Parliament within 3 months of its receipt by the Minister.

### 38. Borrowing powers

(1) Subject to this Act the Corporation may borrow, secure or raise money for all or any of the following purposes—

(a) the financing of the purchasing of any land whether or not within a designated area;

(b) the laying out and development of any such area;

I     the provision of working capital;

(d) redemption of any debentures or debenture stock or other security that the Corporation is entitled or required to redeem;

I     any expenditure properly chargeable to capital account;

(f) meeting any of its obligations or fulfilling its functions under this Act.

(2) The power of the Corporation to borrow shall be exercisable only with the approval of the Minister as to the amount, as to the sources of borrowing and as to the terms on which the borrowing may be effected; and an approval given for the purpose of this section may be either general or limited to a particular borrowing and may be either unconditional or subject to conditions.

### 39. Guarantee of borrowings of Corporation

(1) With the approval of the House of Assembly the Minister may guarantee, in such manner and on such conditions as he or she may think fit, the payments of the principal and interest of any authorised borrowing of the Corporation.

(2) Where the Minister is satisfied that there has been default in the payment of any principal monies or interest guaranteed under this section he or she shall direct the repayment out of the Consolidated Fund of the amount in respect of which there has been such default.

(3) The Corporation shall make to the Accountant General, at such times and in such manner as the Minister may direct, payments of such amounts as may be so directed towards repayment of any sums issued in fulfilment of a guarantee given under this section, and payments of interest in respect of such sums at such rate as the Minister may direct, and different rates of interest may be directed as regards different sums and as regards interests for different periods.

## PART 7 TRANSITIONAL PROVISIONS

### 40. Transfer of assets and liabilities

(1) Upon the commencement of this Act—

(a) all land and other property of every kind, vested immediately before the commencement of this Act in the Saint Lucia Housing Authority established under section 3 of the Housing Act, 1966, and the Housing and Urban Development Corporation established under section 3 of the Housing and Urban Development Corporation Act, 1971, are hereby vested in the Corporation;

(b) all rights, privileges and all the liabilities and other obligations to which, immediately before the commencement of this Act the Saint Lucia Housing Authority and the Housing and Urban Development Corporation were entitled or subject to, are hereby transferred and conferred or imposed upon the Corporation for the purposes of this Act.

(2) A reference in any deed, contract, hypothecary obligation, bond or security, or other document to the Saint Lucia Housing Authority or the Housing and Urban Development Corporation shall upon the commencement of this Act be construed as a reference to the Corporation.

(3) Legal proceedings pending immediately before the commencement of this Act by or against the Saint Lucia Housing Authority or the Housing and Urban Development Corporation may be continued on and after that day by or against the Corporation.

### 41. Transfer of officers

(1) The Public Service Commission may approve the transfer and appointment of an officer from the Public Service to the service of the Corporation or from the service of the Corporation to the Public Service.

(2) Where a transfer and appointment has been approved under subsection (1) arrangements shall be made by the Government or the Corporation to pay to the Government or the Corporation as the case may be, such contribution as may be provided for in rules made by the Cabinet of Ministers in respect of pensions and gratuities, and any such rules may make provision in different classes of cases.

### 42. Limitation of time for legal actions

Where as a result of a liability or obligation imposed upon the Corporation under section 40, the Corporation becomes liable to an action in tort, the proceedings shall be brought within 6 months after the commencement of this Act or after the cause of action giving rise to the proceedings is known, whichever is the soonest.

## PART 8 MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

### 43. Recovery of rent

(1) After December in any year any rent due to the Corporation in the preceding year shall be deemed to be a judgment debt due from its lessees and tenants as defendants in favour of the Corporation, as plaintiff, together with a further sum, by way of interest equal to 2½% of the amount of rent due, for every period of 3 months or part thereof, computed from 1 January preceding, until paid with costs; and execution for the recovery of the same may be issued on the written application of the Corporation as provided in subsections (2) and (3).

(2) A certificate issued under the seal of the Corporation stating the amount of any unpaid rent and the date on which it became due may be registered like a judgment in the Registry of Deeds and Mortgages and with like effect; and after the satisfaction of this judgment, a certificate of payment thereof issued under the seal of the Corporation may be registered in the Registry of Deeds and Mortgages and shall have the effect of discharging this judgment; such certificate shall be registered free of charge.

(3) The Corporation shall on or before 15 December in each year notify in the Gazette and in a newspaper in Saint Lucia at the Corporation's expense, that after December, movables may be taken in execution and sold for rent unpaid after 31 December.

(4) On or before 17 December, or so soon thereafter as practicable, the Corporation shall cause a copy of the notification referred to in subsection (3) to be affixed on or near the outer door of the office of the Corporation as well as the district court offices.

### 44. Exemptions

The Corporation is exempted from liability for any tax, duty, levy or any other similar charge.

### 45. Conditions regarding the letting of houses

(1) The Corporation shall, in relation to the letting of houses, observe the requirements specified in this section.

(2) The Corporation shall, in the selection of tenants of houses to be occupied by low income households, give preference to persons—

(a) who are occupying unsanitary or over-crowded houses;

(b) who have large families;

I     who are living under unsatisfactory housing conditions,

except in so far as the demand for housing accommodation in any area on the part of such persons can be satisfied without such preference being given.

(3) The Corporation shall, in the fixing of rents, take into consideration the rents ordinarily paid by low-income families in the locality of the houses being rented.

(4) The Corporation shall, review the rents fixed under subsection (3), and shall make such changes in rents as circumstances may require.

### 46. Minutes receivable in evidence

The Minutes of meetings of the Board of the Corporation shall, if duly signed by the chairperson or deputy chairperson, be receivable in evidence in all legal proceedings without further proof thereof, and every meeting of the Corporation in respect of the proceedings of which minutes have been so made shall be deemed to have been duly convened and held and all members present at such meetings shall be deemed to have been duly qualified to act.

### 47. Power of entry

(1) A person authorised by the Corporation in writing may at any reasonable time, on giving 24 hours notice to the occupier or owner, enter any building or premises for the purpose of—

(a) ascertaining whether or not circumstances exist which would authorise or require the Corporation to take any action or execute any works under this Act or any regulations under this Act;

(b) inspecting buildings or land which the Corporation is authorised to acquire; and

I     generally, examining and inspecting buildings and lands vested in the Corporation.

(2) A person who obstructs any person acting under the authority of this Act commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $10,000 or to imprisonment for 2 years or to both.

### 48. Vesting of lands

(1) Cabinet may, by order, transfer to the Corporation, the administrative control of any lands or interest therein owned by the Government and thereupon the lands or interest so transferred shall be held and dealt with by the Corporation in accordance with this Act.

(2) Cabinet may by order vest in the Corporation any lands owned by the Government upon such terms and conditions as it considers fit.

(3) With the approval of Cabinet, the Corporation may by order vest any lands owned by it in the Government or any other statutory body.

### 49. Regulations

The Corporation may, with the approval of the Minister, make regulations generally for carrying out the provisions of this Act and with respect to—

(a) determining the number of persons who may occupy a dwelling house let by the Corporation;

(b) the use of buildings let by the Corporation with a view to—

(i) the prevention of fire, nuisance and sanitary defects, and to ensuring the provision of adequate ventilation therein, and

(ii) promoting and enforcing cleanliness of such premises;

I     the taking of precautions in any such dwelling houses in case of any infectious disease;

(d) ensuring the occupation of a dwelling house in a tenantable manner and the prevention of damage to, or destruction of, any part thereof by a tenant or other person;

I     the inspection of buildings and land vested in the Corporation;

(f) the books and accounts to be kept by the Corporation and the manner in which they are to be kept;

(g) the custody of the funds and other property of the Corporation, the making of payments by the Corporation and the signing of cheques;

(h) the execution of documents by or on behalf of the Corporation;

(i) the terms and conditions on which buildings may be let or leased by the Corporation;

(j) the terms and conditions in respect of which loans, advances or guarantees can be made or entered into by the Corporation;

(k) the forms of mortgages, charges, leases, letting, agreements and other instruments.

### 50. Savings

Any subordinate legislation made under the Housing and Urban Development Corporation Act, 1971, the Housing Act, 1966 and the Slum Clearance and Housing Act, 1945 in force immediately before the coming into force of this Act, so far as it is not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act, continue in force as if made under this Act.
