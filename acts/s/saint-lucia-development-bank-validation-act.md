# SAINT LUCIA DEVELOPMENT BANK VALIDATION ACT

**(Act No. 9 of 2009)**

Act 9 of 2009 .. in force 6 July 2009

ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| 1. | Short title |
| 2. | Interpretation |
| 3. | Validation of Bank |

An Act to validate the Saint Lucia Development Bank and for related matters.

Commencement [6 July 2009]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Saint Lucia Development Bank Validation Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Act**” means the Saint Lucia Development Bank Act 2008, No. 12;

“**Bank**” means the Saint Lucia Development Bank established under section 3 of the Act.

### 3. Validation of Bank

Every act or thing done by the Bank for the period 1st September, 2008 to 15th February, 2009 that would have been lawful if the Act had been in force at the time when it was done is validated and declared to have been lawfully done by the Bank.
