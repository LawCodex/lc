### AN ACT to incorporate the Saint Lucia Mission of Seventh-Day Adventists as the body replacing the East Caribbean Conference of Seventh-Day Adventists.

Commencement [1 April 2000]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Saint Lucia Mission of Seventh-Day Adventists Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Corporation**” means the East Caribbean Conference of Seventh-Day Adventists (Saint Lucia) declared and appointed under section 3 of the East Caribbean Conference of Seventh-Day Adventists Ordinance 1963, No. 11;

“**Mission**” means the Saint Lucia Mission of Seventh-Day Adventists established under section 3.

### 3. Establishment of Mission

There is hereby established a body corporate to be known as the Saint Lucia Mission of Seventh-Day Adventists to which section 19 of the Interpretation Act applies.

### 4. Composition of Mission

The Mission shall comprise members as follows—

(a) a President who shall be Livingston Aaron or his or her successor in title;

(b) a Secretary who shall also be the Treasurer and who shall be Johnson Frederick or his or her successor in title;

(c) one Trustee who shall be Elisha Hunte or his or her successor in title.

### 5. Validity of acts

An act or proceeding of the Mission shall not be invalidated by reason of any defect in the appointment of any person to the offices mentioned in section 4.

### 6. Seal

(1) The President shall be the holder of the Seal of the Mission.

(2) A deed or document purporting to be executed by the Mission shall not be valid unless it is sealed with the Seal of the Mission and signed by at least 2 members of the Mission.

### 7. Property

Property movable and immovable vested in any person for or on behalf of the Corporation is hereby vested in the Mission.
