### AN ACT to provide for the Incorporation of the National Spiritual Assembly of the Baha'is of Saint Lucia.

Commencement [1 November 1986]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the National Spiritual Assembly of the Baha'is of Saint Lucia (Incorporation) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act, “**The Baha'is**” means the members of the Baha'is Community in Saint Lucia.

### 3. Incorporation of the National Spiritual Assembly of the Baha'is of Saint Lucia

The National Spiritual Assembly of the Baha'is of Saint Lucia (hereinafter referred to as the Assembly) shall be and is hereby created a body corporate and by that name shall have perpetual succession and may sue and be sued in all Courts of Justice in the State and shall have and use a common seal provided that such seal shall only be used in the presence of at least 2 of the Trustees of the Assembly.

### 4. Trustees of the Assembly

(1) The Assembly shall consist of 9 members who shall be elected annually from among the voting members of the Baha'is in Saint Lucia and who or their successors in office, shall be the Trustees of the Baha'i Community in Saint Lucia.

(2) The Assembly shall give the Registrar of Companies annual notice of the names and addresses of the members of the Assembly.

### 5. Registered address and service of documents

(1) The Assembly shall at all times have a fixed address in Saint Lucia for the service of documents on the Assembly and that address shall be registered in the office of the Registrar of Companies by the Assembly.

(2) All documents to be served on the Assembly may be served by leaving the document at or by sending the document by registered post to the address registered in accordance with subsection (1).

### 6. Aims and objects

The aims and objects of the Assembly are

(a) to administer the affairs of the Baha'is according to the teachings and principles of the Founder of the Baha'i Faith as interpreted and applied by the Universal House of Justice the Supreme Body of the Baha'i Faith;

(b) to hold educational, humanitarian and spiritual meetings and conferences;

(c) to establish temples of universal worship;

(d) to establish institutions for humanitarian services;

(e) to emphasise the unit of mankind and its religion;

(f) to promote world peace.

### 7. Powers of the Assembly

The Assembly shall have the following powers

(a) to enter into or perform any contract for the furtherance of its objects with any person, firm, association, corporation, private or public body;

(b) to hold and be named as beneficiary under any trust established by law or otherwise, or under any will or other testamentary instrument, any gift, devise or bequest in which a trust is established;

(c) to receive gifts, devises or bequests of money or other real or personal property;

(d) to acquire, hold, purchase, lease, possess and enjoy

(i) any land and hereditaments; and

(ii) any personal property absolutely or for leasehold or for any other estate or interest therein;

(e) to give, grant, let, charge, improve, manage, develop, exchange, lease, mortgage, sell, convey, assign, dispose of, turn to account or otherwise deal with all or any of the property both present and future, held or vested or any part thereof;

(f) to borrow, raise or secure the payment of money in such manner as may be thought fit and in particular by the issue of debentures and script charged upon all or any of the property both present and future, held by or vested in the Assembly and to redeem and pay off any such securities;

(g) to publish books, magazines and newspapers in keeping with the principles of the Baha'i Faith;

(h) to supervise the functioning of the local and other elements of the Baha'is;

(i) to do all such things which in the opinion of the Assembly are necessary, proper and advantageous for the promotion of the complete and successful administration of the Assembly.

### 8. Rules

The Assembly may adopt or make rules for the proper conduct of its proceedings and for the proper discharge of its functions and such rules may be varied or revoked by the Assembly.

### 9. Seal of the Assembly

The Seal of the Assembly shall be of such design and pattern as the Assembly may determine.

### 10. Execution of documents

(1) No deed, bond, document or any contract or other agreement shall be entered into on behalf of the Baha'is without the approval of the Assembly.

(2) No deed, bond, document, contract or other agreement purporting to be executed by the Assembly shall be of any force or validity unless it is sealed with the common seal of the Assembly and signed by not less than 2 of the Members of the Assembly.

### 11. Immunity to acts of proceedings of Assembly

No act or proceedings of the Assembly shall be invalidated for any defect in the selection of any person as an officer or Trustee of the Assembly or for any defect in the notification or in the registration required under subsection (2) of section 4 and subsection (1) of section 5.

### 12. Savings of crown and other rights

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or Successors of any body, politic or corporate, or of any other person except such as are mentioned or referred to in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
