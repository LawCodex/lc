### AN ACT to prohibit aliens from acting in certain capacities on ships registered in Saint Lucia.

Commencement [21 September 1929]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Aliens Restriction Act.

### 2. Aliens not to act as masters of ships registered in Saint Lucia

An alien shall not act as master, chief officer or chief engineer of a merchant ship registered in Saint Lucia or as skipper or second hand of a fishing boat registered in Saint Lucia, except in the case of a ship or boat employed habitually in voyages between ports outside Saint Lucia.

However, this prohibition shall not apply to any alien who has acted as a master, chief officer or chief engineer of a British ship, or as skipper or second hand of a British fishing boat, at any time during the War and is certified by the Admiralty to have performed good and faithful service in that capacity.

### 3. Penalty

A person who acts in contravention of this Act or who aids and abets any person in any contravention of this Act is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $96 or to imprisonment, with or without hard labour, for a term not exceeding 6 months, or to both such fine and imprisonment.
