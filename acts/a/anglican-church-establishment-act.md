# ANGLICAN CHURCH (ESTABLISHMENT) ACT

**(Acts 30 of 1916 and 16 of 1933)**

Act 30 of 1916 Revision and Act 16 of 1933.. in force 26 August 1876

### AN ACT to give status to the Church of England in Saint Lucia, and to confer certain powers and privileges upon the said Church.

Commencement [26 August 1876]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Anglican Church (Establishment) Act.

### 2. Interpretation of term “Members of the Church of England”

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, the term “**Members of the Church of England**” means all baptized persons claiming or availing themselves of the ministrations of the said Church; such persons being males of the age of 21 years and upwards, and paying towards the general funds of the said Church such annual sum as the Church Council may from time to time determine.

*The Church Council.*

### 3. Election of a Church Council

The Members of the Church of England in Saint Lucia shall have power to elect certain lay members of a representative body for administering the affairs of the said Church, of which representative body the licensed Clergy of the said Church, resident and ministering in Saint Lucia shall ex officio be Members; and such representative body shall be called “The Church Council of the Anglican Church in Saint Lucia.”

### 4. Voting for lay representative

All Members of the Church of England resident in a Parish or Ecclesiastical District, or forming part of a Congregation, shall have a right to vote for the election of the lay representatives of such Parish, District, or Congregation.

### 5. Powers of administration of the Church Council

The Church Council shall have power to arrange local matters of Church economy, revenue, and administration, and to make provision for the settlement of all questions arising as to the same.

### 6. Power to the Church Council to frame rules and bye-laws

The Church Council shall have power from time to time to frame rules and bye-laws regulating the election of its Members, their qualification and number, and other matters with which it may have to deal, and from time to time, as it may see fit, to alter or amend the same: Provided that each Parish, Ecclesiastical District, or Congregation of the Church of England in Saint Lucia shall be therein represented by such number of Lay Members, to be chosen b themselves, as may from time to time fixed and determined by the said Council.

*The Bishop*

### 7. The Bishop of the Windward Islands to be president of the Church Council

The Bishop of the Windward Islands for the time being shall be President of the Church Council, and his assent shall be requisite to make any act thereof valid: Provided that, in the event of the Church being without Episcopal superintendence, the acts of the Church Council shall then be valid without such assent.

### 8. Jurisdiction of the Bishop

(1) The Bishop shall have and exercise the like control over the Clergy, and, in all ecclesiastical matters, the same ecclesiastical jurisdiction, in this part of his Diocese, as a Bishop of the Church of England in England has in the like case by the law for the time being in his Diocese: Provided, however, that the same be not contrary to the laws, usages, or customs of Saint Lucia.

(2) Every person who is aggrieved by any act or decision of the Bishop, from which act or decision such person would in England have a right of appeal shall, until some other mode of appeal is adopted by the Church Council, within 3 months of such act or decision, after giving to the Bishop notice in writing of his intention so to do, have the right of appealing from the Bishop to His Grace the Archbishop of Canterbury, whose decision shall be final.

*The Trustees*

### 9. Appointment, powers, and duties of trustees

(1) The Church Council shall have power to appoint 3 persons as Trustees, of whom the Bishop shall be one, and who are hereby incorporated as “The Trustees of the Anglican Church in Saint Lucia” in whom may be and hereby are vested, for the use and benefit of the said Church, the Churches, Churchyards, Burial-grounds, Glebe, and other lands at present held and possessed by the said Church in the Parishes or Districts of Castries, Soufriere, Choiseul, and Vieux-Fort.

(2) The Trustees shall also have power to acquire, hold, disburse, and dispose of money, lands, buildings and other property for the use and benefit of the said Church, as the same may be from time to time ordered by the Church Council.

(3) The Trustees may have a common seal, and may institute, maintain, and defend all actions or other proceedings for the defence and protection of their rights.

### 10. Payment to the trustees of endowments

It shall be lawful for the Treasurer to pay over to the Trustees, for the use and benefit of the said Church, such sums of money as may be from time to time appropriated by the Government of Saint Lucia for the maintenance of the Clergy of the said Church.

*Miscellaneous.*

### 11. Savings

Nothing in this Act shall authorise the imposition of any rate or tax upon any person whomsoever, whether belonging to the said Church or not, or the infliction or any temporal punishment, fine, or penalty upon any person, other than his suspension or removal from an office in the said Church, or exclusion from the meetings or proceedings of the Diocesan Synod.
