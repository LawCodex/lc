### AN ACT to provide for the Registration of Nurses and Midwives and for the enrolment of Nursing Assistants, and to regulate their training, practice and conduct, and for connected matters.

Commencement [23 October 1967]

## PART 1 PRELIMINARY

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Registration of Nurses and Midwives Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**Family Nurse Practitioner's Formulary**” means a list of drugs issued by the Council from which a family nurse practitioner is permitted to prescribe in the treatment of common complaints. These common complaints and their course of medication are detailed within the Family Nurse Practitioner's Protocol;

“**Family Nurse Practitioner's Protocol**” means a written presentation in booklet or manual form issued by the Council that defines the standards by which the quality of care can be measured and includes plans of clinical management of the common conditions that a family nurse practitioner is allowed to diagnose and treat. It specifies the problem, gives guidelines for care and the expected outcome and indicates at what stage the case should be referred to a Medical Practitioner;

“**institution**” includes any drug-store, pharmacy or pharmaceutical institution where drugs are dispensed;

“**Minister**” means the Minister responsible for health;

“**nurse practitioner**” includes a person who is qualified as a family nurse practitioner;

“**nurse-midwife**” means a person who is registered both as a nurse and as a midwife under this Act;

“**recognised place of training**” means a place of training referred to in section 31.

*(Amended by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 3. Establishment of the General Nursing Council of Saint Lucia

(1) For the purposes of this Act there is hereby established a General Nursing Council of Saint Lucia (hereinafter referred to as “the Council”) which shall be a body corporate by that name with perpetual succession and a common seal.

(2) The Council is to be constituted in accordance with the provisions of section 4.

### 4. Composition of the Council

(1) The Council shall consist of 9 members, as follows—

(a) the Chief Medical Officer or his or her nominee appointed by writing under his or her hand to represent him or her at any meeting and the Chief Nursing Officer, who are the *ex officio* members;

(b) two persons other than medical practitioner appointed by the Minister;

(c) one medical practitioner appointed by the Medical Association of Saint Lucia;

(d) four nurses at least 2 of whom shall be nurse midwives elected by the Saint Lucia Nurses Association.

(2) It is the duty of the Council to give due notice to the persons or bodies concerned of all necessary appointments or elections.

### 5. Tenure of office

(1) Members of the Council other than the *ex-officio* members shall hold office for 3 years but are eligible for re-appointment or re-election.

(2) If the place of a member of the Council becomes vacant before the expiration of his or her term of office whether by death, resignation or otherwise, the vacancy for the unexpired portion of his or her term shall be filled by appointment by the person or body by whom the member was appointed, or, if the vacating member was an elected member, by the Council, the Council in co-opting a member under the foregoing provision shall, so far as practicable select a person who is representative of the same interest as those represented by the vacating member.

(3) If it appears to the Council that any member is, by reason of illness, absence from the Island, or for any other cause unlikely to be able for some time to perform his or her duties as a member of the Council, the Council may declare the seat of such person to be temporarily vacant, and such temporary vacancy shall be filled, for the period of its duration in accordance with the provisions contained in subsection (2) with respect to the filling of vacancies.

(4) Four members constitute a quorum for meetings of the Council.

(5) The powers of the Council are exercisable despite any vacancy in its number or any defect with regard to the appointment of any of its members.

### 6. Officers of the Council

(1) The Chief Medical Officer is the chairperson of the Council.

(2) The Council shall elect from among its members a Treasurer/Secretary.

(3) The chairperson shall preside over meetings of the Council; if the chairperson is not present at any meeting, the members present shall elect one of their number to preside over that meeting.

(4) The chairperson or other person presiding at any meeting shall have an original vote, and in the event of any equality of votes, a casting vote as well.

(5) The seal of the Council shall be authenticated by the signature of the chairperson and of the Treasurer/Secretary and any document purporting to be sealed with the said seal so authenticated shall be receivable in evidence of the particulars stated in that document.

### 7. Staff and expenses

(1) The Council may, with the previous sanction of the Minister employ, at such salaries or remuneration as the Council may determine, a Treasurer/Secretary and such other staff as it considers necessary for carrying out its functions under this Act.

(2) Any expenses incurred by the Council in carrying this Act into effect, including expenses in connection with examinations under this Act and the travelling expenses of and sums paid as subsistence allowance to members of the Council shall be defrayed out of the sums received by the Council under this Act.

(3) The accounts of the Council shall be audited to 31 December of each year, not later than 21 March in the next ensuing year by such person, as the Council may, subject to the approval of the Minister, appoint, and copies of the accounts and of any report made on the accounts shall be transmitted to all the members of the Council and to the Minister and shall be laid on the table of the House of Assembly.

### 8. Duty of Council to assist and advise Minister

It is the duty of the Council to assist and advise the Minister on matters pertaining to nursing care for the sick and midwifery services in the Island, and in particular on the establishment of a recruitment and training programme for the provision and maintenance of such care and of such services.

### 9. Registers and Roll open to inspection

(1) The Registers and the Roll required to be kept under this Act shall be open to inspection at the office of the Council during the prescribed business hours on payment of such fee as may be prescribed.

(2) The Registers and the Roll, or a copy or extract from any of them duly certified by the Treasurer/ Secretary shall be *prima facie* evidence in all Courts and before all persons that the persons whose names are entered thereon are entitled to be registered or enrolled and are in fact registered or enrolled, and that any person whose name does not appear thereon is not registered or enrolled as the case may be.

## PART 2 NURSES

### 10. Register of Nurses

(1) The Council shall keep for the purposes of this Part of this Act a register, to be known as the Register of Nurses, on which it shall cause to be entered the names of all persons who are entitled to be registered as nurses in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

(2) The entry on the Register of Nurses shall show the date of the registration and the qualification upon which the registration is based.

(3) The Register of Nurses may be divided or classified, according to the manner which the Council considers most appropriate, into male and female nurses, and into nurses qualified for general nursing or for such special classes or branches of nursing as the Council may specify.

### 11. Qualifications for admission to the Register of Nurses

(1) The names of all persons who are registered under the Medical Registration Act and of all persons who are permitted to practise in Saint Lucia as nurse immediately prior to the commencement of this Act shall be entered on the Register of Nurses established under this Part of this Act without need for application on the part of such persons; and pending the entry of their names on the Register of Nurses, all such persons are considered to be duly registered under this Act.

(2) A person who has completed a course of training in a recognised place of training and has passed the examinations prescribed by the Council and who establishes to its satisfaction that he or she is a fit and proper person to be entered on the Register of Nurses as a nurse, on making application to the Council and upon compliance with the requirements of this Act, is entitled to be registered.

(3) A person who establishes to the satisfaction of the Council that he or she is registered or registrable on the Register of the General Nursing Council of England and Wales and that he or she is a fit and proper person to be entered on the Register of Nurses as a nurse, is entitled, on making application in the prescribed manner and paying such fee as the Council may demand, to be registered in a corresponding manner under this Act.

(4) A person not entitled to be registered in accordance with subsections (1), (2) and (3) who establishes to the satisfaction of the Council that he or she is a graduate in nursing from a school that is recognised by the Council as providing a standard of training and examination not lower than that prescribed under this Act, and is registered and in good standing in the country, territory, state or province in which he or she graduated as a nurse, or in which he or she last practised as a nurse, and that he or she is a fit and proper person to be entered on the Register of Nurses shall, on making application to the Council and upon compliance with the requirements of this Act be entitled to be registered.

However, the Council as a condition of registration may require that such person submit to and pass an examination prescribed by the Council.

(5) When there is a reciprocal agreement concerning nurses between this Island and any part of the British Commonwealth or any foreign country, any person who establishes to the satisfaction of the Council that he or she is registered in such part of the British Commonwealth or foreign country generally as a nurse for the sick shall be entitled on making an application to the Council and paying such fee, not being greater than the fee payable on ordinary applications for registration as a nurse under this Act, as the Council may demand, to be registered in the corresponding manner under this Act.

(6) Where the Council is satisfied that a person although not qualified to be registered as a nurse under the provisions of this Act, has undergone such a degree of training as in the opinion of the Council renders such a course expedient, the Council may upon the application of such person, direct that such person be exempted from such portion of the prescribed course of training or from such portion of the examination as to the Council shall seem fit, or that such person shall be registered upon compliance with such conditions as to training and to the passing of examinations as the Council shall think fit.

### 12. Certificate of registration and annual registration fee

(1) The Council shall, on entering the name of any person on the Register of Nurses, issue to such person a certificate of registration and such certificate shall remain in force unless suspended or cancelled.

(2) If any such person fails to pay any annual registration fee which may be payable for 3 months after it becomes due, his or her certificate of registration is considered to be suspended; but it is lawful for the Council to restore the validity of the certificate upon payment of the registration fee and such further sum by way of fine as may be prescribed.

### 13. Cancellation or suspension of registration

(1) The Council may by a 2/3 majority of its members present at a duly convened general meeting suspend for such period as the Council shall think fit, or cancel the registration of any person whose name is registered under this Part of this Act and in such case the certificate of registration issue under section 12(1) is considered to be suspended or cancelled as the case may be.

(2) Suspension or cancellation of registration may made upon proof to the satisfaction of the Council of—

(a) professional dishonesty; or

(b) negligence or incompetence in the performance of duty; or

(c) a habit or illness rendering the nurse unfit to practise nursing; or

(d) conduct that does not conform to the generally recognised standards of the profession of nursing, or that is unbecoming to a nurse; or

(e) fraud or misrepresentation in obtaining a registration whether for himself or herself or some other person,

on the part of the person registered. *(Amended by Act 16 of 1967)*

(3) Proceedings shall not be taken for the suspension or cancellation of a registration, unless at least 14 days' notice in writing is given to the person concerned of the intention to take such proceedings, and of the grounds on which such proceedings are based and such person shall be entitled to be heard in his or her defence and to be represented by counsel or solicitor at every stage of the proceedings.

### 14. Falsely conveying impression of being registered as a nurse

A person who is not registered as a nurse under this Act who takes or uses the name or title of “registered nurse” either alone or in combination with any other word or letters, or any name, title, addition, description, uniform or badge implying or calculated to convey the impression that he or she is registered under this Part or is recognised by law as a registered nurse, commits an offence.

## PART 3 MIDWIVES

### 15. Register of Midwives

(1) The Council shall keep for the purposes of this Part a register, to be known as the Register of Midwives, on which shall be entered the names of all persons who are entitled to be registered in accordance with the provisions of this Part.

(2) The entry on the Register of Midwives shall show the date of the registration and the qualification upon which the registration is based.

### 16. Qualification for admission to the Register of Midwives

(1) The names of all persons who are registered as midwives under the Medical Registration Act immediately prior to the commencement of this Act shall be entered on the Register of Midwives established under this Part without the need for application on the part of such persons; and pending the entry of their names on the Register of Midwives, all such persons are considered to be duly registered under this Act.

(2) Any person who completes a course of training in a recognised place of training and has passed the examinations prescribed by the Council and who establishes to its satisfaction that he or she is a fit and proper person to be entered on the Register of Midwives, upon making application to the Council, and upon compliance with the requirements of this Act, is entitled to be registered.

### 17. Certificate of registration and annual registration fee

(1) The Council shall on entering the name of any person on the Register of Midwives issue to every such person a licence to practise as a midwife bearing the signature of the chairperson and the Treasurer/Secretary; and such licence shall remain in force unless suspended or cancelled.

(2) If any such person fails to pay any annual registration fee which may be payable for 3 months after it has become due her licence issued under subsection (1) is considered to be suspended; but it shall be lawful for the Council to restore the validity of the licence upon payment of the registration fee and such further sum by way of fine as may be prescribed.

### 18. Cancellation or suspension of registration

(1) The Council may by a 2/3 majority of its members present at a duly convened general meeting, suspend for such period as the Council shall think fit, or cancel the registration of any person whose name is registered under this Part and in such case the licence issued under section 17(1) is considered to be suspended or cancelled as the case may be.

(2) Suspension or cancellation of registration may be made upon proof to the satisfaction of the Council of—

(a) professional dishonesty;

(b) negligence or incompetence in the performance of duty;

(c) a habit or illness rendering the midwife unfit to practise midwifery;

(d) conduct that does not conform to the generally recognised standards of the profession of midwifery, or that is unbecoming to a midwife; or

(e) fraud or misrepresentation in obtaining a registration, whether for herself or any other person, on the part of the person registered,

on the part of the person registered. *(Amended by Act 16 of 1967)*

(3) Proceedings shall not be taken for the suspension or cancellation of a registration unless at least 14 days' notice in writing has been given to the person concerned of the intention to take such proceedings, and of the grounds on which such proceedings are based, and such person shall be entitled to be heard in her defence and to be represented by counsel or solicitor at every stage of the proceedings.

### 19. Licence to be surrendered on cancellation or suspension of registration

A midwife whose registration has been suspended or cancelled shall within 14 days of being so notified in writing by the Treasurer/Secretary of the Council surrender her licence to the said Treasurer/Secretary, and if she fails to do so shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $25 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one month.

### 20. Duty of midwife to report deaths and births

If a person who has been attended by a midwife shall within 14 days of being so attended, die or give birth, it is the duty of the midwife, within 72 hours of the death or birth, as the case may be, to make a report to the Chief Medical Officer, and any midwife who fails to comply with the provisions of this section commits an offence.

### 21. Falsely conveying impression of being recognised midwife

A person not being licensed under this Act who takes or uses the name or title of “midwife” either alone or in conjunction with any other words or letters or any name, title, addition, description, uniform or badge, implying or calculated to convey the impression that she is registered under this Part of this Act or is a person specially qualified to practise midwifery, or is recognised by law as a midwife, commits an offence.

### 22. Power of Ministry to bring certain provisions into force

The Minister may by order published in the Gazette declare that section 23 shall come into effect within an area defined and delimited by such order at a date stated in such order, which shall be not less than 30 days after the publication of such order; and upon the date so stated the said section shall come into effect and have effect accordingly.

### 23. Restrictions on activities of unqualified persons at childbirth

From and after the date stated in any order published under section 22, and within the area in such order defined and delimited, any person who does not hold a valid and subsisting licence as a midwife who assists a woman in childbirth otherwise than under the direction and personal supervision of a registered medical practitioner or of a registered midwife commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $50 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 months or to both such fine and such imprisonment.

However, it is not an offence for a person to render assistance without fee or reward in a case of sudden or urgent necessity where no medical practitioner or midwife is available.

## PART 4 NURSING ASSISTANTS

### 24. Roll of Nursing Assistants

The Council shall keep for the purposes of this Part a roll, to be known as the Roll of Nursing Assistants on which shall be entered the names of all persons (in this Act referred to as Nursing Assistants) whose applications for enrolment have been approved by the Council.

### 25. Discretionary power of Council to enter names on Roll

The Council may in its discretion authorise the entry on the Roll of Nursing Assistants of the name of any person who has completed a course of training approved by the Council and who has passed the examinations prescribed by the Council and who has otherwise complied with the requirements of this Act.

### 26. Issue of certificates to persons enrolled

The Council shall on entering the name of any person on the Roll of Nursing Assistants issue to every such person a certificate under the hand of the chairperson and of the secretary/treasurer to that effect.

### 27. Removal of name from Roll by Council

The Council may, after due inquiry and on such grounds as it shall think proper, either for a fixed time or permanently, remove the name of any nursing assistant from the Roll of Nursing Assistants.

### 28. Falsely conveying the impression of being enrolled

A person who is not enrolled as a nursing assistant under this Act, who takes or uses the name or title of “nursing assistant” either alone or in combination with any words or letter, or who uses any name, title, addition or description, uniform or badge implying or calculated to convey the impression that he or she is enrolled as a nursing assistant under this Act, commits an offence.

## PART 5 NURSE PRACTITIONERS

### 29. Nurse Practitioners

(1) Despite the provisions of any other law, it is lawful for the Council to issue a licence, in the form set out in Schedule 1, to any person who is a qualified nurse practitioner and who has applied in writing to the Council for such a licence.

(2) Any application for a licence under this section shall be accompanied by evidence satisfactory to the Council of the applicant's qualification as a nurse practitioner.

(3) For the purposes of this section, a person is qualified to be licensed as a nurse practitioner, who—

(a) is a registered nurse;

(b) has completed a course of training for nurse practitioners approved by the Council; and

(c) has passed the prescribed examinations.

(4) A licence may only be issued under this section to those qualified persons for the purpose of practising nursing as a nurse practitioner and shall be of a year's duration renewable annually on application thereafter, if the nurse practitioner meets the criteria set by the Council prior to the renewal of the licence.

(5) A nurse practitioner licensed under this section shall be allowed to treat those diseases listed in Schedule 2, and in the manner specified in the Saint Lucia Family Nurse Practitioner's Protocol.

(6) In performing their duties under subsection (5) all nurse practitioners shall perform such duties under the general supervision of a senior registered nurse practitioner or a registered medical practitioner.

(7) Every nurse practitioner licensed under this section is allowed to prescribe only those drugs included in the Saint Lucia Family Nurse Practitioners' Formulary or are classified in Schedule 3.

(8) All medical prescriptions from a nurse practitioner shall be dispensed at any licensed institution by a registered pharmacist.

*(Inserted by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 30. Nurse Practitioners' prescription

(1) A registered pharmacist shall dispense a medical prescription signed with the name of any duly licensed nurse practitioner:

Except that such prescription shall include only those drugs that appear in the Saint Lucia Nurse Practitioners' Drug Formulary or are classified in Schedule 3.

(2) An alphabetical list of all licensed nurse practitioners shall be sent by the chairperson of the Council to all licensed institutions and all registered by 1 February of each year, so as to facilitate the proper implementation of this section.

*(Inserted by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 31. Amendment of Schedules

The Minister may by order made by statutory instrument amend, add to or delete the Schedules. (*Inserted by Act 1 of 1993)*

## PART 6 GENERAL

### 32. Appeal

A person aggrieved by the refusal of the Council to enter his or her name on the Register of Nurses or on the Register of Midwives, as the case may be, or by the suspension or cancellation of his or her registration as a nurse, midwife or nurse practitioner may, within one month from the date on which notice is given to him or her by the treasurer/secretary, appeal against the Council's decision to a judge in chambers, and the judge in giving his or her decision may make such order as to costs as he or she shall think fit. *(Amended by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 33. Power of Council to suspend from practice pending inquiry

If it appears to the Council on reasonable grounds to be inexpedient or dangerous in the public interest or in the interest of the health of his or her patients that any nurse, midwife, or nurse practitioner or nursing assistant should continue to practise pending a full inquiry, it is lawful for the Council to suspend such nurse, midwife, or nurse practitioner or nursing assistant from practice pending the results of a full inquiry by the Council which shall be instituted into the matter. *(Amended by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 34. Designation and approval of places of training

The Council may with the approval of the Minister designate such schools, hospitals or institutions, as it shall think fit, to be recognised as places of training or as affiliated places of training for nurses, midwives or nursing assistants as the case may be.

### 35. Rules

(1) The Minister may, after consultation with the Council make rules for carrying the purposes and provisions of this Act into effect and, without limiting the generality thereof, for —

(a) prescribing the conditions and qualifications for persons to be admitted for training as nurses, midwives, nursing assistants and nurse practitioners;

(b) prescribing curricula of study and programmes of training to be followed at places of training or affiliated places of training for nurses, midwives, nursing assistants and nurse practitioners;

(c) prescribing, regulating and administering the Regional Examination for Nurses Registration (RENR) in accordance with the guidelines established by the National Nursing Councils;

(d) regulating and defining the nature of the services which may be performed by nurses, midwives, nursing assistants and nurse practitioners and directing the procedures to be followed in cases of emergency;

(e) providing for charges to be made or fees to be paid and the amounts thereof, for any of the purposes of this Act; and

(f) prescribing anything that is authorised or required to be prescribed under this Act.

*(Amended by Act 1 of 1993 and substituted by Act 24 of 2014)*

(2) Rules made by the Council shall not take effect until they have been approved by the Governor General.

### 36. Fees

There shall be paid to the Council in respect of every application to be examined or to be registered or enrolled or as a registration or an enrolment fee, such amount as may be prescribed.

### 37. Power of Council to arrange examinations and appoint examiners

The Council may decide upon the times and places at which examinations under this Act are to be held and may appoint examiners and fix their remuneration.

### 38. Restoration of names removed from Register or Roll

It is lawful for the Council at any time to restore to the Register of Nurses, to the Register of Midwives, to the Roll of Nursing Assistants or to the list of licensed nurse practitioners as the case may be, any name which has been removed therefrom, whether permanently or for a fixed time. *(Amended by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 39. Making of false representation for purpose of registration

A person who, for the purpose of obtaining registration as a nurse or a midwife or a licence as a nurse practitioner or enrolment as a nursing assistant under this Act, either for himself or herself or for any other person, makes any false representation, whether by words, in writing, or by conduct, commits an offence. (Amended by Act 1 of 1993)

### 40. False certificate of licences

A person who falsely makes or issues a certificate or licence purporting to be a certificate or licence under this Act, or any person who knowingly has in his or her possession any certificate or licence so falsely made or issued commits an offence.

### 41. Falsification of Register or Roll

A person who wilfully makes or causes to be made any falsification of the Register of Nurses or of the Register of Midwives or of the Roll of Nursing Assistants or of the list of licensed nurse practitioners commits an offence. *(Amended by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 42. Approved badges or uniform

The Council may approve the design or description of any badge or uniform to be worn by nurses, by midwives, by nursing assistants or by nurse practitioners. *(Amended by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 43. notice of refusal or of order of removal or suspension

Where the application of any person for registration as a nurse or as a midwife or for enrolment as a nursing assistant or for a licence as a nurse practitioner has been refused by the Council, or when any order has been made for the removal or suspension of the name of any person from the Register of Nurses, Register of Midwives or the Roll of Nursing Assistants, or the list of licensed nurse practitioners it is the duty of the Secretary/Treasurer of the Council to give notice of such fact to the person concerned. *(Amended by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 44. Penalties

(1) A person who commits under this Act, except in cases where some other penalty is specifically provided, is liable on summary conviction to a fine of $2,500 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 4 months for a first offence, and for a second or subsequent offence to a fine of $5000 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 12 months.

(2) A rule made under this Act may prescribe in respect of any contravention of it or failure to comply with it a penalty not exceeding a fine of $5000 or imprisonment to a term not exceeding 3 months on summary conviction.

*(Amended by Act 1 of 1993)*

### 45. What constitutes “unauthorised practice”

In any prosecution under this Act it is sufficient to prove that the accused has done or committed a single act of unauthorised practice, or has committed on a single occasion any of the acts prohibited under this Act, and the word “practice” as used in this Act shall be construed in accordance with this section.

### 46. Collection and disposal of fines and other payments made under this Act

(1) Any moneys payable upon a summary conviction under this Act shall be paid to the Court, which shall cause the sums to be paid to the Secretary/Treasurer on behalf of the Council.

(2) All other moneys payable under this Act, whether by way of fees, fines or otherwise, shall be paid to the Council to be used for carrying the purposes of this Act into effect.
