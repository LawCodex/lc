### AN ACT to provide for the incorporation of the Roman Catholic Bishop of Castries.

Commencement [17 March 1959]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Roman Catholic Bishop Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act the term “**successor in office**” means the persons from time to time having, by virtue of the canon law of the Roman Catholic Church, or by appointment by the proper authority in Rome, supreme ecclesiastical jurisdiction in Saint Lucia in that Church.

### 3. Incorporation of Bishop

The Right Reverend Charles Gachet, Bishop of Castries, the Roman Catholic Bishop in Saint Lucia, and his or her successors in office shall be a body corporate, and have the name of “The Roman Catholic Bishop in Saint Lucia”, and by that name shall have perpetual succession, and full power to purchase, take, hold, enjoy, sell, transport, mortgage, lease, invest, and otherwise deal with, and dispose of, all property, moveable and immoveable, of what nature or kind soever, hereby vested and hereafter acquired, and may sue and be sued in all courts of justice, and before all magistrates in Saint Lucia.

### 4. Vesting of property held by Archbishop of Port of Spain

All property in Saint Lucia, moveable or immoveable, of what nature or kind soever, now held by or vested in the Archbishop of Port of Spain, and not heretofore transferred or otherwise disposed of, whether the transfers or conveyances vest the property in that Archbishop and in his or her own proper name or as that Archbishop shall be held by, and the same are vested in, the said corporate body.

### 5. Vesting of property transferred to Church

All property, moveable or immoveable, of what nature or kind soever, heretofore and hereafter bequeathed by will, or otherwise given to the Roman Catholic Church in Saint Lucia, shall be held by, and is vested in, and shall be deemed to be the property of, the said body corporate.

### 6. Property to be held for use of Church

All property vested in, or hereafter acquired by, the said body corporate shall be held by the said body corporate for use and benefit of the Roman Catholic Church in Saint Lucia.

### 7. Property to be held for groups without status

In all cases where property, moveable and immoveable, of what nature or kind soever, has been or is bequeathed by will or otherwise given to any number or class of persons being members of the Roman Catholic Church in Saint Lucia, who have not as that number or class any legal status, and no person is authorised by will or otherwise to deal with the property the said body corporate shall be the administrator, and as the administrator shall have power to sell, transport, mortgage or lease the property, and all other powers necessary for the absolute control and disposal without, however, imposing any personal liability on any Roman Catholic Bishop or any other Roman Catholic dignitary in Saint Lucia.

### 8. Saving of crown and other rights

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or Successors or of any bodies politic or corporate or of any persons except such as are mentioned or referred to in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
