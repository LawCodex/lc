### AN ACT to authorise the carrying out by Rodney Bay Limited of land improvement works; and for other purposes.

Commencement [13 June 1970]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Rodney Bay (Development) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act—

“**the appointed day**” means the day appointed pursuant to section 8;

“**the Company**” means Rodney Bay Limited, a company incorporated under the Commercial Code;

“**the swamp area**” means the area described in the Schedule;

“the works” means the works authorised by section 3 and 4 of this Act and works incidental thereto.

### 3. Nature of works to be performed

Subject to the provisions of this Act the Company may carry out the following works—

(a) Excavation of an inlet channel from Gros Islet Bay across Reduit Beach to the swamp area with protective rock fill structures extending seaward from the inlet channel and inland therefrom to the swamp area;

(b) Dredging of the swamp area and discharging the dredged material by pipe on the sea-bead in Gros Islet Bay;

(c) Construction of perimeter banks around the dredged area so as to establish tidal basins in place of the swamps;

(d) Reclamation of a neck of land between Pigeon Island and the mainland, thereby permanently closing the sea-gap.

### 4. Power to perform acts in execution of works

The Company, in connection with and for the purposes of the works authorised by section 3, may do any of the following things—

(a) make junctions with any existing public road intersected by or continguous to the works, and divert or widen or alter the line or alter the level of any existing public road for the purposes of the works;

(b) provide alternative means of access to premises the existing access to which has been or will be stopped up or interfered with by or as a result of the carrying out of the works;

(c) temporarily or permanently use, strengthen, widen, improve, alter or divert drains, sewers and water and other pipes;

(d) dig or dredge sand and excavate rock from the sea-bed, beaches, the sea shore or the Gros Islet Bay.

### 5. Duty to minimise disturbance

The Company shall ensure that, in carrying out the works, as little disturbance as is reasonably practicable, having regard to current engineering practice, is caused to residents in and visitors to the vicinity of the site of the works.

### 6. Powers to cease after 30 months

If the works are not completed within 30 months next following the appointed day then the powers granted by this Act for carrying out the works shall cease, but the powers shall be deemed to have been validly exercised as to so much of the works as are then complete.

### 7. Restriction of liability

Provided the works are carried out in conformity with section 5 no claim shall lie against the company or its servants or agents or against contractors employed by the Company or their servants or agents in respect of any inconvenience or detriment occasioned thereby or arising therefrom.
