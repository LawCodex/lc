### AN ACT to repeal and replace the Roman Catholic Church (Temporalities) Ordinance, Cap. 66.

Commencement [1 February 1972]

### 1. Short title

This Act may be cited as the Roman Catholic Church (Temporalities) Act.

### 2. Interpretation

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires—

“**Minister**” means the Minister of Lands; (*Inserted by Act 14 of 1983*)

“**parish**” means the extent of territory erected for the ecclesiastical purposes of the Church into a parish by the Bishop;

“**the Bishop**” means the Roman Catholic Bishop in Saint Lucia as created by the Roman Catholic Bishop Incorporation Act and includes the Bishop of Castries;

“**the Church**” means the Roman Catholic Church.

### 3. Division of parishes

For the purposes of this Act, Saint Lucia shall be and is hereby divided into as many parishes having the same boundaries as the existing parishes into which immediately before the commencement of this Act Saint Lucia is divided for the purposes of registering acts of civil status.

### 4. Establishment of Vestries

There shall be in each and every parish, a Vestry which shall consist of the Curé or other Incumbent of such parish and eight members who shall be elected in accordance with the provisions of section 9:

Provided that the Bishop may acting in his or her own liberate judgment delay the establishment of a Vestry in a new parish for such purposes as he or she may deem desirable.

### 5. Qualifications for membership of Vestry

No person shall be qualified to be elected as a member of a Vestry, and shall not be so qualified unless he or she—

(a) is qualified to vote at parochial meetings;

(b) is able to speak and, unless incapacitated by blindness or other physical cause, to read the English language with a degree of proficiency sufficient to enable him to take an active part in the proceedings of the Vestry.

### 6. Qualification of voters

No person shall be entitled to vote at an election of members of Vestry unless—

(a) he is a Roman Catholic who has attained the age of 18 years;

(b) has resided in the parish for at least 6 months prior to his registration as a voter in such parish;

(c) has availed himself of the ministrations of the Church;

(d) is a contributor to the funds of the parish.

### 7. Registration of voters

(1) The Curé or other Incumbent of each parish shall in every year prepare or cause to be prepared for his parish from the parish register or in such form as the Bishop may from time to time by directions in writing prescribe a list of all persons who to the best of his information and knowledge, are qualified to vote at parochial meetings therein.

(2) The Curé or the Incumbent of the parish shall insert in such list the name of every person who is proved to him to be qualified to vote at parochial meetings and shall strike out from such list the name of every person who is proved to him to be not so qualified, and shall also correct any error which is proved to him to be in such list.

(3) The Curé or the Incumbent shall affix or cause to be affixed in the Presbytery and in Church a certified copy of such list, which shall remain posted up for 14 days.

(4) The list so revised and corrected shall be kept at the Presbytery and any expense incurred in its preparation shall be laid before the Vestry at its first meeting and shall be paid out of the funds of the parish.

### 8. Term of office of vestryman

(1) Before the first election after the coming into operation of this Act, 2 of the members of the Vestry shall go out of office, and thereafter in every succeeding year before the holding of an election.

(2) The 2 members to go out of office under the provisions of the preceding section shall be—

(a) in the case of a newly established Vestry, those who received the smallest number of votes at their election as such members, or in the event of an equality of votes by drawing lots;

(b) in all other cases, the members oldest in office.

(3) Any retiring member of a Vestry shall be eligible for re-election.

### 9. Duty to hold election and conduct of election

(1) The Curé or other Incumbent shall within 28 days of such vestrymen vacating office hold an election for the purposes of filling any vacancies.

(2) At every election of vestrymen held in any parish the Curé or other Incumbent shall preside over the election which shall be carried out by ballot and shall publicly declare then and there the candidates who have attained the majority votes to be duly elected as vestrymen for each parish.

### 10. Notice of meeting

A notice of meeting of the Vestry shall be given by the Curé or other Incumbent at the request of the chairman or on the requisition in writing of at least 3 members.

### 11. Quorum

The quorum of a meeting of the Vestry shall be 4 members including the Curé or other Incumbent of the parish.

### 12. Validity of proceedings

(1) A vestry may act notwithstanding a vacancy among the members thereof and the validity of any proceedings of a Vestry shall not be effected by any vacancy among the members or by any defect in the election of members thereof.

(2) The acts of a quorum at any meeting of a Vestry shall be considered as the acts of the Vestry and shall be valid and effectual as if the same had been done by all the members of the Vestry for the time being and shall be binding on all the members of such Vestry.

### 13. Voting

(1) The decision of a Vestry with regard to any matter shall be determined by vote of the members present at the meeting of the Vestry and where the votes of the members present in regard to a matter shall be equally divided, the chairman or other member presiding in his place shall have a casting vote in addition to his own vote.

(2) Notwithstanding the foregoing provision where in the opinion of the Curé or other Incumbent any matter so determined may affect the interests of the Church, he may refer such matter to the Bishop whose decision shall be final and binding on the matter.

### 14. Election of chairman, churchwarden and auditor

(1) The first meeting of the Vestry shall be held as soon as possible after the election of new vestrymen.

(2) The members of the Vestry present shall elect by a majority of votes from among themselves a chairman to preside at all meetings of such Vestry, a church warden, and an auditor who shall all hold office for one year from the date of their election.

(3) A retiring chairman, churchwarden or auditor shall be eligible for re-election.

(4) The members of the Vestry present may appoint one of their number to perform the duties of chairman during the temporary absence of the chairman owing to illness, inability to attend, absence from the State or other cause.

### 15. Vacancy of membership of Vestry

A vestryman's office shall be deemed to be vacant on account of and shall include among other things—

(a) the vacation of the office caused by rotation;

(b) the member wilfully refusing to act; or

(c) where the member in the opinion of the Bishop has misconducted himself by virtue of his office;

(d) the resignation of a member;

(e) incapacitated on medical or other grounds which have been communicated to the satisfaction of the Bishop;

(f) absence from the State;

(g) death;

Provided that in respect of paragraph (f) a member shall not be held to be incapacitated unless—

(i) he has been out of the State for a period exceeding 3 months preceding the date of the parochial meeting; or

(ii) in excess thereof without the permission in writing of the Vestry.

### 16. Death etc. of chairman or churchwarden

(1) Where the chairman, churchwarden or auditor—

(a) dies; or

(b) leaves the State; or

(c) otherwise becomes incapacitated or disqualified from executing his office before the expiration of his term of office;

the Vestry of the parish shall forthwith meet and choose another member to be the chairman, churchwarden or auditor as the case may be.

(2) The vestryman so appointed as chairman, churchwarden or auditor as the case may be shall have and exercise all powers granted by this Act to a chairman, churchwarden or auditor elected by any Vestry.

### 17. Parochial meetings

(1) Subject to the provisions of the next following subsection a parochial meeting shall be held once in every year in each parish or on such other occasions as may be warranted to elect members to fill vacancies within the provisions of this Act:

Provided that the Vestry of any parish may submit to a parochial meeting any specific question in any matter of importance respecting which such Vestry may think fit to take the opinion of the parishoners.

(2) No parochial meeting shall be held in any parish until public notice of such meeting, the day and the hour of holding the same, and the special purpose thereof, has been given by the Curé or other Incumbent on 3 consecutive Sundays on the church notice-board for 15 days before the date of the meeting.

### 18. Minutes of proceedings of Vestry

The Vestry of every parish shall cause a book to be provided in which shall be fairly and distinctly entered the minutes of the proceedings and resolutions of the Vestry during the year, and such book shall be kept at the Presbytery, and shall at all reasonable times be open to inspection by any member of the Vestry, and the Bishop.

### 19. General rights and duties

It shall be the right and duty of the Vestry of every parish—

(a) to administer the property of the parish;

(b) to collect parish rents and dues;

(c) to superintend the expenditure of ordinary funds of the parish;

(d) to provide out of these funds all furniture, articles and things necessary for the service of the Church and the Presbytery;

(e) to fix salary of Church employees;

(f) to superintend the cemetery belonging to the parish;

(g) to take care that the Church and other parish buildings are being kept in good repair;

(h) to determine as to the expediency of extending or altering the Church and other parish buildings, or of erecting new buildings;

(i) to provide funds for the building and upkeep of the schools under management of the church;

(j) to determine as to the expediency of accepting or repudiating any donations or bequests which may be legally made to the parish and if accepted to superintend and administer the same;

Provided that in regard to paragraphs (h), (f) and (j) respectively—

(i) no major extension or alteration in the Church or other parish buildings shall be made, and no major work shall be done, until the approval of the Bishop has been obtained therein;

(ii) no repudiation of any donation or bequest shall take place until the question has been submitted to the Bishop and his sanction obtained to such repudiation.

### 20. Disposal of immovables

(1) Where lands within the State are held as Glebe or Vestry lands by the Cure or Vestry of a Parish in the State, the Minister, on being satisfied by a report from the Commissioner of Crown Lands that the boundaries of such Glebe or Vestry lands are demarcated, may by an Order published in the Gazette declare such lands to be duly vested in the appropriate Vestry as owner.

(2) The Vestry of any parish, or the churchwarden of any parish, acting under the directions of such Vestry, shall not sell, exchange or otherwise dispose of any immoveable property being part of the temporalities under the administration of such Vestry, and grant any lease of such property, or alter the conditions of any existing lease, without the consent in writing of the Bishop. In default of such consent all sales, alienations or leases of such property shall be null and void to all intents and purposes.

(*Amended by Act 14 of 1983*)
